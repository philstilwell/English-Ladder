US and Canada Trade Conflict Begins

The US and Canada have started a trade war over new import taxes on goods.

Read the story

President Trump from the US talked about Canada. He said Canada wants to enjoy the benefits of being a US state. Trade talks between the US and Canada did not succeed. The discussions collapsed, meaning they failed completely. Canada's Prime Minister announced new taxes on goods from the US. These are retaliatory tariffs, taxes made as a punishment. He said Canada was reluctantly introducing these new tariffs. The Prime Minister accused the US of starting a problem. He stated that the US started a trade war between the countries. This means both nations are putting higher taxes on each other's products.

Words and grammar

1. collapsed (verb): Stopped working or failed suddenly; fell down.
2. retaliatory tariffs (noun phrase): Special taxes placed on imported goods from another country as a form of punishment or response.
3. reluctantly (adverb): To do something unwillingly or without really wanting to.
4. accused (verb): Said that someone did something wrong or was guilty of something.
5. trade war (noun phrase): When countries put high taxes or restrictions on each other's goods, causing economic conflict.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that happened and finished at a specific time in the past. For regular verbs, we often add '-ed'. For irregular verbs, the form changes.
Example from the text: "Canada's Prime Minister announced new taxes on goods from the US."
