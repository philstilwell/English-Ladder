📅 October 12, 2026 - Zelensky's Conditional Support for Energy Ceasefire Amid New Strikes

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated he would support an "energy ceasefire" if Russia also agreed. He initially expressed surprise at Donald Trump's announcement of this ceasefire. Donald Trump claimed that Russia and Ukraine had already agreed to an "energy ceasefire," though Moscow has yet to comment. However, Zelensky later accused Russia of striking Ukrainian energy facilities after Trump's announcement. Trump has been urging Ukraine to stop strikes on Russian oil infrastructure. He wants Russia to fulfill a deal to release diesel to the global market. This diesel deal, which temporarily undoes sanctions on Russian imports, is attracting significant criticism. Zelensky himself called it "a gift to Russian President Vladimir Putin." Some European officials and US politicians also stated they do not support the deal. Trump suggested that it was "time for Ukraine to get a new president." For many months, Ukraine and Russia have been exchanging strikes on each other's energy infrastructure.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. stated (verb): said something clearly and formally.
2. initially (adverb): at the beginning; at first.
3. announcement (noun): a public statement giving information about something new or important.
4. urging (verb): strongly advising or trying to persuade someone to do something.
5. infrastructure (noun): the basic systems and structures, like roads and power plants, that a country needs.
6. fulfill (verb): to do or complete what is required or promised.
7. undoes (verb): cancels the effect of something; reverses a previous action.
8. criticism (noun): comments expressing disapproval of someone or something, often pointing out faults.
9. facilities (noun): buildings, services, or equipment provided for a particular purpose.

Grammar Focus: Reporting Verbs
Reporting verbs are used to tell someone what another person said, thought, or felt without directly quoting them. They can introduce reported speech or ideas. Common reporting verbs include "said," "stated," "claimed," "accused," and "suggested." The tense of the reported clause often shifts (e.g., present becomes past) if the reporting verb is in the past tense.
Example from the text: "Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated he would support an "energy ceasefire" if Russia also agreed."
