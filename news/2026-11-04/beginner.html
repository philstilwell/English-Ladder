New Graduates Use AI at Work

Beginner · A1–A2

📅 November 04, 2026 - New Graduates Use AI at Work

Read the story

Lotte Underwood has finished university and uses AI tools for much of her work. Her team's AI tools are computer programs that help with tasks like research and making pictures. She checks their work and shares it with other people in her team. She also joins meetings to learn how these tools can help customers. Underwood wants to learn other skills for her job too. Patel builds AI tools that give information to different teams. Patel also looks for tasks that AI could do, such as work that people repeat. Some graduates work with experienced staff to learn their jobs. They move to a different area of work every six months. Claire Reed believes too much AI work may not help graduates build their future careers. She says workers need AI skills and knowledge of what their customers want.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. tasks (plural noun): Pieces of work that someone needs to do.
2. checks (verb): Looks at something to see if it is correct.
3. shares (verb): Gives something to other people so they can use it too.
4. meetings (plural noun): Times when people come together to talk about something.
5. skills (plural noun): Things you can do well because you have learned or practised them.
6. staff (noun): The people who work for a company.

Grammar Focus: “Can” followed by a verb
Use “can” to say that someone or something is able to do something. Put the verb in its simple form after “can”, without “to”. Use the same form with every person: “I can help”, “she can help” and “they can help”.
Example from the text: "She also joins meetings to learn how these tools can help customers."
