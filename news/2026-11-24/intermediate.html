📅 November 24, 2026 - Don Toliver postpones London shows after Manchester evacuation

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

US rapper Don Toliver has postponed two London shows after an unknown substance was released at his Manchester concert on Sunday. Live Nation said his stage equipment remained in Manchester and could not be moved in time for the London shows. The Wembley Arena performance has been rescheduled for 14 December, while The O2 show will take place on 15 December. Police said 12,000 people were evacuated, meaning they were moved out of the arena for safety. According to police, one person was taken to hospital and 89 people showed symptoms believed to be linked to exposure. Some fans reported problems with their eyes and breathing, according to the ambulance service. Police are testing items from the venue to identify the substance, and no arrests have been made. Some fans complained about security and a lack of searches as people entered the Manchester concert. The reported security system used technology to detect banned items without requiring people to empty their pockets. A statement said everyone with a ticket for the Manchester show would receive a full refund automatically. Toliver said he wanted to make sure everyone was safe and that the next show went ahead properly.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. postponed (verb, past participle): Changed an event so that it will happen later than originally planned.
2. rescheduled (verb, past participle): Arranged for an event to happen at a different time or date.
3. evacuated (verb, past participle): Moved out of a place to keep people safe from possible danger.
4. symptoms (plural noun): Physical changes or problems that can show someone is ill or affected by something.
5. exposure (uncountable noun): Contact with something that may affect your health.
6. identify (verb, base form): Find out exactly what something is.
7. venue (countable noun): A place where an organised event happens.
8. refund (countable noun): Money returned to someone who paid for a product or service.

Grammar Focus: Past simple passive
Use "was" or "were" followed by a past participle to describe a completed past action affecting someone or something. In "were evacuated", the focus is on the people who were moved; the sentence does not name who moved them.
Example from the text: "Police said 12,000 people were evacuated, meaning they were moved out of the arena for safety."
