Public Asked About New Renewable Energy Company in Wales

📅 September 07, 2026 - Public Asked About New Renewable Energy Company in Wales

Read the story

The public can share their ideas about a new renewable energy company. The Welsh government wants to own this company. This company would develop wind and solar power plants. It hopes to make energy cheaper for people over time. But this company will not directly set electricity prices for homes. It wants to help people use local energy and get investment for Wales. Ministers say they want an energy system that is "clean, cheap and owned by us."

Words and grammar

1. public (noun): all the people in a country or area.
2. share (verb): to tell other people your ideas or feelings.
3. renewable (adjective): describing energy from sources that will not run out, like wind or sunlight.
4. company (noun): an organisation that sells things or services.
5. develop (verb): to build or create something new.
6. cheaper (adjective): costing less money.
7. prices (noun): the amounts of money you pay for things.
8. local (adjective): belonging to a particular area or neighbourhood.

Grammar Focus: Talking about the future (will / will not)
We use 'will' when we talk about things we expect to happen in the future. We use 'will not' or 'won't' to say something will not happen.
Example from the text: "But this company will not directly set electricity prices for homes."
