Read the story

According to officials, a recent Ukrainian strike has tragically impacted Russia's Tatarstan region. This particular incident is reported to have caused at least 13 fatalities, making it a very serious event. It is currently recognized as one of the deadliest Ukrainian drone attacks within Russian territory since the war began. The ongoing conflict between Ukraine and Russia has seen an increase in such long-range operations. Drone technology, utilized in this strike, allows for targeting areas far from the front lines. Tatarstan is situated quite a distance from the Ukrainian border, highlighting the evolving nature of the attacks. Russian authorities are actively conducting investigations to determine the full scope of the damage and the total number of casualties. Such incidents demonstrate Ukraine's developing capability to reach targets deeper within Russia. Throughout the hostilities, both nations have increasingly deployed various types of sophisticated weaponry. The international community continues its close observation of the situation, expressing concerns about further escalation.

Words and grammar

1. officials (noun): People who hold positions of authority in an organization or government.
2. strike (noun): An attack, especially a military one involving bombs or missiles, often carried out from the air.
3. region (noun): A large area of a country or the world, often characterized by specific geographic or cultural features.
4. deadliest (adjective): Causing the most deaths; most dangerous or destructive in terms of loss of life.
5. conflict (noun): A serious disagreement or argument, often involving prolonged fighting or war between opposing groups or countries.

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice
We use the passive voice when the action of the verb is more important than who or what performed it, or when the performer is unknown or obvious. The structure is generally 'to be' + past participle. This grammatical structure helps to focus attention on the event itself rather than the agent.
Example from the text: "It is currently recognized as one of the deadliest Ukrainian drone attacks within Russian territory since the war began."
