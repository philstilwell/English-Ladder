Trump and Xi Meet: Warm Words, Little Progress on Key Issues

Intermediate · B1–B2

📅 September 25, 2026 - Trump and Xi Meet: Warm Words, Little Progress on Key Issues

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

US and China's presidents shared polite words at a White House state dinner, but showed little sign of progress on important issues. Xi mentioned they had "in-depth exchanges" and "common understanding" on many topics, yet crucial matters dividing the two powerful countries were barely discussed. China thinks the U.S. wants to limit its growth as a superpower, especially regarding trade, technology, and Taiwan. Trump hoped to make deals on trade and tariffs, such as reducing Chinese taxes on American goods. However, tensions remained, especially concerning Taiwan, which Beijing claims as its own. The U.S. is legally required to help Taiwan defend itself, but Trump delayed a large arms package after Xi's warning. Trump was open about his goals for artificial intelligence (AI) and saw China as the main competitor in its development. Despite Xi's comment on AI needing "human control," there was little sign of any real progress on this topic during their talks. As a gesture, Trump gave the Chinese president a sculpture of a bald eagle, America's national symbol. Guests at the dinner ate from tables with red tablecloths, a color symbolizing luck and good fortune in China.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. polite words (phrase): Kind and respectful language used in conversation.
2. sign (noun): An indication or piece of evidence that suggests something is happening or exists.
3. common understanding (noun phrase): A shared agreement or clear grasp of information or a situation between people.
4. crucial matters (noun phrase): Very important issues or subjects that need to be addressed.
5. limit (verb): To restrict something or set a boundary on it, preventing it from increasing or spreading.
6. tariffs (noun): Taxes that a government places on imported or exported goods.
7. tensions (noun): Feelings of strain, hostility, or disagreement between people or groups.
8. claims (verb): States or asserts that something is true, often without providing proof.
9. delayed (verb): Caused something to be late or to happen at a later time than planned.
10. competitor (noun): A person, team, or company that is trying to win or be more successful than others in an activity or business.

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice (Past Simple)
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action or the receiver of the action, rather than the doer. We often use it when the doer is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from the context. For the past simple passive, we use "was/were" + the past participle of the main verb.
Example from the text: "Xi mentioned they had "in-depth exchanges" and "common understanding" on many topics, yet crucial matters dividing the two powerful countries were barely discussed."
