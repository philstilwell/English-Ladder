📅 July 11, 2026 - US Wants Iran to Stop Shooting at Ships

Read the story

The US wants Iran to make a pledge. This pledge means Iran will stop shooting at ships. Iran should not attack ships in the future. These ships travel through the Strait of Hormuz. The US wants peace in this important area. Vice-President JD Vance is an important US official. He will join other US officials. They will take part in important talks. These talks are called negotiations. The negotiations will start again on Saturday in Oman.

Words and grammar

1. pledge (noun): a serious promise to do something
2. shooting (verb): attacking with a weapon, like a gun
3. ships (noun): large boats that carry things or people on the sea
4. officials (noun): people who have important jobs in a government
5. negotiations (noun): formal talks between groups to agree on something
6. Strait of Hormuz (noun phrase): A narrow sea passage connecting the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea, crucial for global oil transport.

Grammar Focus: Want to + verb
We use 'want to' when we talk about something we wish for or hope to do. It shows a desire for an action. For example, 'I want to eat pizza' means I desire pizza.
Example from the text: "The US wants Iran to make a pledge."
