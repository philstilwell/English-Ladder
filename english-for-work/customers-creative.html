English for Customers, Sales and Creative Work

Customer and creative conversations combine clear information with an appropriate tone. Practice understanding a request, explaining a limitation, handling an objection and agreeing on a useful next step without promising an outcome you cannot control.

Choose the work you do

Restaurant servers, kitchen staff, baristas, hairdressers, nail technicians and retail associates each have a practical occupation course. Hospitality and retail/ecommerce offer broader operational contexts. Sales, customer success, marketing, media and real estate focus on their own audiences and decisions.

Make the practice practical

Read both sides of a dialogue and identify what each speaker needs. Switch roles, change one fictional constraint and rehearse a new ending. Compare the model response with your spoken version, checking commitments and unresolved questions.
