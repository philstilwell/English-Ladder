📅 November 27, 2026 - Zelensky Calls for More US Pressure on Russia

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ukraine's president, Volodymyr Zelensky, expressed frustration with the US, saying it was not putting enough pressure on Russia during negotiations. He said delays in receiving missiles used to stop other missiles were costing Ukrainian lives. He also complained that the US was focusing on the Middle East and providing more help there. Zelensky said it was strange that Donald Trump had posted a picture showing himself embracing Vladimir Putin and describing him as a friend. The latest negotiations, held with help from a third party in Abu Dhabi, made no clear progress. The talks were expected to resume in December, according to Zelensky. He called for support from allies, countries that help Ukraine, and urged the US to put pressure on Russia. The US says it is neutral, meaning it does not take either side, while seeking a peace agreement that can last. Zelensky reported a serious shortage of defensive missiles and said the US was delaying deliveries of Patriot air defence systems. Russian attacks have become more frequent and deadly, and the number of civilians killed has reached its highest level. Power cuts and attacks on transport networks have raised operating costs so much that some businesses have shut. Zelensky acknowledged that Ukraine was preparing for a difficult and dangerous winter.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. frustration (uncountable noun): The feeling of being upset because a problem continues or something prevents progress.
2. delays (plural noun): Situations in which something happens later than expected or needed.
3. negotiations (plural noun): Discussions in which people try to reach an agreement.
4. resume (verb): To continue an activity after a pause.
5. allies (plural noun): Countries or groups that support and help one another.
6. neutral (adjective): Not supporting either side in a disagreement or conflict.
7. shortage (countable noun): A situation in which there is less of something than people need.
8. operating costs (plural noun phrase): The money needed to keep a business running.

Grammar Focus: Passive reporting with “was/were expected to”
Use “was/were expected to” followed by the base form of a verb to report what people thought would happen later. This passive structure leaves out who held the expectation. It reports an expectation, so it does not confirm that the event happened.
Example from the text: "The talks were expected to resume in December, according to Zelensky."
