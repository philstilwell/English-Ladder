# English for Work: book-aligned web revision

## Scope

All 66 industry and occupation pages, covering 528 lessons. The reviewed book
content is the source for the main cases, vocabulary, phrases, language notes,
questions, extended dialogues, and follow-up exchanges. The professional-content
review is documented in `books/AUDIT-2026-10.md`; this web revision preserves its
facts, limitations, roles, calculations, and answer explanations.

## Published learning material

Each course now includes:

- Eight book-aligned lesson briefings with three four-choice fact checks each.
- 192 vocabulary entries, each with its field-specific meaning and collocation.
- 128 phrases organized by communication purpose, plus 48 language notes.
- Four case-specific language checks in each lesson.
- Eleven 20-turn cloze dialogues: eight within lessons and three additional cases.
- Eleven four-turn follow-up exchanges with four supplied choices per gap.
- The existing three distinct 10-turn conversations in each lesson, explicitly
  identified as separate fictional situations rather than extensions of its case.
- Complete dialogue models, explained answers, and links to the corresponding
  pages in the learner book.

Across the collection this gives 726 extended dialogues, 726 follow-up exchanges,
6,600 four-choice items, and 6,468 extended-dialogue gaps. Short-exchange choices
are reordered without changing their meanings or correct answers. Extended
dialogue banks are shuffled and every selection box has the same width.

## Usability and teaching safeguards

- Six A-F sections and the eight-lesson navigation are retained.
- Expanded vocabulary, phrases, conversations, and additional scenarios use
  closed disclosures to keep the initial reading view manageable.
- The sticky menu retains its course illustration, progress dots, open-lesson
  borders, and Prompts link. New conversation anchors account for both menus.
- Spoken practice uses supplied scripts and facts. The old unbounded written
  transfer and final composition tasks are removed.
- All courses' AI vocabulary and grammar extensions now request structured
  four-choice practice. Their lesson references use the revised book facts.
- Answer selection, feedback, and retry do not create browser records.
- Word banks, questions, answer explanations, and completed scripts remain in
  the HTML and usable without JavaScript.
- Existing PDFs and artwork are unchanged by this revision.

## Verification

The publication tests compare every extended script and answer with its book
source, check all language banks and four-choice items, and verify PDF page
references. Browser checks cover the directory, six category pages, and all 66
courses at 320, 390, 768, and 1280 pixels, including expanded content, keyboard
navigation, deep links, feedback, retry, prompt copying, and no-JavaScript reading.

The dated browser report records completed checks and any remaining errors.
This is not a professional-content certification or a complete accessibility audit.
