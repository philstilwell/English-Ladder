A recent Russian attack caused deaths and hit important places in Ukraine.

Read the story

A Russian strike happened recently. This attack killed at least 17 people. President Zelensky talked about the strike. He said special interceptors could have saved lives. The Russia defence ministry gave a statement. They said their forces struck targets. These targets were in Kyiv. They also struck places in the surrounding region. The ministry mentioned logistics hubs and supply centres. These were important places that were hit.

Words and grammar

1. strike (noun): An attack, often from the air, using bombs or missiles.
2. killed (verb, past tense): Made someone die; ended a life.
3. interceptors (noun): Special equipment or planes designed to stop incoming attacks or missiles.
4. ministry (noun): A department of a government, like a defence ministry or education ministry.
5. hubs (noun): Central places where many things meet, are stored, or move from.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. It describes things that happened at a specific time before now. For most regular verbs, we add -ed to the base form.
Example from the text: "This attack killed at least 17 people."
