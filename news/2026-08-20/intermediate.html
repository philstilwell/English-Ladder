Read the story

Kyiv experienced a devastating night on Thursday following a massive attack. Tragically, at least 13 people were killed as a direct result of these strikes. Many residential apartment blocks and commercial warehouses were targeted during the assault. These structures sustained significant damage, highlighting the intensity of the offensive. Ukraine currently grapples with severe air defence shortages across the country. The lack of sufficient protective systems makes cities more vulnerable to aerial bombardments. According to officials, the capital suffered extensive damage from the large-scale missile and drone barrage. Emergency services immediately responded to the sites, working to rescue survivors and clear debris. Such incidents underscore the ongoing challenges faced by Ukrainian forces in protecting civilian areas. The government continues to seek international support to strengthen its defensive capabilities.

Words and grammar

1. massive attack (noun phrase): A very large, powerful, and intense military action or assault, typically involving many resources or forces.
2. targeted (verb (past participle)): Chosen or selected as the object of an attack or an action; directed towards a specific aim.
3. grapples with (verb phrase): To struggle or contend with a difficult problem or situation, trying hard to overcome it.
4. air defence shortages (noun phrase): A situation where there are not enough systems, equipment, or resources to protect against attacks from the sky, such as missiles or aircraft.
5. officials (noun): People who hold a public office or have authority in an organization or government, often speaking on behalf of it.

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself and the object of the action, rather than the subject performing the action. It's formed with a form of 'to be' + the past participle of the main verb. It is very common in news reporting when the 'doer' of the action is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from the context.
Example from the text: "Many residential apartment blocks and commercial warehouses were targeted during the assault."
