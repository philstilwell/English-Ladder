Read the story

Russia made missile attacks near Kyiv. Three people died in these attacks. A child was among the people killed. Russia continued these attacks overnight. President Zelensky warned about a problem. Ukraine has less supplies of important weapons. These weapons are called missile interceptors. The number of interceptors is dwindling. This means Ukraine has fewer missile interceptors now. The attacks happened after President Zelensky spoke.

Words and grammar

1. missile attacks (noun phrase): When a country shoots rockets or bombs at another country or place.
2. killed (verb (past tense)): Made someone or something die.
3. warned (verb (past tense)): Told someone about a danger or problem before it happened.
4. supplies (noun): Things that people need and have available, like food or weapons.
5. continued (verb (past tense)): Kept doing something without stopping.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or events that started and finished in the past. It tells us what happened. We often add "-ed" to regular verbs, but some verbs are irregular.
Example from the text: "Russia continued these attacks overnight."
