Lesson 01: Confirming the visit and the care plan
Original fictional case: You arrive at 9:00 for a two-hour visit. Your assignment lists breakfast and light household tasks. The client expects transport to a 10:00 appointment, but transport is not listed and you have no authorization. The coordinator is available by phone.
Language workshop: Check expectations before starting
Communication goal: Name the mismatch between the assignment and the client's expectation without blaming either person.
Language guidance: A care plan records agreed support; a client's immediate request may differ. Acknowledge the need, identify what your current assignment actually says, and ask the person who can clarify it. An omission is not proof that nobody arranged support.
Useful frames (complete the gaps with case facts): My assignment lists ___; were you expecting something else? / Could we check whether ___ was arranged separately? / I can confirm ___, but I still need to check ___.
Vocabulary: care plan: The current agreed record of a client's needs, support, and authorized arrangements. / assigned visit: A scheduled period of support with duties assigned to a particular worker. / scope of duties: The limits of the tasks a worker is authorized to carry out. / client preference: A person's stated choice about how they want support provided.
Speaking task: Prepare for two minutes. Speak for 45-60 seconds using the case facts, then respond to your partner's question. Switch roles and repeat without reading the model response.
Partner: You are the client. The appointment is at 10:00, and you remember discussing transport last week, but you do not know whether it was booked.
Guided response task: Complete a workplace dialogue by selecting one of four supplied options. Compare the feedback and say the completed exchange aloud. Do not write an open-ended message.
Optional second-round challenge: The coordinator says a separate transport service may have been contacted but needs five minutes to confirm. Explain the uncertainty and agree what to do while waiting.
Model for comparison AFTER my attempt, not a response to give me first: Your appointment is at ten, and I understand why you expected a ride. My assignment today lists breakfast and light household tasks, but it does not include transport. May I contact the coordinator now to check what was arranged? I cannot confirm a ride until we have that information.
