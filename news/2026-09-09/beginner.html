Worries about Dangerous AI

📅 September 09, 2026 - Worries about Dangerous AI

Read the story

An AI researcher thinks AI could be very dangerous. He believes there is a chance AI 'could kill all humans' in the future. This is one of many warnings about AI safety. A computer expert was surprised by these warnings. She thinks some warnings might be for marketing. Companies are working to build human ethical ideas into AI. But some researchers worry these efforts are failing. AI systems that work alone have caused cyber-attacks.

Words and grammar

1. researcher (noun): a person who studies something carefully to discover new facts
2. dangerous (adjective): not safe; able to cause harm or injury
3. chance (noun): a possibility that something will happen
4. warnings (noun): messages or signs that tell you about a possible problem or danger
5. expert (noun): a person who has a lot of special knowledge or skill in a particular area
6. marketing (noun): the activities involved in advertising and selling a product or service
7. efforts (noun): hard work or attempts to do something, especially something difficult
8. cyber-attacks (noun): harmful actions done using computers and the internet to damage or steal information

Grammar Focus: Using "could" for possibility
We use the word "could" to talk about something that is possible in the future, but not certain. It means there is a chance it might happen.
Example from the text: "He believes there is a chance AI 'could kill all humans' in the future."
