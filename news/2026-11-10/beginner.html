📅 November 10, 2026 - TV Star Tommy Walsh Dies at 69

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

TV star Tommy Walsh died at the age of 69. He worked as a builder before he became famous on TV. He got his chance on TV after he fixed up a house for someone at the BBC. He was on the BBC show Ground Force from 1997 to 2005. The team gave people’s gardens a new look. He joined the TV show Homes Under the Hammer in 2021. His agent said Walsh died peacefully after a long illness, with his family around him.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. builder (noun): A person whose job is to build or fix houses and other buildings.
2. famous (adjective): Known by many people.
3. chance (noun): A time when you can do something you want to do.
4. gardens (plural noun): Areas of land near homes, often with grass, flowers or other plants.
5. joined (past tense verb): Became part of a group or started working with it.
6. illness (noun): A health problem that makes someone feel sick.

Grammar Focus: Simple past with regular verbs
Use the simple past to talk about an action that happened before now. For many verbs, add “-ed”, as in “work” and “worked”. For regular verbs that end in “e”, add only “-d”, as in “die” and “died”.
Example from the text: "He worked as a builder before he became famous on TV."
