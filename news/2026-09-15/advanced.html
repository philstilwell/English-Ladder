Matthew Rhys: Emmy History-Maker and Welsh Cultural Ambassador

📅 September 15, 2026 - Matthew Rhys: Emmy History-Maker and Welsh Cultural Ambassador

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Hollywood actor Matthew Rhys has made history at the Emmys by becoming the first person to win two lead acting prizes in one night. He was named best lead actor in a limited series for the psychological thriller The Beast In Me , and best lead actor in a comedy for his role in Widow's Bay. Early in his career, Rhys seriously considered enlisting in the British Army due to a significant lack of acting opportunities. For nearly two years afterwards, he couldn't secure work, which deepened his resolve to explore alternatives. He later received a formal communication from the army, politely declining his application, which he found ironically telling. In 2006, Rhys moved to Hollywood and surrounded himself with fellow Welsh actors, including Michael Sheen and Ioan Gruffudd. He affectionately called their expatriate group the "LA Tafia," reflecting their shared Welsh roots in Los Angeles. Rhys met Keri Russell in Los Angeles at a kickball game in 2002, initially leaving a drunken message she found far from charming. A decade later, the pair finally became an item when they starred together as Russian spies in the TV series The Americans. Rhys owns a 1939 wooden cruiser boat, named Rarebit, which shares its name with a traditional Welsh dish of seasoned melted cheese on toast. He admitted not foreseeing "the disaster of buying an old wooden boat" before making the purchase, highlighting the challenges of its upkeep. The Welshman dedicated his second award to "the great and good people of Wales, who were as much a nurture as anyone else," expressing profound gratitude. Rhys is also recognized as the voice of Han Solo in an upcoming Welsh language rendition of Star Wars. This project, which involves translating familiar Star Wars elements into Welsh, elicited unexpected feelings for him. He remains keen to stay connected to all things Welsh, notably taking on the role of iconic Richard Burton in a one-man show in 2025.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. psychological thriller (noun phrase): A genre of fiction that explores the mental and emotional states of its characters, often creating suspense and tension through psychological elements rather than overt action.
2. enlisting (verb): The act of formally joining a military force or other organization.
3. secure work (verb phrase): To successfully obtain employment or a job.
4. deepened his resolve (verb phrase): Increased his determination or strengthened his firmness of purpose regarding a particular action or decision.
5. ironically telling (adjective + adjective): Revealing something significant or meaningful in a way that is contrary to what would be expected or intended, often with an element of subtle humor or profound insight.
6. surrounded himself with (phrasal verb): To intentionally be in the company of certain people, often for support, shared interests, or personal growth.
7. became an item (idiom): Started a romantic relationship or became a recognized couple.
8. cruiser boat (noun phrase): A type of motorboat designed for comfortable travel over water, often equipped with amenities for extended trips or overnight stays.
9. upkeep (noun): The process of keeping something in good condition, including maintenance, repairs, and general care.
10. profound gratitude (noun phrase): A deep and intense feeling of thankfulness or appreciation.

Advanced Grammar: Participle Clauses for Consequence or Additional Information
Participle clauses can be used to add information about a noun or a verb, often indicating a consequence, cause, or additional detail related to the main clause. They can be introduced by a present participle (-ing form) or a past participle (-ed form), acting like an adjective or an adverbial phrase. In the example, "highlighting" introduces a consequence or an additional, explanatory detail about his admission, efficiently linking the two ideas.
Example from the text: "He admitted not foreseeing "the disaster of buying an old wooden boat" before making the purchase, highlighting the challenges of its upkeep."
