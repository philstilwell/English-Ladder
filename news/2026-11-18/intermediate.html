📅 November 18, 2026 - Waymo Pauses Driverless Taxi Services on US Freeways

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Waymo has temporarily suspended freeway services for its driverless taxis across the United States. The decision followed a California ban after a series of traffic incidents involving the company's vehicles. California officials said the taxis created a safety risk by repeatedly entering construction zones where people were working. In one incident, several Waymo vehicles entered an active construction zone on US Route 101. Waymo said no injuries or collisions were reported after the incident on US Route 101. In another incident, video appeared to show a Waymo car trying to pass vehicles that were deliberately slowing traffic. California's ban took effect on 11 November. The restrictions will remain until Waymo shows state officials it has taken effective steps to deal with the problems. Waymo said it also paused freeway services in its other markets to evaluate how its vehicles were performing. The company said it was working with state officials to resume freeway services. Its driverless taxis can still operate on regular city streets.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. suspended (verb, past participle): Stopped an activity for a period of time.
2. a series of (phrase): Several similar or related things happening one after another.
3. construction zones (plural noun phrase): Areas where roads or other structures are being built or repaired.
4. collisions (plural noun): Accidents in which vehicles hit something, such as another vehicle.
5. deliberately (adverb): With a clear intention, rather than by accident.
6. restrictions (plural noun): Rules that limit what someone or something is allowed to do.
7. evaluate (verb): Examine something carefully to judge how good or effective it is.
8. resume (verb): Start an activity after it has stopped for a time.

Grammar Focus: Past simple passive
Use “was” or “were” plus a past participle to form the past simple passive. It focuses on what happened rather than who did it. Here, “were reported” is passive within the information introduced by “said”.
Example from the text: "Waymo said no injuries or collisions were reported after the incident on US Route 101."
