Read the story

People are being forced to leave Pakistan and Iran for Afghanistan. Some people have never been to Afghanistan. Nazia was born in Pakistan and crossed the border with her children. Aid groups offer families a place to stay for a short time. Some families have little money and no way to earn more. They need help as they return and start a new life.

Words and grammar

1. forced (verb): made to do something they did not choose
2. return (verb): go back to a place
3. never (adverb): not at any time

Grammar Focus: Have never been
Have never been describes an experience that has not happened before now. Never means not at any time.
Example from the text: "Some people have never been to Afghanistan."
