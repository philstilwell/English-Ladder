Title: US President Cancels Iran Strikes Amidst Deal Discussions

Read the story

US President Donald Trump recently announced he cancels planned military actions against Iran. This decision follows a period of escalating tensions in the Middle East region. The President claims that both Iran and other Middle Eastern nations approached Washington. They reportedly asked the United States to hold off on any potential strikes. Trump indicated that these countries requested a delay because the general perimeters of a diplomatic deal had been discussed. The President suggested that an initial agreement's boundaries were already laid out. This diplomatic effort aims to de-escalate the conflict and prevent further military engagement. International observers are closely monitoring the situation as discussions continue. The sudden cancellation highlights the complexities of foreign policy and international negotiations. It also suggests a willingness from various parties to seek a resolution through dialogue.

Words and grammar

1. cancels (verb): To decide or announce that something arranged will not happen.
2. strikes (noun): Military attacks, usually from the air.
3. claims (verb): To state something is true, often without proof.
4. hold off (phrasal verb): To delay doing something or to prevent something from happening.
5. perimeters (noun): The outer limits or boundaries of an area or situation.
6. deal (noun): an agreement between people or groups
7. conflict (noun): A serious disagreement or argument, often involving prolonged fighting or hostility between groups or nations.
8. negotiations (noun): Formal discussions between people trying to reach an agreement or resolve a dispute.

Grammar Focus: Past Perfect Passive
The Past Perfect Passive is used to talk about an action that was completed before another past action, and the focus is on the action itself rather than who performed it. It's formed with 'had + been + past participle.'
Example from the text: "the general perimeters of a diplomatic deal had been discussed."
