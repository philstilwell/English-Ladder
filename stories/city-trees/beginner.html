📅 September 05, 2026 - Title: Can trees cool a city?

Before you read

Look at the photo. Where would you stand on a hot day?

Read the story

Trees can help cool a city. Their leaves give people shade on hot days. Trees also release water into the air, and this helps cool it. A tree near a building can reduce the need for air conditioning. People need to choose trees that suit the local climate. Young trees need care after people plant them.

Words and grammar

1. shade (noun): a place where something blocks direct sunlight
2. reduce (verb): to make something smaller or less
3. climate (noun): the usual weather in a place over many years

Grammar Focus: Can + verb
Use can with the basic form of a verb to describe what is possible.
Example from the text: "Trees can help cool a city."
