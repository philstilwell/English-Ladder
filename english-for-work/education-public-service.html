English for Education and Public Service

Education and public-service communication brings together people with different responsibilities and expectations. Practice explaining evidence, inviting a missing perspective, discussing limited resources and clarifying how a decision will be made.

Choose the work you do

Education administration focuses on institutional and student-support communication. Higher education and research covers academic collaboration and research discussions. Public administration and nonprofit courses address community-facing work, grants, coordination and accountability.

Make the practice practical

Prepare a short explanation for a specific audience, then ask a partner to raise a concern that has not been considered. Clarify which points are agreed and which still need review. Close with a written summary that names the next question or action.
