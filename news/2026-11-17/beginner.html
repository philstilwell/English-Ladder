📅 November 17, 2026 - Robert Pattinson Will Be the Next James Bond

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Robert Pattinson will play James Bond after Daniel Craig. The British actor is known for Twilight and The Batman. Amazon MGM Studios announced the news on social media on Monday. Pattinson is 40 and will be the seventh actor to play Bond. The next Bond film is expected in 2028. Denis Villeneuve will direct the film, and Steven Knight will write it. The film's title and exact release date have not been announced yet. Pattinson said he was excited to work with the team.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. actor (noun): A person whose job is to play people in films or plays.
2. play (verb): To be someone else in a film or play.
3. announced (verb, past simple): Shared some news with many people.
4. title (noun): The name of a film, book or song.
5. excited (adjective): Very happy about something that is happening or will happen.
6. team (noun): A group of people who work together.

Grammar Focus: Talking about the future with “will”
Use “will” and the basic verb form to talk about the future. The verb stays the same after “will”, so use “will play”, not “will plays”. Use this pattern for future actions, such as making a new film.
Example from the text: "Robert Pattinson will play James Bond after Daniel Craig."
