📅 November 05, 2026 - UK Interest Rate May Rise

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The Bank of England may raise its interest rate on Thursday. Most experts think the rate will rise from 3.75% to 4%. This rate affects the cost of borrowing money and the extra money banks pay on savings. The Bank has kept the rate unchanged at its last six meetings. Experts say prices are rising because petrol and diesel cost more. The Bank will make its announcement on Thursday. Some people with home loans would have higher monthly payments if the rate rises. People with savings could get more money from their banks if the rate rises. The Bank last raised its rate in August 2023.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. raise (verb): To make a number or amount higher.
2. borrowing (noun): Taking money from a bank and paying it back later.
3. savings (plural noun): Money you keep instead of spending.
4. unchanged (adjective): Staying the same.
5. announcement (noun): Information that someone gives to a group of people.
6. monthly (adjective): Happening every month.

Grammar Focus: Using “may” for possibility
Use “may” before a verb to show that something is possible, but not certain. Use the verb without “to” or an added “-s”, as in “may rise”. This helps us talk about a possible change in the future.
Example from the text: "The Bank of England may raise its interest rate on Thursday."
