Beginner ESL: A small question. A new conversation.

Before you read

You want to buy something, but you do not know the price. What could you ask?

Read the story

Mina visits a food market on Saturday. She wants to buy tomatoes for dinner. She asks a vendor, 'How much are these tomatoes?' The vendor says, 'They cost three dollars for one kilo.' Mina asks, 'Could I have half a kilo, please?' The vendor puts the tomatoes in her bag, and Mina says thank you.

Words and grammar

1. vendor (noun): a person who sells things
2. cost (verb): to have a particular price
3. half (noun): one of two equal parts

Grammar Focus: A polite request with could
Could I have…? is a polite way to ask for something.
Example from the text: "Mina asks, 'Could I have half a kilo, please?'"
