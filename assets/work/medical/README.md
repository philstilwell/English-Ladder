# Medical Profession Illustrations

Created with OpenAI's native image generator, as approved by the user, for
the six medical-profession courses added in October 2026. These are original
illustrations, not stock images or substituted symbols from the old atlas.

- General practitioners: a physician-patient consultation and examination tools.
- Oncologists: microscopy, a cell illustration, a cancer-awareness ribbon,
  and a supportive-care motif.
- Cardiologists: an illustrated heart, stethoscope, and rhythm display.
- X-ray technicians: plain-radiography equipment and a chest image, not a CT scanner.
- Pediatricians: a pediatrician with a child, growth chart, and toys.
- Obstetricians: an obstetric consultation with a pregnant patient and ultrasound image.

The transparent 1254-by-1254 PNG originals are retained; the PDF builder embeds
a 672-pixel copy for approximately 300-dpi cover printing. Transparent
384-pixel WebP copies serve the 104-pixel webpage icons, including high-density
displays, with lighter downloads and smooth small-size rendering.
No new artwork was generated during that format conversion. CSS display
bounds center the visible artwork in the directory's equal-size cells.
Illustrations are decorative teaching artwork, not anatomical or equipment
diagrams for clinical use.
