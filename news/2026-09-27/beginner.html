📅 September 27, 2026 - Bangkok Floods After Heavy Rain

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Bangkok roads were submerged by floodwaters. A flood emergency disaster was declared across Bangkok. More than 300mm of rain fell since Thursday. The heavy rains have subsided, but the flooding is not yet over. Water levels in canals remain high. People were urged to stay at home. More heavy rain is forecast through the week.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. submerged (verb): covered completely with water
2. disaster (noun): a very bad event that causes a lot of damage or harm
3. declared (verb): said officially and publicly
4. subsided (verb): became less strong or intense
5. remain (verb): to stay in the same place or condition
6. urged (verb): strongly advised someone to do something

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that happened and finished in the past. We often use it for news about past events.
Example from the text: "More than 300mm of rain fell since Thursday."
