New Huw Edwards Interviews Make People Angry

📅 November 28, 2026 - New Huw Edwards Interviews Make People Angry

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Huw Edwards used to read the news for the BBC. Some people are angry about new interviews with him. In 2024, a court found him guilty of crimes involving sexual images of children. He gave an interview for a Channel 4 documentary, a programme about real people or events. There is also an interview for a podcast, an audio programme people can listen to online. The prime minister's office said it did not want to hear Edwards's views. It said television companies should think carefully about the people watching their programmes. Channel 4 said Edwards answered questions about his crimes in the programme. One government minister said Channel 4's plan made her feel sick. The documentary and podcast are due to come out on Tuesday.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. interviews (plural noun): Conversations where someone asks a person questions to learn about their life or ideas.
2. angry (adjective): Feeling very unhappy about something and wanting to shout or complain.
3. guilty (adjective): Having done something against the law, as decided by a court.
4. views (plural noun): What someone thinks about something.
5. carefully (adverb): With time and thought, so you can avoid mistakes.
6. questions (plural noun): Things you ask when you want to know something.

Grammar Focus: Using “should” for advice
Use “should” to say what you think is a good thing to do. Put the basic verb after “should”, without “to”. For example, use “should think”, not “should thinks”. The form stays the same with “he”, “she” and “they”.
Example from the text: "It said television companies should think carefully about the people watching their programmes."
