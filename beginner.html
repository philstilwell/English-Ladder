📅 September 06, 2026 [0 days, 19 hours old] - Title: Weather and air travel
Could changing weather mean more problems for travelers?

Beginner ESL: Weather and travel

Before you read

Read the title above. What do you think the story is about?

Read the story

Changing weather may cause more disruption to air travel. Summer storms brought long delays to flights near London. Some planes had to wait on the ground while staff managed busy skies. Some planes might need to change their routes. Pilots use weather information to help them avoid dangerous storms. Airports need space and services for passengers whose planes arrive somewhere unexpected.

Words and grammar

1. disruption (noun): a problem that interrupts normal activity
2. delays (plural noun): times when something happens later than planned
3. routes (plural noun): the ways used to travel from one place to another
4. pilots (plural noun): people who fly aircraft
5. passengers (plural noun): people travelling in a vehicle who are not driving or flying it
6. unexpected (adjective): not thought likely to happen

Grammar Focus: Might for possibility
Might need to means a change is possible. It does not say every plane will change its route.
Example from the text: "Some planes might need to change their routes."

Beginner ESL: Courts · mentions deaths of children

Before you read

Read the title above. What do you think the story is about?

Read the story

A judge has declared a mistrial in the Lindsay Clancy murder case. The jury could not agree after discussing the case for a week. The case concerns the deaths of her three children. Her lawyer tried to stop the mistrial, but the attempt failed. Another court meeting is planned for later in the month. The legal outcome remains uncertain.

Words and grammar

1. mistrial (noun): a trial that ends without a valid final decision
2. jury (noun): a group of people who decide facts in a trial
3. uncertain (adjective): not known or not decided
4. judge (noun): a person who leads a court and makes legal decisions
5. lawyer (noun): a person trained to help with legal matters
6. attempt (noun): an effort to do something

Grammar Focus: Remain + adjective
Remains uncertain means continues to be uncertain. It describes the current state, not a future verdict.
Example from the text: "The legal outcome remains uncertain."

Grammar Focus: A claim with a source
In the report limits the statement to what this source says. Suspect does not mean proven responsible.
