Read the story

The number of ship attacks near the Strait of Hormuz is very high. Twelve ships were attacked in just one week. Experts say Iran wants to control the water. One attack caused a fire on a large tanker. Teams rescued eleven sailors, but two men are missing. Middle East oil exports have slowed because of these attacks.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. attacks (noun): violent actions to hurt or damage someone or something
2. experts (noun): people who know a lot about a special subject
3. control (verb): to have power over a place or thing
4. tanker (noun): a large ship that carries oil, gas, or water
5. rescued (verb): saved someone from a bad or dangerous situation
6. missing (adjective): lost or not able to be found
7. exports (noun): products sold and sent to other countries

Grammar Focus: Using "but" to show contrast
We use the word "but" to connect two ideas that are different. In this story, saving eleven sailors is good news, but losing two men is bad news. The word "but" links these two opposite ideas in one sentence.
Example from the text: "Teams rescued eleven sailors, but two men are missing."
