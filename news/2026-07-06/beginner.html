📅 July 06, 2026 - Ukraine: Massive Attack on Kyiv Reported
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

Sunday was a difficult day for Ukraine. There was a massive Russian attack on Kyiv. President Zelensky spoke about this event. He said Russia used 68 missiles. Russia also used 351 strike drones. These attacks caused many deaths. Nineteen people were killed in the Kyiv region. Ukraine now warns of an interceptor missile shortage. They need more interceptor missiles to defend the country. This is a big problem because Ukraine uses them to stop incoming threats.

Words and grammar

1. massive (adjective): very big or very strong
2. attack (noun): a violent act to hurt or damage someone or something
3. missiles (noun): weapons that fly through the air to hit a target
4. strike drones (noun phrase): unmanned flying machines that can hit targets
5. shortage (noun): not having enough of something that is needed
6. killed (verb): caused a person or animal to die

Grammar Focus: Using "because"
We use "because" to explain why something happened or why something is true. It connects a reason to a result.
Example from the text: "This is a big problem because Ukraine uses them to stop incoming threats."
