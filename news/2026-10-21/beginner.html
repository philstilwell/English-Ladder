📅 October 21, 2026 - US Citizen Freed by Russia Without a Prisoner Swap

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Russia freed US citizen Stephen Hubbard. Donald Trump said the former teacher was released without any swap required. A US envoy met Vladimir Putin in St Petersburg for talks. Trump later said the man was on his way back home. Trump said he made a request directly to Putin last week. Hubbard was in poor health and needed treatment in Texas.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. freed (verb): let someone leave a prison or a bad place
2. citizen (noun): a person who legally belongs to a country
3. former (adjective): true in the past but not true now
4. swap (noun): a trade where you give one thing to get another thing
5. envoy (noun): a person who speaks for a leader in another country
6. treatment (noun): medical care for a sick or hurt person

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about finished actions. Some verbs are irregular and do not end in "-ed". For example, the past form of "meet" is "met".
Example from the text: "A US envoy met Vladimir Putin in St Petersburg for talks."
