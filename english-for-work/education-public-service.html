English for Education and Public Service

Education and public-service communication brings together people with different responsibilities and expectations. Practice explaining evidence, inviting a missing perspective, discussing limited resources and clarifying how a decision will be made.

Choose the work you do

Childcare and early education focuses on daily routines, families and classroom colleagues. Education administration covers institutional and student-support communication. Higher education and research covers academic collaboration. Public administration and nonprofit courses address community-facing work, grants, coordination and accountability.

Make the practice practical

Rehearse a short explanation for a specific audience using the supplied facts and frames, then ask a partner to raise the stated complication. Clarify which points are agreed and which still need review. Close by saying the next question or action clearly.
