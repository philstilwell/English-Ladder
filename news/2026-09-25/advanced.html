US and China Exchange Warm Words Amid Lingering Tensions

Advanced · C1+

📅 September 25, 2026 - US and China Exchange Warm Words Amid Lingering Tensions

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

US President Donald Trump and China's President Xi Jinping exchanged warm words at a lavish White House state dinner but gave little indication of progress on pressing issues. Despite diplomatic niceties and gifts, little was shared on substantial issues separating the leaders. Xi stressed that they should ensure AI remain "under human control" and act "as responsible major countries". Trump toasted a future of "harmony, peace and success" for both nations. But tensions remain despite the diplomatic niceties, including over Taiwan, which Beijing claims. Xi urged Trump earlier in the day to take the "correct position" on the self-governing island amid a weapons package paused by Trump. China believes the US is trying to contain its rise as a superpower, and it wants Washington to stay out of its way. Trump has meanwhile been expected to aim for deals around trade and tariffs during the visit, like lowering Chinese tariffs on American goods. Billionaire business leaders and US tech CEOs joined the leaders at the extravagant dinner late on Thursday. The US is legally bound to provide Taiwan with the means to defend itself, but Trump delayed approving a $14bn arms package for it. OpenAI's Sam Altman, Google's Sundar Pichai, and Amazon's Jeff Bezos were also present, as were Mark Zuckerberg and Jeff Yass, the American billionaire who owns a share of TikTok. The evening instead marked a continuation of the lavish welcome Xi has so far received in the US.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. lavish (adjective): Sumptuously rich, elaborate, or luxurious, suggesting great expense or abundance.
2. pressing issues (noun phrase): Urgent and critical matters that demand immediate attention and resolution due to their significance.
3. diplomatic niceties (noun phrase): Polite and formal gestures, remarks, or customary courtesies observed in international relations, often used to maintain civility.
4. substantial issues (noun phrase): Significant and weighty topics or problems that involve considerable importance, impact, or amount.
5. stressed (verb): Strongly emphasized or highlighted a particular point, statement, or idea to convey its importance.
6. toasted (verb): Raised a glass and drank in honor of someone or something, often accompanied by a short speech or celebratory remark.
7. self-governing (adjective): Having the power to govern itself independently; autonomous or self-ruling.
8. amid (preposition): In the middle of, surrounded by, or during a particular situation, event, or set of circumstances.
9. contain (verb): To prevent something, such as an opposing force, influence, or spread, from developing further or having a greater impact.
10. superpower (noun): A very powerful and influential nation that plays a dominant role in international relations and global affairs.
11. legally bound (adjective phrase): Formally obligated or required by law to take a specific action, fulfill a duty, or adhere to an agreement.
12. continuation (noun): The act of maintaining or prolonging an activity, state, or process; something that follows or extends from a previous state.

Advanced Grammar: Expressing Concession with "Despite"
"Despite" is a preposition used to introduce a contrasting idea, indicating that something happens or is true even though there is an obstacle or a different situation. It is typically followed by a noun, a noun phrase, a pronoun, or a gerund (-ing form). It conveys a sense of unexpectedness or contrast between two elements. For example, in the sentence, the lack of progress occurred "despite" the polite gestures.
Example from the text: "Despite diplomatic niceties and gifts, little was shared on substantial issues separating the leaders."
