📅 September 06, 2026 - Weather and air travel

Read the story

Air travel may face more disruption as climate change brings more severe weather. During a busy summer travel period, storms near London caused long delays. Air traffic teams guided aircraft around the storms, reducing the space available for other flights. Some departures had to wait on the ground because the skies were congested. Some planes might need to change their routes. When aircraft land at a different airport, there must be space and facilities for the extra arrivals. Pilots use radar and forecasts to help them avoid dangerous weather. However, fast-moving storms can force changes late in a flight.

Words and grammar

1. severe (adjective): very serious or strong
2. departures (plural noun): flights or journeys that leave a place
3. congested (adjective): too crowded for traffic to move easily
4. facilities (plural noun): buildings, equipment or services provided for a purpose
5. forecasts (plural noun): statements about what is likely to happen in the future
6. air traffic (noun phrase): the movement of aircraft through the sky or around airports
7. radar (noun): equipment that uses radio waves to locate objects or weather conditions
8. available (adjective): ready or possible to use

Grammar Focus: Might for possibility
Might need to means a change is possible. It does not say every plane will change its route.
Example from the text: "Some planes might need to change their routes."
