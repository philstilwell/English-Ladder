Read the story

Japan had very heavy rain recently. This rain was "unprecedented", meaning it was very unusual. Eight people were killed because of the strong rain. The weather caused a big storm. This storm cut power to many homes. More than 20,000 households lost electricity. Also, 7,000 people were stranded at Narita airport. They could not leave the airport. The strong rain made many problems for people. It shows how dangerous bad weather can be.

Words and grammar

1. unprecedented (adjective): Never happened before; very unusual.
2. killed (verb (past tense)): Caused someone to die.
3. storm (noun): Very bad weather with strong wind and rain.
4. cut power (verb phrase): Stopped electricity from working.
5. stranded (adjective): Unable to leave a place because of a problem.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. We often add '-ed' to regular verbs. Irregular verbs change their form, like 'is' becoming 'was' or 'are' becoming 'were'.
Example from the text: "Eight people were killed because of the strong rain."
