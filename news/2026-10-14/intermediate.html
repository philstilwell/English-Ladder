How AI Job Applications Can Backfire for Graduates

Intermediate · B1–B2

News archive →
📅 October 14, 2026 - How AI Job Applications Can Backfire for Graduates

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Recent graduates are increasingly using artificial intelligence to misrepresent themselves when they apply for jobs. One applicant explained that the process was exhausting and demoralising without some help from these tools. He thinks it is hypocritical for companies to ban artificial intelligence when they use it themselves. Meanwhile, a recent survey found that most employers have suspected applicants of misconduct during the selection process. One recruiter does not blame graduates for using these tools, given how brutal the current market is. However, she noted that this creates an unfair situation because some people use the programs heavily while others do not. To better judge applicants, companies are now relying on online assessments and video interviews. Employers stress that they still want to see true authenticity and diversity of thought in new recruits.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. increasingly (adverb): more and more over a period of time
2. misrepresent (verb): to give a false or misleading account of something or someone
3. exhausting (adjective): making you feel extremely physically or mentally tired
4. demoralising (adjective): making you lose confidence, hope, or enthusiasm
5. hypocritical (adjective): behaving in a way that goes against the rules or beliefs one claims to have
6. misconduct (noun): unacceptable, improper, or dishonest behaviour
7. brutal (adjective): extremely difficult, harsh, or unforgiving
8. authenticity (noun): the quality of being real, true, or genuine

Grammar Focus: Present Continuous for Trends
We use the present continuous tense (a form of "be" + verb-ing) to describe situations that are currently changing or developing over time. In this reading, the phrase "are increasingly using" shows a growing trend among people looking for jobs.
Example from the text: "Recent graduates are increasingly using artificial intelligence to misrepresent themselves when they apply for jobs."
