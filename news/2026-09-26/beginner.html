📅 September 26, 2026 - Ed Sheeran Concerts Cancelled Due to Bad Weather Warnings

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ed Sheeran's concerts in Massachusetts on Friday and Saturday have been cancelled because of severe weather warnings in the area. He was scheduled to perform two shows at the Gillette Stadium. The venue made the difficult decision to cancel the Ed Sheeran concerts. This decision was made with the safety of fans and staff as the top priority. Weather warnings for high winds are in force in the area until Sunday. People can get refunds for their tickets now.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. cancelled (verb): stopped from happening; an event that will not take place.
2. severe (adjective): very bad or serious, like a very strong storm.
3. warnings (noun): messages that tell you about possible danger or a problem that might happen.
4. scheduled (verb): planned to happen at a specific time or date.
5. venue (noun): the place where a public event, like a concert or sports game, happens.
6. safety (noun): the condition of being protected from danger or harm.
7. refunds (noun): money that is given back to you, for example, if you return something or an event is cancelled.

Grammar Focus: Using "because of" for reasons
We use "because of" to explain the reason for something. It always comes before a noun or a noun phrase. For example, "The game was cancelled because of the rain."
Example from the text: "Ed Sheeran's concerts in Massachusetts on Friday and Saturday have been cancelled because of severe weather warnings in the area."
