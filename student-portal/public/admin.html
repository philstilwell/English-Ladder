    HOW YOUR STUDENT PORTAL WORKS

    Technical notes

    A reference for the current rules, timing, privacy measures, and limits.

    Specifications updated October 16, 2026. These notes describe the system’s behavior; student details and passwords belong on individual account pages.

    Conversation and meaning activities

    Content
    Conversation Rescue, Meaning Switch, Ask the right question, Story builder, and Missing Details each have 60 original multiple-choice items in six sets of ten: 300 items altogether. Later sets use more demanding language or situations. Start, Build, and Stretch describe the sets, not a CEFR level.
    Practice
    Students choose one of four answers and see an explanation. They may retry or use Show answer. Story builder asks for the order of four sentences. Missing Details includes a model reply to the chosen question.
    Saved choices
    Each activity privately saves the first and latest answer, Finished and Questions choices, Hide finished items, and the last item. Finished and Questions can be undone separately. A retry does not change the original first answer. The administrator's combined Questions list shows the exercise and saved choices. Teacher previews never save.
    AI help and costs
    Copy AI prompt includes only the exercise, answer key, and explanation. It excludes student answers and personal information. The four AI links open regular chat sites. No prompt is sent automatically and this feature makes no paid AI calls. Ordinary hosting and storage usage applies.
    Availability
    Enable the activities for each existing student using Edit account → Student activities. Existing checkbox selections, including none, are preserved. All nine activities are selected by default for new accounts.

    Vocabulary practice

    First release
    50 words in five fixed batches of ten. Another 5,134 distinct words are reserved for later release. Each word has simple teaching notes, a short answer and a student sentence.
    Saved work
    Students save answers privately. Batch changes save first. Conflicting changes need an explicit retry. Finished, Questions, Practice again and the saved batch are separate choices. Teacher previews cannot change them.
    AI self-check
    Copy AI prompt includes only the current ten words, their exercises and the student's typed answers. Students choose an AI site and paste the prompt themselves. Nothing is sent automatically. There are no paid AI calls from this feature.
    Availability
    Existing activity selections are preserved. Enable Vocabulary practice on a student's account page. New students have all nine activities selected by default.

    Combined Questions

    Directory link
    The Questions column shows a count and link when a student has saved questions. It combines English Road Practice, Grammar practice, Business terms, Vocabulary practice, the five conversation and meaning activities, and Study on your own.
    Review
    The combined list includes full exercises, saved Vocabulary answers, and the first and latest choices for the five conversation and meaning activities, twenty questions per page. It includes hidden activities and paused accounts. Earlier saved study topics remain available.
    Undo
    Each item resolved on this Questions page has an Undo option until you leave the page or sign out. Undo restores the original item to both lists, including older study topics and English Road sets. It preserves Finished, answers and scores. It checks the specific resolution and saved revision; newer changes are kept. Failed requests can be retried safely. Recently resolved options stay available through list refreshes and paging, with no countdown or browser storage.
    Resolved
    After a lesson discussion, Resolved clears the item’s saved Questions choice for both the student and instructor. It preserves Finished, answers, first choices, scores, and practice history. Students can save the item to Questions again. An already open student page sees the change when its activity is reopened or refreshed. Only the signed-in administrator can resolve items here; teacher previews stay read-only. Conflicting changes require a refresh before retrying.
    Privacy
    The administrator’s combined lists and directory counts remain restricted to the administrator host. Each student can also read their own combined list through a separate account-bound endpoint. Opening it does not change student work. Use Refresh to check for new questions. Failed checks show Unable to check. These records do not feed CEFR or nightly lesson generation.

    CEFR estimates

    Existing estimates remain available. Automatic CEFR authoring is currently disabled with Cloudflare AI. The Codex nightly task updates lesson feedback, assessments and quizzes only; the evidence rules below apply to a separately authorized estimate review.

    First estimate
    After two distinct usable, redacted transcripts have been saved. The review uses the latest two sessions.
    Monthly review
    No sooner than one calendar month after the previous review. It uses the latest four available sessions within the retention period. If only two or three remain, it uses those. Fewer than two means it waits.
    Evidence and limits
    CEFR means Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. The estimate covers conversational language visible in transcripts, from A1 to C2. It is not a formal test result or an independent assessment of pronunciation, pace, listening, reading, or writing.
    What keeps the level stable
    Every selected transcript is read in full, with learner quotations checked against each session. Teacher examples and quiz scores do not establish the level. Unchanged evidence does not trigger another review. Failed or inconclusive monthly reviews keep the earlier estimate.

    Estimates and supporting examples appear only in the administrator interface. Each account shows its next eligible review date.

    Study on your own

    Saved choices
    Each topic has Finish and Questions buttons. Finish turns the topic grey. Questions adds it to a list for discussion. Students can click either button again to undo it. Choices are saved privately in their account and return on another device.
    Shared review
    Students see their Questions list under Study on your own. The instructor sees it on the student page and on the administrator account page, including paused accounts. Students change their choices. The administrator can resolve a discussed topic in the combined Questions list without changing Finished.
    Later assessments
    An unchanged topic keeps its choices. A rewritten topic starts fresh. Saved questions keep the original topic so it remains available for discussion after a new assessment replaces it. Lists show up to 20 topics per page.
    Privacy and cost
    Choices stay in private server storage, are removed when the account is deleted, and are not used as CEFR or quiz evidence. Saving and viewing them makes no AI calls. The AI prompt text stays hidden; its copy button and icons remain directly available.

    Transcript handling

    Automatic lesson updates
    As requested on October 16, 2026, automatic updates and manual retries no longer run a separate AI transcript-privacy check. Known speaker names and contact patterns are removed by fixed rules. The full remaining text is used temporarily to write the lesson.
    Speaker roles and lesson review
    Student and Instructor labels stay distinct. Every turn and timestamp is preserved. A separate editor still checks the proposed feedback, assessment, and quiz for accurate, English-only teaching and excludes unrelated personal details.
    Full transcript storage
    New full transcripts are not saved without privacy approval. Earlier privacy-reviewed copies remain usable until their normal expiry. Monthly proficiency estimates can use only those reviewed copies, so they wait when fewer than two are available.

    Original Zoom records are unchanged. Basic name and contact removal does not make the complete text anonymous.

    Storage and retention

    Where copies live
    In the website’s private server storage, separated by student account. Full cached transcripts are not sent to browser pages or saved in browser storage.
    Three-month limit
    Nightly cleanup deletes cached copies older than three calendar months, using Eastern lesson dates. It includes all saved versions and paused students, and still runs when publishing is disabled or Zoom is disconnected.
    Records that remain
    Feedback, assessments, brief CEFR evidence, quizzes, and first-choice answer history remain. This cleanup does not delete original Zoom records.

    Account deletion removes that account’s cached transcripts and learning records together. The retired student address cannot be reused.

    Nightly updates and Zoom

    Schedule
    11:30 p.m. New York time through the Codex task in Phil’s existing chat. Keep the Mac awake, online, and running Codex. Cloudflare AI is disabled; the server only checks sources, stores finished results, and maintains existing transcript retention.
    Finding a lesson
    The job checks Zoom for new or changed notes, matches an exact configured speaker name, and confirms the meeting date. It requires the complete transcript and allows time for a newly finished meeting to settle.
    Updates needing attention
    Codex processes eligible queued lessons and reports blockers in the scheduled chat. Completed lessons are skipped. Cloudflare AI retry controls are disabled. Ignore session removes an unmatched session from the queue without deleting its original Zoom transcript.
    Checking source changes
    Completed lessons are skipped. Codex reads each new full transcript temporarily. Before publishing, the server checks that the source, student mapping, assessment and saved quiz attempts still match the reviewed draft. A changed source requires another preparation and review.

    Automatic CEFR authoring is disabled. Historical lessons are imported separately; a software update does not automatically backfill old recordings.

    Feedback and quizzes

    Each eligible lesson
    Produces reviewed language feedback, an updated assessment, and seven new practice questions when the evidence is sufficient. Failed generation keeps the existing useful pages.
    Original answers
    The first choice is kept even after a successful retry. Teacher previews do not count as student attempts. Older quiz wording and saved attempts are preserved.
    Student-facing language
    Instructions use short, clear English. This reading style and quiz performance are not treated as proof of a CEFR level. Study plans suggest independent practice.

Refresh checks for new saved changes; hidden tools and paused accounts keep their history. These records are practice information, separate from lesson quizzes and CEFR estimates.
