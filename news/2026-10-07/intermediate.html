School Demonstrations Grow Across France Over Conditions and Police Actions

Intermediate · B1–B2

📅 October 07, 2026 - School Demonstrations Grow Across France Over Conditions and Police Actions

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Tear gas was used in Paris and Marseille as school demonstrations increased across France. Large gatherings of mostly teenagers demanded better funding for schools and an end to what they called severe actions by officers. Hundreds of thousands of people joined the street actions across France, with high-school pupils, parents, university students, and unions participating. Police encountered demonstrators in Lyon and used tear gas in Paris and Marseille. The government stated that 891 high schools experienced disruption on Tuesday, representing almost a quarter of all lycées. Students explained that school buildings were often dilapidated, with leaking ceilings making it difficult to study. Official offers, such as a website for students to express their grievances, have made little impact so far. There is significant worry about claims of "disproportionate force" used by officers, with investigations now ongoing. A 14-year-old student suffered a fractured jaw and lost teeth after being hit in the face during a school blockade. What began with local anger at overcrowded classrooms and dilapidated school buildings has since extended across the country, reaching some universities.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. increased (verb): became greater in size, number, or degree.
2. gatherings (noun): meetings or assemblies of people, often for a specific purpose.
3. actions (noun): the process of doing something, typically to achieve an aim or in response to a situation.
4. disruption (noun): an interruption in the normal process or activity of something.
5. dilapidated (adjective): in a state of disrepair or ruin, often due to age or neglect.
6. grievances (noun): formal complaints about something considered unfair or wrong.
7. disproportionate (adjective): too large or too small in comparison with something else; not fair or balanced.
8. fractured (adjective): broken or cracked, typically used to describe a bone.
9. blockade (noun): an act or means of sealing off a place to prevent goods or people from entering or leaving.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Simple
The Present Perfect Simple connects an action or state from the past to the present. It is used to describe an action that started in the past and continues into the present, or an action completed in the past but with a result that is relevant now. The structure is 'have/has' + past participle. In the example, "has since extended" shows the spread began in the past and continues or affects the present situation.
Example from the text: "What began with local anger at overcrowded classrooms and dilapidated school buildings has since extended across the country, reaching some universities."
