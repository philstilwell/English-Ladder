Lesson 01: Confirming the booked grounds work
Original fictional case: At Oak Court, worker Asha explains the booked work to resident Ben. Today includes front-lawn mowing and path leaf clearance. Ben asks the crew to shape the rear hedge as well. Hedge work is excluded from this booking. The office can review a separate request, but no price, date, or approval is confirmed. Asha must preserve the existing work scope while passing on the hedge request accurately, without implying that a quick favor is automatically covered.
Language workshop: A hedge request during a lawn visit
Communication goal: Explain the current work order, acknowledge an additional request, and refer it without inventing a price or appointment.
Language guidance: The current booking includes front-lawn mowing and path leaf clearance, not rear-hedge shaping. Referring Ben to the office preserves the additional request but does not approve it, price it, or book it for another day.
Useful expressions (preserve the supplied facts): I understand you would like the rear hedge shaped as well. / Let me confirm today's work order. / Front-lawn mowing is included. / Path leaf clearance is included too. / Rear-hedge work is outside this booking. / You are asking for the rear hedge, not the front lawn. / The office can review a separate request. / I do not have a confirmed price for that work. / No service date is confirmed for the hedge. / I cannot add it as a quick favor to today's scope. / Would you like me to pass the hedge request to the office? / A review request is not task authorization. / Today's listed tasks remain mowing and path leaf clearance. / I am explaining this booking, not ruling out future hedge work. / Confirming the scope does not mean the work is finished. / I will refer the rear-hedge request with price and timing still open.
Vocabulary: work order: Record specifying the tasks assigned for a particular job. / booked scope: Work included in the agreed booking. / grounds maintenance: Upkeep of outdoor areas and landscape features. / front lawn: Grassed area at the front of the property. / mowing: Cutting grass as part of the specified lawn task. / path: Defined walking route through an outdoor area. / leaf clearance: Removal of fallen leaves from the specified area. / rear hedge: Row of shrubs at the back of the site. / hedge shaping: Work intended to change or maintain a hedge's form. / included task: Activity explicitly covered by the booking. / excluded task: Activity outside the current agreed work. / additional request: New work requested beyond the existing arrangement. / separate quotation: Price proposal for work outside the current booking. / site visit: Attendance at a location for defined work or assessment. / crew: Group of workers assigned to the job. / grounds lead: Person supervising or assessing grounds work. / office review: Assessment of a request by the coordinating office. / scope change: Amendment to the agreed work content. / task authorization: Permission to carry out the specified work. / service date: Day on which agreed work is scheduled. / price estimate: Provisional cost figure for specified work. / confirmed booking: Arrangement explicitly agreed and entered through the actual process. / request handoff: Transfer of a new request to the responsible person. / completion claim: Statement that assigned work has been finished.
Speaking task: Read the included tasks: front-lawn mowing and path leaf clearance. Repeat Ben's rear-hedge shaping request and the separate office-review route. Read the price and date answers without adding a quotation or appointment.
Partner: Ben: Resident / Asha: Grounds worker
Guided response task: Select supplied answers for the dialogue gaps. Check the explanations, then read the completed exchange aloud; no original writing is required.
Optional second-round challenge: Elm Court has booked front-lawn mowing and driveway leaf clearance. A resident requests side-hedge shaping. It is excluded from the current booking; the office can review it, with no price or date confirmed.
Model for comparison AFTER my attempt, not a response to give me first: Ben: Can you repeat the two tasks before I speak to the residents? I don't want to say the hedge was missed.
Asha: Mowing is the included task for the front lawn; path leaf clearance is also listed. The rear hedge was not part of today's booking.
Ben: Thanks. Please refer the hedge separately. I'll wait for the office to discuss the price and a possible day.
Asha: Yes. No scope change has been approved in this conversation. I'll send the request with both price and timing still open.

Worker: I'll note that the office owns the inquiry; existing scope remains unchanged.
Scheduling clerk: That preserves today's completion and the customer's right to consider the options without creating an accidental scope change.
