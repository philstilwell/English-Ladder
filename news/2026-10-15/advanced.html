Read the story

Naval authorities state that eight of twelve crew members are unaccounted for after a drone strike left a tanker severely damaged off Romania's coast. A substantial search and rescue operation is currently underway in the Black Sea following this latest attack in the region. The immediate focus is to reach the four personnel confirmed alive, while safeguarding rescue teams against hazardous conditions, including very high waves. Romania's authorities have deployed Black Hawk helicopters and marine rescue boats to assist the stricken vessel. The western portion of the Black Sea has witnessed numerous incidents, and a Russian drone swarm was detected overnight near Ukraine's Snake Island. Earlier in the week, another commercial ship sank further south along the Bulgarian coast following a drone strike, leaving eleven additional sailors missing. In response to that suspected attack, Bulgaria officially summoned the Russian envoy to address the mounting crisis. Consequently, Romanian and Bulgarian leaders are scheduled to deliberate shortly on whether to trigger Article 4 of the NATO treaty. This diplomatic mechanism permits any member state to demand formal discussions if its territorial integrity or national security is perceived to be under threat. Meanwhile, Moscow maintains that it strikes vessels delivering weaponry or dual-use cargo to Ukrainian ports. Conversely, European leaders emphatically condemn the strikes, warning that the Black Sea is becoming another front of escalation in the conflict.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. unaccounted for (adjective phrase): Describing people whose location and physical safety remain unknown following a disaster or accident.
2. substantial (adjective): Of considerable importance, size, scale, or worth.
3. safeguarding (verb): Protecting someone or something from harm, damage, or dangerous conditions.
4. stricken (adjective): Seriously affected by an undesirable condition, extensive damage, or a disaster.
5. swarm (noun): A large number of objects, such as drones or insects, moving together in a dense and coordinated group.
6. envoy (noun): A representative or messenger, often diplomatic, sent by a government to deal with another state.
7. deliberate (verb): To think about or discuss an issue carefully and thoroughly before making a decision.
8. integrity (noun): The state of being whole, undivided, and securely established, particularly regarding a nation's territorial borders.
9. dual-use (adjective): Designed or suitable to be used for both civilian commercial applications and military purposes.
10. escalation (noun): A rapid or sudden increase in intensity, seriousness, or scale, particularly in conflicts.

Advanced Grammar: Participle clauses after conjunctions
In Advanced English, writers frequently reduce a time clause by combining a conjunction like "while" with an -ing participle (e.g., "while safeguarding"). This concise structure shows two simultaneous actions occurring, keeping the sentence flowing without repeating the subject.
Example from the text: "The immediate focus is to reach the four personnel confirmed alive, while safeguarding rescue teams against hazardous conditions, including very high waves."
