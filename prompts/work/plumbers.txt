Lesson 01: Identifying fixtures and service history
Original fictional case: Tenant Mina reports a sink problem in Unit 3B. A bathroom basin and a utility sink stand beside one another. She means the bathroom basin and noticed water on the cabinet base at 07:00. The source of the water has not been established. Service coordinator Ellis needs an accurate description for the plumber, using what Mina has already observed. No earlier repair history is supplied, and no instruction to operate or dismantle equipment is needed.
Language workshop: Which sink in Unit 3B?
Communication goal: Clarify a customer report using fixture, location, and observation time without turning a symptom into a diagnosis.
Language guidance: Water was noticed on the cabinet base at 07:00. This does not establish when it first appeared or which component caused it. Keep the fixture identity, observed location, and time together.
Useful expressions (preserve the supplied facts): Do you mean the bathroom basin or the utility sink? / I have the location as Unit 3B. / The utility sink is beside it, but it is not the fixture you mean. / Where had you already noticed the water? / You first noticed it at 07:00. / I will record that as the observation time. / The water was on the cabinet base. / The tenant reports water beneath the bathroom basin. / The source has not been established. / I cannot identify a failed connection from that description. / A supply connection and a waste connection are different. / By tap, do you mean the basin faucet? / No earlier repair history has been supplied. / I cannot say the issue has been resolved. / Bathroom basin, Unit 3B, cabinet base, noticed at 07:00. / I will pass that description to the plumber.
Vocabulary: fixture: Plumbing appliance such as a basin, sink, or toilet. / bathroom basin: Bowl used for washing hands or face in a bathroom. / utility sink: Sink intended for cleaning or general utility tasks. / vanity unit: Bathroom cabinet assembly associated with a basin. / cabinet base: Bottom surface within the cabinet. / service call: Request or attendance for a reported plumbing issue. / tenant report: Account given by the person occupying the premises. / unit number: Identifier of an individual apartment or premises. / fixture location: Position of the particular plumbing appliance. / observation time: Time when someone noticed a condition. / onset: Point when a condition actually began. / source: Origin of the reported water or problem. / supply connection: Connection associated with water delivery to a fixture. / waste connection: Connection associated with carrying used water away. / trap: Fitting designed to retain a liquid seal in a drainage system. / faucet: Fitting that controls delivery of water at a fixture. / tap: Common alternative term for a faucet. / visible moisture: Wetness that can be seen at the reported location. / service history: Record of earlier attendance or work. / previous repair: Corrective work completed on an earlier occasion. / reported symptom: Condition described by a customer before assessment. / call summary: Concise record of information from the conversation. / static pressure: In this plumbing report, pressure at a point with no flow. / attendance: Visit by the relevant service person.
Speaking task: Read turns 1-10 in pairs. Stress bathroom, cabinet base, and noticed at seven to keep fixture, location, and time distinct. Switch roles for turns 11-20. Keep not supplied separate from never repaired, and arrange attendance separate from a booked time. Complete and check the handoff exchange. Read it twice, preserving basin, base, 07:00, and unestablished.
Partner: Mina: Tenant / Ellis: Service coordinator
Guided response task: Select supplied answers for the dialogue gaps. Check the explanations, then read the completed exchange aloud; no original writing is required.
Optional second-round challenge: Complete the handoff using the tenant account. Keep the fixture, observed surface, time, and cause status distinct.
Model for comparison AFTER my attempt, not a response to give me first: Mina: Could you read the request back? Especially the location. I don't want the two sinks mixed up when the plumber sees the job.
Ellis: The call summary is Unit 3B, bathroom basin, water on the cabinet base, noticed at 07:00. Source unestablished; earlier repair history not supplied.
Mina: That's right. It includes what I saw and doesn't add a cause. I haven't been given a visit time yet, either.
Ellis: I'll pass it through to arrange attendance. I haven't confirmed a time in this conversation, so please don't treat seven as the plumber's arrival time.

Lesson 02: Clarifying pipe and component specifications
Original fictional case: Buyer Omar compares a nominal pipe designation on an order with a measured outside diameter and assumes the delivery is wrong because the numbers differ. The order names a product series, but the delivery label is incomplete. Supplier coordinator Leila must explain the two kinds of size information and request complete identification. Neither material compatibility nor connection compatibility has been verified. Explaining nominal size does not establish that the delivery is correct.
Language workshop: A designation is not a measurement

Building client: Please don't copy the previous model's terms under a different title.

Building client: Can the service office own that query, since your site team is moving to another project next week?

Plumber: Please give Leo the agreed contact details. The register can retain the unresolved document status during that transfer.
Building client: Should I promise the tenant that any future problem with the faucet will be covered once this is corrected?
Plumber: No broad coverage promise is established here. The actual terms and the particular circumstances need appropriate review.
Building client: I'll call this a document-reference correction, keeping coverage questions separate from the mismatch.
Vocabulary in this exchange: asset register: A list linking plumbing equipment or fixtures to their locations, identifiers, and relevant records. / warranty reference: The product or document identifier used to locate applicable warranty terms for an item.
