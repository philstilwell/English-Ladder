Read the story

A comprehensive police manhunt is underway in Berlin following a grave incident at a recent Pride event. On Saturday night, an unidentified suspect deliberately rammed a vehicle into a crowd of participants, causing immediate alarm and disruption. The unknown perpetrator fled the scene promptly after the egregious act, prompting urgent law enforcement mobilisation. Authorities are currently piecing together the precise timeline of events that began unfolding in the German capital. The incident occurred amidst festive celebrations for the LGBTQ+ community, tragically marring the occasion. Security forces have intensified their efforts to apprehend the individual responsible for this targeted assault. Investigators are examining all available evidence, including witness statements and surveillance footage, to identify the culprit. The gravity of the situation has prompted increased public vigilance and calls for enhanced security measures at public gatherings. This shocking act underscores ongoing concerns regarding the safety of public events and the potential for politically or ideologically motivated violence. The wider community is now awaiting further details from the extensive and ongoing investigation into the incident.

Words and grammar

1. manhunt (noun): A systematic and intensive search by police or military for a person, especially a criminal or fugitive.
2. suspect (noun): A person thought to be guilty of a crime or offence.
3. rammed (verb (past participle)): Forcefully struck or pushed something, often with a vehicle, typically causing damage or injury through violent impact.
4. perpetrator (noun): A person who carries out a harmful, illegal, or immoral act.
5. egregious (adjective): Outstandingly bad, shocking, or reprehensible, often used to describe severe mistakes or offenses.

Advanced Grammar: Idiomatic Phrasing: 'is underway'
The phrase 'is underway' is a formal and idiomatic expression used to indicate that an event, process, or activity is currently happening or in progress. It functions as a predicative adjective, meaning it describes the state of the subject (e.g., 'the manhunt') and conveys a sense of continuation. This construction is frequently encountered in news reports and official statements, offering a concise way to communicate ongoing actions.
Example from the text: "A comprehensive police manhunt is underway in Berlin following a grave incident at a recent Pride event."
