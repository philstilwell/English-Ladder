📅 August 27, 2026 - Glacier Collapse Causes Devastating Nepal-Tibet Floods

Read the story

Big floods happened in Nepal and Tibet. These floods caused much damage. Scientists are studying the reason for the floods. They think a glacier collapsed. A glacier is a very big piece of ice. When a glacier melts or breaks, it can be dangerous. The collapsed glacier caused the water to rise very fast. This led to devastating floods in the region. Scientists are doing preliminary investigations. They see that Himalayan ice is melting quickly.

Words and grammar

1. collapsed (verb): fell down suddenly
2. glacier (noun): a very large area of ice, moving slowly
3. floods (noun): a lot of water covering land that is usually dry
4. devastating (adjective): causing a lot of damage and destruction
5. preliminary investigations (noun phrase): first studies or checks to find out something
6. region (noun): an area of a country or of the world

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that are finished. We use it for things that happened before now. For many verbs, we add “-ed”. For some verbs, the word changes completely.
Example from the text: "Big floods happened in Nepal and Tibet."
