Read the story

The United States launched new strikes. These strikes happened recently. Iran says the US strikes hit bridges. Bridges are part of civilian infrastructure. Tehran is the capital city of Iran. The US also boarded a ship. This ship was in the Strait of Hormuz. The Strait of Hormuz is an important sea area. The US acted because of recent events. Iran says its infrastructure was hit.

Words and grammar

1. launched (verb (past tense)): started something new, like an attack.
2. strikes (noun (plural)): attacks, often by planes or missiles.
3. hit (verb (past tense)): made contact with something with force.
4. civilian infrastructure (noun phrase): public buildings and systems like roads or bridges, used by everyone, not the military.
5. boarded (verb (past tense)): went onto a ship or plane.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or events that started and finished in the past. We often add "-ed" to regular verbs. For irregular verbs, the form changes.
Example from the text: "The US also boarded a ship."
