Read the story

It is too early to say if Iran had any involvement in an attack by a pilot who stabbed another pilot and tried to take over a flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv, Israel's prime minister has said. The Flydubai plane plunged more than 17,000ft (5,200m) two hours into its journey on Wednesday, before passengers and crew overpowered the attacker and retook control to land safely. One passenger, Yaniv Hayun, described how he put the attacker, who was trying to disable the plane, in a chokehold before other passengers helped pin him down. The alleged attacker, who was arrested in Saudi Arabia, is expected to be transferred to the United Arab Emirates (UAE), where the plane took off from, Netanyahu told Fox News. Asked whether there was any indication as of now of Iran's involvement, he replied: "It's too early to say". Preliminary findings from a separate Israeli investigation, suggest that the alleged attacker intended to crash the plane, an Israeli security source told BBC Arabic. Eyewitness accounts and a statement by Netanyahu confirmed one of the pilots had stabbed the other and, according to the Israeli prime minister, "apparently tried to crash" the aircraft. One passenger, Hayun, described how he and others on the flight had restrained the attacker. He added that he "pulled the controls" of the plane and after it levelled out, another pilot took over control. Flydubai said "an altercation occurred in the flight deck" and the flight was "successfully secured by on-duty Flydubai crew travelling on the flight, who diverted and landed the aircraft safely at Tabuk airport". All of the airline's flights to and from Israel will be suspended during the investigation, a spokesperson said in a statement.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. involvement (noun): The act or state of being connected or implicated in an event, activity, or situation.
2. plunged (verb): Fell or dropped sharply and rapidly from a great height or into a difficult situation.
3. chokehold (noun): A wrestling or self-defense hold that applies pressure to a person's neck, restricting their breathing or blood flow.
4. pin him down (phrasal verb): To hold someone firmly against a surface, preventing them from moving or escaping.
5. alleged (adjective): Stated as a fact but without definite proof; often implying that a suspected wrongdoing has taken place.
6. indication (noun): A sign or piece of information that suggests something is true or exists, but is not conclusive evidence.
7. preliminary (adjective): Denoting an action or event preceding or done in preparation for something fuller, more important, or conclusive.
8. intended (verb): Had a particular purpose, aim, or plan in mind; meant to do something.
9. aircraft (noun): Any machine capable of flight, such as an airplane, helicopter, or glider.
10. restrained (verb): Held back or kept under control by physical force, preventing someone from moving or acting freely.
11. levelled out (phrasal verb): Reached a stable or horizontal position after ascending or descending, or became steady after a period of change.
12. altercation (noun): A noisy argument or dispute, often public or minor in scale.
13. flight deck (noun): The cockpit of an aircraft, where the pilots operate the controls.
14. diverted (verb): Caused a journey, course, or attention to turn aside from an intended route or destination.
15. secured (verb): Successfully gained control of something, making it safe, stable, or protected from danger.

Advanced Grammar: Impersonal "It" for General Statements
The impersonal pronoun "It" is frequently used at the beginning of a sentence to introduce general statements, observations, or assessments without a specific subject. This construction, common with expressions of time, weather, or evaluation, allows for a more formal, objective, or delayed introduction of the main clause, making the statement sound less personal and more broadly applicable. It helps to foreground the statement itself rather than who is making it.
Example from the text: "It is too early to say if Iran had any involvement in an attack by a pilot who stabbed another pilot and tried to take over a flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv, Israel's prime minister has said."
