📅 September 04, 2026 - A series of suspected attacks

Read the story

The report describes a growing concentration of suspected sabotage incidents at European military and defence facilities. Germany has blamed Russia for an attack involving explosive drones at Leipzig airport. The airport is used by Ukraine to transport military supplies. Russian officials reject the accusation. Suspicious fires at other defence facilities have raised concerns, although some may ultimately be judged accidental. Investigators in Estonia are considering possible Russian involvement following a factory fire and arrests. Slovak police have reported preventing a fire attack on a Ukrainian-owned drone manufacturer. One analyst interprets the suspected campaign as an attempt to pressure countries supporting Ukraine. Another sees it as a way to test vulnerabilities and assess governments’ willingness to respond. Russia is the main suspect in the report.

Words and grammar

1. sabotage (noun): deliberate damage intended to disrupt an activity or organisation
2. facilities (plural noun): buildings or equipment provided for particular activities
3. accusation (noun): a statement that someone has done something wrong
4. ultimately (adverb): finally, after other events or considerations
5. manufacturer (noun): a person or business that makes products
6. vulnerabilities (plural noun): weaknesses that could allow harm or disruption
7. assess (verb): carefully judge or evaluate something
8. concentration (noun): a collection of incidents or activities occurring closely together
9. interprets (verb): offers an explanation of what an event or pattern may mean
10. willingness (noun): readiness to choose to act or respond

Advanced Grammar: A claim with a source
“In the report” limits the statement to what this source says. “Suspect” does not mean proven responsible.
Example from the text: "Russia is the main suspect in the report."
