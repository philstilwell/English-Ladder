📅 November 07, 2026 - Katherine Ryan Feels Sadness and Relief After Court Decision

Read the story

Comedian Katherine Ryan spoke about her feelings after Russell Brand was found guilty in court. On Thursday, the court found him guilty of two kinds of sexual crime. Ryan said she felt both sadness and relief after the court's decision. She shared how she felt on her podcast, an online show people can listen to. Ryan said she admired the people who had been hurt. Ryan said what happened would still affect their lives, whatever the court decided. Brand will learn his punishment on Monday.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. spoke (verb, past simple): Talked about something.
2. found guilty (verb phrase): A court decided that someone broke the law.
3. sadness (noun): The feeling you have when you are unhappy.
4. relief (noun): The feeling you have when you are less worried after something difficult.
5. shared (verb, past simple): Told other people about something.
6. admired (verb, past simple): Felt respect for someone, for example because they did something brave.
7. affect (verb): To change something about a person's life.
8. punishment (noun): Something a person must do or accept because they broke a rule or law.

Grammar Focus: Past simple for finished actions
Use the past simple for actions that are finished. Many verbs end in “-ed” in the past simple, like “shared”. Some verbs change in a different way. For example, “speak” becomes “spoke”.
Example from the text: "She shared how she felt on her podcast, an online show people can listen to."
