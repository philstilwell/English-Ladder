Read the story

Nepal had a very sudden flash flood. The flood happened in an area north of Kathmandu. This area is close to the Tibet border. At least 17 people were killed by the flood. Many people feel sad about this terrible news. The big flood might have swept away villages. Some homes are now missing in the affected places. The flood happened quickly after very heavy rain. News reports feared more damage to other areas. The government will now help the people affected by the water.

Words and grammar

1. flash flood (noun): A very sudden and powerful flood that happens quickly, often after heavy rain.
2. killed (verb (past tense)): Caused someone or something to die.
3. border (noun): A line that separates two countries, states, or areas.
4. swept away (verb phrase (past tense)): Carried away by water or wind with great force.
5. feared (verb (simple past)): Thought that something bad or dangerous might happen.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense for Finished Actions
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that happened and finished completely in the past. For regular verbs, we usually add '-ed'. For irregular verbs, the form changes.
Example from the text: "At least 17 people were killed by the flood."
