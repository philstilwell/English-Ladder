📅 September 25, 2026 - Trump and Xi Meet: Warm Words, Little Progress on Key Issues
US President Trump and China's President Xi met for a dinner but did not agree on big issues.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

US President Donald Trump and China's President Xi Jinping met for a big dinner. They exchanged warm words at the dinner, but they did not show much progress on big issues. Many business leaders and tech company heads also went to the dinner. President Trump gave President Xi a sculpture of a bald eagle as a gift. The dinner food had American and Chinese styles. They will meet again for tea the next day.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. President (noun): The highest leader of a country.
2. dinner (noun): The main meal of the day, usually eaten in the evening.
3. exchanged (verb): To give something to someone and receive something in return.
4. progress (noun): Movement forward or improvement.
5. leaders (noun): People who guide or direct others.
6. gift (noun): Something given to someone, often to show friendship or thanks.

Grammar Focus: Using "but" to connect ideas
"But" is a word we use to connect two different ideas in one sentence. It shows a contrast or something unexpected between the two parts. For example, 'It was sunny, but it was cold.'
Example from the text: "They exchanged warm words at the dinner, but they did not show much progress on big issues."

📅 September 24, 2026 - Protein Powder Prices Go Up
The cost of protein powder is increasing, and people are feeling it.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Amarpreet has been drinking protein shakes for many years. His protein shakes are now much more expensive. In 2020, his protein cost about £60. Last year, the price went up to £120. More people want protein, but there is not enough. So, the cost of ingredients is higher. Amarpreet will buy a cheaper brand now. Evie will not buy protein powder anymore.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. protein shakes (noun): A drink with a lot of protein, often used by people who exercise.
2. expensive (adjective): Costs a lot of money.
3. cost (verb): To have a certain price.
4. price (noun): The amount of money you need to pay for something.
5. ingredients (noun): Different foods or things you mix to make food or a product.
6. cheaper (adjective): Costs less money; not as expensive.
7. anymore (adverb): Not now; not happening in the future.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or situations that happened at a specific time in the past and are now finished. For example, 'went up' tells us about a price change that already happened.
Example from the text: "Last year, the price went up to £120."

A UK regulator is checking if Pornhub's new age verification system effectively stops children from seeing adult content.

Companies can pay a large fine if they do not follow the rules.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. investigated (verb): checked by officials to find out facts about something.
2. regulator (noun): a group that officially controls a business or activity.
3. system (noun): a set of parts that work together for a specific purpose.
4. worries (verb): feels anxious or unhappy about something.
5. adult content (noun): pictures or videos that are only suitable for people aged 18 or older.
6. fine (noun): money that you must pay as a punishment for breaking a rule or law.
7. follow (verb): to obey or act according to a rule, instruction, or example.

Grammar Focus: Present Simple for Facts and Ongoing Actions
We use the present simple tense to talk about facts that are always true or actions that happen regularly. It also describes situations that are generally ongoing.
Example from the text: "Pornhub is a website that is being investigated."
