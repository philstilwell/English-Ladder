English for Technology and Data Teams

Technical communication connects specialist knowledge with decisions other people can understand. Practice explaining a result, separating evidence from a hypothesis, clarifying requirements and handing over an unresolved issue.

Choose the work you do

Choose general IT for service and infrastructure conversations, cybersecurity for risk and incident communication, or AI development for model and research discussions. Engineering, semiconductor, product management and data analytics each provide their own cases and terminology.

Make the practice practical

Study a dialogue and underline an expression that qualifies a claim or asks for evidence. Recreate the conversation without the script, keeping the numbers and uncertainty accurate. Try a second version for a colleague who does not share your technical vocabulary.
