Read the story

Israel is currently accused of a controversial strategy in the West Bank. The nation is allegedly weaponising the field of archaeology to support its territorial claims. Critics argue that ancient sites in the West Bank are becoming tools for political ends. These accusations concern plans for large-scale land expropriations in historical Palestinian areas. Such actions could significantly alter the demographic and cultural landscape of the region. The international community is closely monitoring the situation regarding these ancient territories. Many believe that historical narratives should not be exploited for modern political gain. Palestinian residents are deeply concerned about losing their ancestral lands and heritage. These disputed areas have long been a source of tension and conflict. The debate highlights complex issues surrounding land rights, history, and international law.

Words and grammar

1. large-scale (adjective): Involving a lot of people, things, or money; extensive.
2. conflict (noun): A serious disagreement or argument, often involving prolonged fighting or hostility between groups or nations.
3. argue (verb): To give reasons or evidence in support of an idea, action, or theory, often with the aim of persuading others.
4. significantly (adverb): to a degree that is important or noticeable
5. territorial (adjective): connected with control or ownership of an area of land
6. monitoring (verb): watching a situation carefully over time to notice changes
7. regarding (preposition): about or in connection with a particular subject
8. residents (plural noun): people who live in a particular place

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself rather than who or what is performing the action. It is formed with a form of 'to be' + the past participle of the main verb. We often use it when the agent (doer) is unknown, unimportant, or obvious, or when we want to shift emphasis to the action or recipient of the action.
Example from the text: "Israel is currently accused of a controversial strategy in the West Bank."
