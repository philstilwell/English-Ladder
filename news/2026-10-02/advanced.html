📅 October 02, 2026 - Pilot's Heroism on Flydubai Flight After Co-Pilot Attack

Read the story

The Indian pilot of the Flydubai plane, who was stabbed by his co-pilot and opened the cockpit door to let others in, has said he was determined not to let others die. Capt Smit Machchhar said he was on the floor and still being attacked when he realised the plane carrying more than 170 passengers was sinking and he had to do something. "I was lying on the floor injured but I told myself to go for one last push to open the door from inside," he said in a video call with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Others came in and also played a role in controlling the situation. Flydubai's flight FZ1073, bound for Tel Aviv from Dubai, plunged more than 17,000ft (5,200m) two and a half hours into its journey. It first sent an emergency signal to air traffic controllers before switching to code squawk 7500, which indicates hijacking. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu later told the media that one pilot had stabbed the other before "apparently trying to crash" the plane. After fellow passengers overpowered the attacker, two reserve pilots, who were onboard, safely landed the plane at Tabuk Airport in north-western Saudi Arabia. Machchhar, who has been hailed as a "true hero" by Netanyahu, was taken to hospital before being airlifted to Abu Dhabi, where he is reported to be in a stable condition. Netanyahu told Fox News the attacker was an Omani national and said investigations suggested he had undergone "Islamist radical indoctrination". He said that once the attacker had been subdued and tied up by passengers and crew, he was "calling out to the aircrew 'kill me, kill me', so it's clear he was suicidal - that's not a question". The United Arab Emirates has begun investigating "whether the incident was linked to any terrorist activity or purpose", according to the country's state news agency. Indian social media has been awash with praise for Machchhar since his identity emerged. The Indian Prime Minister told him that "his courage, patience and quick decision-making saved the lives of so many people" and the whole country was proud of him. The Indian embassy in the UAE said Machchhar was "in good health and recovering well". "I am not looking at this wound like a wound because that would make my recovery even more difficult," Machchhar said.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. played a role (verb phrase): To have been involved in or contributed to a particular action or situation.
2. airlifted (verb): Transported by aircraft, typically a helicopter, especially from an inaccessible area or in an emergency.
3. stable condition (noun phrase): A medical status indicating that a patient's vital signs are normal and their condition is not deteriorating, although they may still be seriously ill.
4. undergone (verb (past participle)): Experienced or subjected to something, typically something unpleasant, challenging, or transformative.
5. radical indoctrination (noun phrase): The process of teaching someone a set of beliefs or principles in a way that is intense, thorough, and often resistant to change, leading to extreme views.
6. subdued (verb (past participle)): Overpowered and brought under control, typically by force.
7. suicidal (adjective): Having or expressing the intention of taking one's own life.
8. awash with praise (idiomatic phrase): Completely covered or overwhelmed with expressions of approval, admiration, or commendation.
9. decision-making (noun): The process of making choices, especially important ones, often in a complex situation.
10. recovering well (verb phrase): Making good progress in regaining health or strength after an illness or injury.
11. recovery (noun): The process of regaining health, strength, or a normal state after an injury or illness.

Advanced Grammar: Past Perfect Passive Voice
The past perfect passive voice is used to describe an action that was completed before another past action, where the subject of the sentence is the receiver of the action. It is formed using 'had been' followed by the past participle of the main verb. This structure emphasizes the state or result of a past action that occurred before another point in the past.
Example from the text: "He said that once the attacker had been subdued and tied up by passengers and crew, he was "calling out to the aircrew 'kill me, kill me', so it's clear he was suicidal - that's not a question"."
