Summer Wildfires Cause Hidden Damage

Beginner · A1–A2

News archive →
📅 September 13, 2026 - Summer Wildfires Cause Hidden Damage

Read the story

Summer wildfires caused a lot of damage. Scientists say the damage is much bigger than what people can see. Fires burned about 2,760 acres in north Wales. People often see black plants, but the real problem is under the ground. This soil is like a sponge for water and helps store carbon. Losing the soil makes climate change worse and the land weaker. This important soil can take thousands of years to grow again. Scientists are collecting soil samples to study the damage.

Words and grammar

1. wildfires (noun): large fires that burn in forests or natural areas, often out of control.
2. caused (verb, past tense): made something happen or created a result.
3. damage (noun): harm or hurt to something, making it less good or useful.
4. scientists (noun): people who study nature and the world to understand how things work.
5. problem (noun): something that is difficult or causes trouble.
6. soil (noun): the top layer of earth where plants grow.
7. collecting (verb): bringing things together, often to keep or study them.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that happened and finished in the past. For regular verbs, we often add '-ed', but some common verbs have irregular forms.
Example from the text: "Summer wildfires caused a lot of damage."
