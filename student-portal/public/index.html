    Activities

    Resources for your English

    Choose a skill. Open one site and practice for 5–10 minutes. The “Start here” links are good places to begin.

    All resources Listening Speaking Reading and news Grammar and words Writing and work

    Links open in a new tab. Some sites show ads or offer paid extras. Your work on these sites is not saved in English Ladder.

    Listening

    A1 · Start here

    British Council: A1 listening ↗

    Listen to short conversations. Read the words, then try the exercises.

    Try this: Listen once without reading. Then listen again with the text.

    A1–C1 · Choose your level

    ELLLO ↗

    Hear people from different countries. Many lessons have text and a short quiz.

    Try this: Choose A1 or A2 for an easier start.

    Start with “Easy”

    Randall’s Listening Lab ↗

    Listen to everyday conversations and answer questions.

    Try this: Try one quiz. Listen again to the parts you missed.

    Clear, slow audio

    Spotlight English ↗

    Listen to stories about people, places, and ideas. Read the words as you listen.

    Try this: Choose one story. Say two things you learned.

    Speaking

    A1 · Start here

    British Council: A1 speaking ↗

    Watch short videos. Learn useful phrases for everyday conversations.

    Try this: Pause the video and say each phrase aloud.

    Short conversations

    ESL Fast: Easy Conversations ↗

    Practice simple conversations about daily life.

    Try this: Read both parts aloud. Then try one part without looking.

    Words in real videos

    YouGlish ↗

    Type a word or phrase. Hear how people say it in video clips.

    Try this: Listen to one short sentence. Pause and say it aloud.

    Reading and news

    Levels 1–3 · New stories

    News in Levels ↗

    Read and listen to short news stories. Each story has three versions.

    Try this: Start with Level 1. Tell the story in two sentences.

    Levels 0–6 · New lessons

    Breaking News English ↗

    Read news with listening and language exercises.

    Try this: Choose an “Easier” lesson and start with Level 0 or 1.

    Grammar and words

    A1–A2 · Start here

    British Council: A1–A2 grammar ↗

    Read a short explanation. Check your understanding with exercises.

    Try this: Choose one topic from your assessment’s “Things to practice”.

    A1–C2 · Choose your level

    Cambridge English activities ↗

    Find short exercises for grammar, words, and other English skills.

    Try this: Choose “Basic” for A1–A2 practice. Pick a five-minute activity.

    Sentences and translations

    ManyThings: Sentences with Audio ↗

    Hear example sentences at normal or slow speed. Translations include Japanese and other languages.

    Try this: Choose three sentences. Listen and repeat each one.

    Writing and work

    Free writing practice

    Cambridge Write & Improve ↗

    Choose a task and write a short answer. Get automatic feedback, then make changes.

    Try this: Try a beginner task. You can start without an account.

    A2–B1 · Work emails

    British Council: English for emails ↗

    Learn how to start, finish, and check an email in English.

    Try this: Write a short practice email to arrange a meeting.

    Longer explanations

    The Punctuation Guide ↗

    See how to use commas and other marks in writing.

    Try this: Read one rule and its examples. Then write your own sentence.
