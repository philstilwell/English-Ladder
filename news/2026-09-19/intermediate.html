📅 September 19, 2026 - Ed Sheeran Addresses Macklemore Controversy and Gaza

Read the story

Singer Ed Sheeran addressed a controversy and the Israel-Gaza situation at his recent Philadelphia concert. Rapper Macklemore was dropped from Sheeran's concert series following pro-Palestinian comments. Sheeran opened his show alone, acknowledging the fallout from Macklemore's removal and apologizing to fans. He stated that he never intended to be an "activist musician" but was now in the middle of a significant argument about free speech. Sheeran described the 7 October 2023 attacks as "horrific" and the situation in Gaza as "unjustifiable." This controversy caused several artists to exit the concert dates, and ticket sales reduced. The singer said his promoters, not he, decided to remove Macklemore from the lineup. He emphasized that his concerts have always been a welcoming environment, aiming for connection, not separation. Sheeran hoped that his performance could offer a gathering place for everyone, regardless of their views. Some fans attended the show, expressing their resolve to enjoy the music and support freedom of speech. Protesters also gathered outside the venue, showing support for the Palestinian people's struggle. Robert Kraft, owner of Gillette Stadium, confirmed he had forbidden Macklemore from performing at future shows.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. controversy (noun): A serious public disagreement or debate involving different opinions.
2. acknowledging (verb): Accepting or admitting the truth or existence of something, especially something difficult or unwelcome.
3. fallout (noun): The negative results or consequences that follow an event or situation.
4. significant (adjective): Important or noticeable.
5. unjustifiable (adjective): Impossible to excuse or defend because it is too wrong or unfair.
6. exit (verb): To leave a place, situation, or group.
7. connection (noun): A relationship in which people, things, or ideas are linked or associated.
8. resolve (noun): A strong determination to do something, especially after a period of doubt or difficulty.
9. struggle (noun): A difficult effort or a forceful attempt to achieve something, often against opposition.
10. forbidden (adjective): Not allowed; prohibited.

Grammar Focus: Reported Speech
Reported speech is used to tell someone what another person said without quoting their exact words. We often use verbs like "said," "stated," or "confirmed," and typically shift the original verb tense back one step. For example, if someone says "I am tired," in reported speech it becomes "He said he was tired." We often use 'that' after the reporting verb, though it can sometimes be omitted.
Example from the text: "He stated that he never intended to be an "activist musician" but was now in the middle of a significant argument about free speech."
