📅 October 19, 2026 - Japan plans mission to get dust from a Mars moon
A space mission from Japan will try to bring dust from a Martian moon back to Earth.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

A space mission will launch soon to visit a moon of Mars. The spacecraft will leave from Japan and arrive at the moon in 2027. A small robot will look for a safe place to land. The spacecraft will touch down on the surface and grab some dust. A small capsule will bring this dust back to Earth in 2031. Scientists hope the dust will explain how the moons of Mars formed. The spacecraft will bring back only a tiny amount of material. Even a small amount is enough to learn more about space.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. mission (noun): an important job that usually involves travelling somewhere
2. launch (verb): to send a machine into the sky or space
3. spacecraft (noun): a vehicle that travels in space
4. surface (noun): the top or outside part of something like a planet
5. tiny (adjective): very small
6. amount (noun): how much there is of something

Grammar Focus: Future tense with will
We use the word "will" before a main verb to talk about the future. In this story, it describes what the spacecraft is planned to do.
Example from the text: "The spacecraft will leave from Japan and arrive at the moon in 2027."

📅 October 18, 2026 - Thousands Protest Over Cost of Living in France
More than half a million people protested in France about high prices and problems in schools.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

More than half a million people protested in France. They are angry about the high cost of living. Many students also protested because their schools need more teachers. Most people marched peacefully during the afternoon. Later in the day, some groups started fires in the streets. Police arrested more than 1,200 people because of the violence. The government promised to find extra teachers to help the schools.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. protested (verb): showed that you disagree with something by standing together with others in the street.
2. cost of living (noun phrase): the amount of money you need to pay for basic things like food and housing.
3. peacefully (adverb): in a quiet and calm way, without fighting or breaking things.
4. fires (noun): things that burn and produce heat, flames, and light.
5. arrested (verb): caught people and took them away because they did something against the law.
6. extra (adjective): more than what is usual or what you already have.

Grammar Focus: Using "because" to Give a Reason
We use the word "because" to answer the question "Why?" It connects an action or a feeling to the reason it happened.
Example from the text: "Many students also protested because their schools need more teachers."

Fewer trees are being lost in the Amazon rainforest ahead of a big election.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. rainforest (noun): A thick forest with a lot of rain, usually in warm places.
2. protect (verb): To keep someone or something safe from danger.
3. cut down (phrasal verb): To make a tree or plant fall to the ground.
4. government (noun): The group of people who make laws and run a country.
5. drop (noun): A change to a lower amount or level.
6. illegal (adjective): Not allowed by the rules or the law.
7. election (noun): A time when people vote to choose a leader.

Grammar Focus: Simple Present Tense
We use the simple present tense to talk about facts and things that are true right now. In this sentence, the verb "helps" shows what the rainforest does every day.
Example from the text: "It helps to protect the Earth from climate change."
