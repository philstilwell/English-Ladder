Read the story

A US soldier was killed in Iraq. Another soldier was injured there. This happened after an attack. The attack was by Iran. The US military gave this news. This death follows other deaths. Two US soldiers died earlier. They were killed in Jordan. This happened just one day before. These events show ongoing conflict.

Words and grammar

1. killed (verb): Died because of a violent event or action.
2. injured (verb): Hurt physically in an accident or an attack.
3. attack (noun): A violent act meant to cause harm or damage.
4. military (noun): The official armed forces of a country, like the army, navy, or air force.
5. soldiers (noun): People who serve in an army and fight in wars.

Grammar Focus: Past Simple Verbs
We use past simple verbs to talk about actions that started and finished in the past. We often add '-ed' to verbs, like 'happened'. Some verbs change, like 'kill' becomes 'killed' and 'is' becomes 'was'.
Example from the text: "A US soldier was killed in Iraq."
