📅 September 05, 2026 - Title: A trial ends without a decision
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

A Massachusetts judge has declared a mistrial in Lindsay Clancy’s murder case after the jury failed to reach agreement. The case concerns the deaths of her three children. Jurors had deliberated for a week after hearing several weeks of testimony. The deadlock produced neither a conviction nor a finding of not guilty. Clancy’s lawyer attempted unsuccessfully to prevent the mistrial from being declared. The state’s highest court refused a request to require the jury to continue its deliberations. A further hearing has been scheduled for later in the month to address the next steps. The prosecutor said the legal team would consider whether to seek another trial. Clancy remains held on the existing charges following the mistrial. The legal outcome remains uncertain.

Words and grammar

1. deliberated (verb): considered and discussed a matter carefully before deciding
2. deadlock (noun): a situation in which disagreement prevents a decision
3. conviction (noun): a formal decision that someone is guilty of a crime
4. reach agreement (verb phrase): arrive at a decision that the participants accept
5. finding of not guilty (noun phrase): a formal decision that a defendant is not guilty of the charge being considered
6. refused (verb): declined to agree to a request or allow a proposed action
7. require (verb): to make an action necessary through an order, rule or condition
8. address (verb): to deal with an issue or take steps to resolve it
9. held (verb): kept in custody rather than released
10. existing (adjective): already in place at the time being discussed

Advanced Grammar: Remain + adjective
Remains uncertain means continues to be uncertain. It describes the current state, not a future verdict.
Example from the text: "The legal outcome remains uncertain."
