Read carefully when responsibility is uncertain.

Read the story

The report describes a growing series of suspected attacks on defence sites in Europe. Germany has blamed Russia for an attack involving drones at Leipzig airport. The airport is used to transport military supplies for Ukraine. Russian officials reject Germany’s accusation. Other countries have reported suspicious fires at defence facilities. Some governments have blamed Russia, while investigations elsewhere are still continuing. Some of the fires may eventually be found to have been accidental. Russia is the main suspect in the report.

Words and grammar

1. blamed (verb): said that someone was responsible
2. suspicious (adjective): making people think something may be wrong
3. suspect (noun): a person or party thought to be responsible
4. defence sites (noun phrase): places used for military protection or related activities
5. drones (plural noun): aircraft that fly without pilots on board
6. supplies (plural noun): materials or equipment needed for an activity
7. reject (verb): refuse to accept an idea, claim or proposal
8. accidental (adjective): happening by chance rather than through a deliberate action

Grammar Focus: A claim with a source
In the report limits the statement to what this source says. Suspect does not mean proven responsible.
Example from the text: "Russia is the main suspect in the report."
