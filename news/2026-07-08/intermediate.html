Trump Declares Ceasefire 'Over' Amid US-Iran Strikes Ahead of Nato Summit

Read the story

US President Donald Trump recently declared that the ceasefire between the United States and Iran is now "over". This declaration came shortly after both nations engaged in trade strikes, exchanging military actions against each other. The President made his comments while speaking ahead of an important Nato summit. During his remarks, Trump emphasized that American negotiators remain prepared to continue discussions with Iran. He added that talks can proceed "if they want," indicating an open invitation for dialogue. The situation highlights ongoing tensions in the region, despite previous efforts to reduce conflict. High-ranking officials from both sides are closely monitoring developments. International observers are concerned about the potential for further escalation after these military exchanges. The President's statement suggests a shift in diplomatic strategy regarding Iran. Global leaders at the Nato summit are likely to discuss the implications of these recent events.

Words and grammar

1. ceasefire (noun): A temporary stop in fighting agreed by all sides involved in a war or conflict.
2. trade strikes (verb phrase): To exchange attacks or military actions with an opponent, usually meaning both sides have launched attacks.
3. negotiators (noun): People who discuss something formally with another party to reach an agreement.
4. summit (noun): An official meeting or conference between leaders, especially of governments or countries, to discuss important issues.
5. officials (noun): People who hold a position of authority in an organization or government and perform duties.

Grammar Focus: Reporting Verbs
Reporting verbs are used to describe what someone said or thought. They are often followed by 'that' and then a clause (subject + verb). Using different reporting verbs (like 'declare', 'emphasize', 'add') can give more specific information about the speaker's intention or tone, rather than just using 'said'.
Example from the text: "US President Donald Trump recently declared that the ceasefire between the United States and Iran is now "over"."
