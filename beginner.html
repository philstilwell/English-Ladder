📅 October 14, 2026 - Using Technology for Job Applications
Many young people use artificial intelligence to help them apply for jobs.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Many young people use technology to help with job applications. They use it to write cover letters or CVs. This helps candidates send multiple applications at the same time. Now, companies receive large volumes of applications. Some managers do not blame them because the jobs market is difficult. However, some people use the technology to cheat on assessments. Employers still want to see the real personality of each person.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. technology (noun): computers, programs, and machines that help people do things.
2. CVs (noun): documents that show a person's education and work history.
3. candidates (noun): people who are trying to get a job.
4. jobs market (noun): the number of jobs available for people who need work.
5. cheat (verb): to do something dishonest to get an advantage.
6. personality (noun): the character and qualities that make a person who they are.

Grammar Focus: Giving reasons with "because"
We use the word "because" to answer the question "Why?" and to give a reason for something. In this sentence, "because" introduces the reason why managers do not blame young people.
Example from the text: "Some managers do not blame them because the jobs market is difficult."

📅 October 13, 2026 - Breaking Bad Actor RJ Mitte Dies at 34
Actor RJ Mitte has died at 34 after playing a famous role and helping people with disabilities.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

He created a foundation to support his community. Police confirmed his death, but they do not know the cause yet. One actor from the show shared a kind message about his friend.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. actor (noun): A person who performs in movies, plays, or television shows.
2. series (noun): A television show that tells a story over many episodes.
3. disabilities (noun): Physical or mental conditions that can make some everyday tasks harder to do.
4. foundation (noun): An organization created to give money and support to others.
5. community (noun): A group of people living in the same place or sharing a common interest.
6. cause (noun): The reason why something happens.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about finished actions in the past. Regular past tense verbs often end in "-ed" (like "played" or "worked"), while irregular verbs change their spelling.
Example from the text: "He played the son of Walter White in the drama series Breaking Bad."

Ukraine's leader says he will support a stop to energy attacks if Russia also agrees, but attacks continue.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

President Zelensky supports an 'energy ceasefire'. Ukraine's leader says he will support such a truce if Moscow does. Donald Trump said Russia and Ukraine agreed to this 'energy ceasefire'. But President Zelensky said Russia continued to hit power plants. Russia attacked Ukrainian energy places after Trump's announcement. For many months, Ukraine and Russia have attacked each other's energy buildings. These attacks caused problems with energy for both sides. Russia said its attacks were fair because these places helped Ukraine's army.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. supports (verb): to give help or approval to something or someone.
2. ceasefire (noun): an agreement to stop fighting for a period of time.
3. agreed (verb): past tense of 'agree'; to say 'yes' to a plan or idea.
4. power plants (noun phrase): large buildings where electricity is made.
5. attacked (verb): past tense of 'attack'; to hurt or try to destroy something or someone.
6. energy (noun): power from fuel or electricity that can be used to make things work.

Grammar Focus: Using 'because' to show reason
'Because' helps us explain why something happens. It connects an action to its reason. For example, 'I am happy because it is sunny' shows the reason for happiness.
Example from the text: "Russia said its attacks were fair because these places helped Ukraine's army."

Definitions in English.

Grammar Focus: Using "could" for Possibility
We use the word "could" to talk about something that is possible in the future, but not certain. It means it might happen, but it's not guaranteed. For example, 'This project could create many jobs' means it is possible it will create jobs, but it hasn't happened yet.

Read the story

Israel's Prime Minister said it is too early to know if Iran was involved in the flight attack. A pilot stabbed another pilot on a plane from Dubai to Tel Aviv. The plane lost 17,000 feet quickly, but passengers and crew stopped the attacker and landed safely. The pilot who was stabbed is Captain Smit Machchhar, and he is now stable in hospital. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu called Captain Machchhar a "true hero." The attacker was arrested in Saudi Arabia and will go to the UAE. Flydubai said two crew members who were traveling landed the plane safely. The attacker tried to crash the plane.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. involved (adjective): connected to something or taking part in an event.
2. stabbed (verb): hurt someone with a sharp object like a knife.
3. attacker (noun): a person who tries to hurt another person.
4. stable (adjective): not changing, in a good and steady condition, especially for health.
5. hero (noun): a very brave person who helps others in danger.
6. crew (noun): the group of people who work on a plane or ship.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that started and finished in the past. It tells us something happened at a specific time before now.
