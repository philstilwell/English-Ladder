Read the story

The United States and Iran continue to trade attacks amidst heightened tensions in the Middle East. This dangerous escalation of hostilities occurred during a sensitive period, linked to the burial of Khamenei. Centcom, the US Central Command, confirmed its military forces launched strikes in the region. Specifically, Centcom stated that approximately 90 Iranian targets were struck in these recent operations. These actions represent the latest round of military engagement between the two nations. The Iranian health ministry has also provided details regarding the human impact of these events. They reported that 14 people were killed since Tuesday due to the ongoing conflict. Such incidents highlight the deep political disagreements and military confrontations between the countries. The international community is closely monitoring these developments, urging for restraint. Efforts towards de-escalation are seen as crucial to prevent a broader regional conflict.

Words and grammar

1. escalation (noun): A rapid increase in the intensity, seriousness, or scope of a conflict or situation.
2. struck (verb): To hit forcefully, often used in a military context when bombs or missiles hit a target.
3. conflict (noun): A serious disagreement or argument, often involving prolonged fighting or hostility between groups or nations.
4. strikes (noun): Military attacks, often from the air or with missiles, aimed at a target.
5. monitoring (verb): watching a situation carefully over time to notice changes
6. operations (plural noun): planned activities carried out for a purpose, such as rescue work or military action
7. regarding (preposition): about or in connection with a particular subject
8. launched (verb (past tense)): started something new, like an attack.

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself and the object that receives the action, rather than the subject performing the action. It is often employed in news reports when the agent (the one doing the action) is unknown, less important, or obvious from the context.
Example from the text: "They reported that 14 people were killed since Tuesday due to the ongoing conflict."
