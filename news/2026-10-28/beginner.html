Companies Try to Make Delivery Drones Quieter

Beginner · A1–A2

News archive →
📅 October 28, 2026 - Companies Try to Make Delivery Drones Quieter

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Some companies use drones, or flying machines, to bring orders to people's homes. These deliveries are becoming part of everyday life for many people in the US. Ashley Schmidt lives near Dallas and spends less time in her garden now. She does not like the noise from delivery drones above her home. She can hear them even inside her home. Companies are looking for ways to make drones quieter. Greenjets puts the parts that turn inside a cover to make less noise. Wing changes where its drones fly to avoid flying over the same homes again and again.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. orders (plural noun): Things that people buy and ask a shop to send.
2. deliveries (plural noun): Times when someone brings things to a person or place.
3. garden (noun): An area outside a home where people grow flowers or other plants.
4. noise (noun): A sound, often one that people do not like.
5. inside (preposition): In a building or place, not outside it.
6. quieter (comparative adjective): Making less noise.

Grammar Focus: Can + verb
Use “can” to say what someone is able to do. Put the basic verb after “can”, without “to” or an added “s”. The reading says, “She can hear them even inside her home.”
Example from the text: "She can hear them even inside her home."
