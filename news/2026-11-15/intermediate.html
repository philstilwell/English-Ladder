📅 November 15, 2026 - SpaceX Catches Starship’s Upper Section for the First Time

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

SpaceX has recovered the upper section of its Starship rocket for the first time after a journey lasting twenty-two and a half hours. Near the launch tower, it was secured by mechanical arms nicknamed chopsticks. The company also planned to catch the lower section, but cancelled this attempt a few minutes after launch. The lower section, called the Super Heavy booster, was due to return six and a half minutes after launch. However, the booster automatically changed direction towards the ocean, hovered above the water and then exploded. The upper section deployed 26 satellites that are part of Starlink, a system providing high-speed internet. It completed 14 orbits, or full journeys around Earth, before returning to the launch tower. SpaceX wants both sections to be reusable, meaning they can be used for future flights. Recovering both sections reliably would allow SpaceX to launch Starship repeatedly and greatly reduce costs. The company had already caught the booster on three earlier test flights. Before the flight, SpaceX said it would try to catch the upper section only after carefully assessing its condition in space.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. secured (verb, past participle): Held something firmly in place.
2. attempt (noun): An act of trying to do something, especially something difficult.
3. deployed (verb, past simple): Released equipment into the place where it would be used.
4. orbits (plural noun): Complete journeys around a planet or another object in space.
5. reusable (adjective): Able to be used more than once.
6. reliably (adverb): In a way that can be trusted to work successfully each time.
7. reduce costs (verb phrase): Make the amount of money needed for something smaller.
8. assessing (verb, present participle): Carefully checking something to make a judgment about it.

Grammar Focus: Past simple passive
Use the passive to focus on what happened to something. Form the past simple passive with “was” or “were” plus a past participle. Add “by” to identify the person or thing that performed the action.
Example from the text: "Near the launch tower, it was secured by mechanical arms nicknamed chopsticks."
