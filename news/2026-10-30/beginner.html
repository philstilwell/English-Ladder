Israel's Leaders Argue About Election Results

Beginner · A1–A2

News archive →
📅 October 30, 2026 - Israel's Leaders Argue About Election Results

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Netanyahu's group of parties lost its majority in Israel's election. A majority means more than half the seats in parliament, the group that makes a country's laws. His Likud party accused Arab-led parties of stealing the election. The Arab-led parties rejected this claim and said it was not true. Israel's security service is checking whether these parties received money illegally from Qatar and Turkey. Qatar said the claims about its money were not true. The Arab-led parties won 19 of the 120 seats in parliament. Gadi Eisenkot's group won 48 seats, while Netanyahu's group won 53. Eisenkot said it was too early to know if anything had happened. He accused Netanyahu of trying to make people think the election results were not fair. Eisenkot could form a government with support from the Arab-led parties.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. election (noun): A time when people vote to choose leaders.
2. lost (verb, simple past): Stopped having something.
3. seats (plural noun): Places for people chosen to be members of parliament.
4. rejected (verb, simple past): Said that something was not true.
5. results (plural noun): The numbers showing how many votes or seats each party won.
6. support (noun): Help for someone or their plans.

Grammar Focus: Simple past
Use the simple past to talk about a finished event. “Won” is the simple past of “win”. Use “won” with different subjects, such as “the parties” and “his group”.
Example from the text: "The Arab-led parties won 19 of the 120 seats in parliament."
