Anthropic Stops AI Being Used for Bad Things

Beginner · A1–A2

News archive →
📅 September 11, 2026 - Anthropic Stops AI Being Used for Bad Things

Read the story

Anthropic is an AI company. It said it stopped people from using its AI. They tried to make biological weapons. This information is in a report from the company. Other groups used AI for internet scams and bad actions. They also used AI to make software for other weapons. Anthropic will work to stop these bad activities in the future.

Words and grammar

1. company (noun): A business that makes or sells products or services.
2. stopped (verb): Made something end; prevented it from continuing.
3. using (verb): Doing something with a tool, a machine, or a method.
4. weapons (noun): Objects used to fight, hurt, or kill others.
5. report (noun): A written paper or document that gives facts or information about something.
6. scams (noun): Clever but dishonest plans to trick people, often to get their money or information.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished at a specific time in the past. For many verbs, we add '-ed' to the base form, like 'stop' becomes 'stopped'. For irregular verbs, the form changes, like 'say' becomes 'said'.
Example from the text: "It said it stopped people from using its AI."
