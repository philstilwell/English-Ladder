📅 August 10, 2026 [0 days, 0 hours old] - Deadly Strike in Russia's Tatarstan Region
Ukrainian drones attacked a region in Russia, causing many deaths.

Beginner ESL: Military conflict between Ukraine and Russia

I. The News Brief

Ukraine made a strike on Russia. This strike was in the Tatarstan region. Ukrainian drones caused the attack there. At least 13 people were killed. Russian officials gave this information. They said it was a very strong attack. It was one of the deadliest attacks on Russia. This happened during Moscow's war on Ukraine. The war started some time ago. Many people are sad about these events.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. strike (noun): An attack, often by military force.
2. region (noun): A large area of a country or the world.
3. killed (verb): Died because of an action or violence.
4. officials (noun): People who have an important job in a government or organization.
5. war (noun): A fight between two or more countries, using weapons.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the Simple Past Tense to talk about actions that happened in the past and are completely finished. For regular verbs, we add '-ed'. For irregular verbs, the form changes.
Example from the text: "At least 13 people were killed."

📅 August 09, 2026 [1 day, 0 hours old] - News: Israel and West Bank Sites
Israel plans to take land in the West Bank, which makes some people unhappy.

Beginner ESL: Land and Archaeology in the West Bank

I. The News Brief

There is news about Israel and the West Bank. Israel is accused of weaponising archaeology. This means using old sites in a bad way. These sites are ancient and important. Israel is planning large-scale land expropriations. This means taking a lot of land. The land is in historic Palestinian areas. The West Bank is a special place. Many people are worried about these plans. Some say Israel uses archaeology to control land.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. accused of (verb phrase): told that you did something wrong or bad.
2. weaponising (verb): using something as a weapon to hurt or control others, not for its original purpose.
3. ancient (adjective): very, very old; from a long time ago.
4. planning (verb): thinking about what you will do in the future and making arrangements for it.
5. land expropriations (noun phrase): when a government takes private land for public use or other reasons, sometimes without agreement.

Grammar Focus: The Verb 'To Be' and Present Continuous
We use 'is' for one thing or person, and 'are' for many things or people. We often use 'is' or 'are' with a verb ending in '-ing' to talk about actions happening now or plans for the future.
Example from the text: "Israel is planning large-scale land expropriations."
