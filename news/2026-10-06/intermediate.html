📅 October 06, 2026 - US Pulls Bombers from UK Base Due to Unspecified Threats

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

President Donald Trump stated that the US moved its aircraft out of RAF Fairford because of "some threats" to the base. The Pentagon confirmed on Sunday that the B-1s were removed from the base and sent back to their home stations in America. US media reports suggested that the decision was made due to intelligence indicating that Iran was plotting a drone attack. One source close to the White House mentioned Ukraine's Operation Spiderweb as an example of what an enemy force could potentially do, even from a distance. This operation involved Ukraine smuggling drones into Russia on lorries driven by people unaware of their cargo. A No 10 spokesperson had previously insisted that RAF Fairford was safe. Trump later indicated that the US "actually knows the people that made the threat" and suggested they would be "linked to Iran". Marco Rubio noted it was "not unusual" for military units and assets to rotate and be moved globally. Counter-terror police are investigating an incident at RAF Fairford. The redeployment from RAF Fairford will reduce America's capacity to "quickly surge sorties against Iran".

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. stated (verb): expressed something clearly and formally in words.
2. intelligence (noun): secret information, especially about the plans and activities of a competitor or enemy.
3. plotting (verb): planning secretly to do something, often something harmful or illegal.
4. smuggling (verb): moving goods or people illegally and secretly into or out of a country.
5. insisted (verb): demanded forcefully that something should happen or be done, or stated something very firmly.
6. indicated (verb): showed or pointed out something; suggested something without stating it directly.
7. rotate (verb): to regularly change places or positions, especially as part of a planned cycle.
8. redeployment (noun): the act of moving military forces, equipment, or workers to a different location or for a different purpose.
9. capacity (noun): the ability or power to do or produce something.

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself and the object that receives the action, rather than on the subject that performs the action. It is formed using a form of the verb "to be" followed by the past participle of the main verb. For example, in "the decision was made," the focus is on the decision and the fact that it was made, not necessarily on who made it.
Example from the text: "US media reports suggested that the decision was made due to intelligence indicating that Iran was plotting a drone attack."
