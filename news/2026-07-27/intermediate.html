Read the story

The global oil price has significantly dived recently, indicating a major shift in market dynamics. This sudden drop follows a crucial development in the ongoing tensions between the United States and Iran. The US government officially stated that its attacks on Iran have been halted. This strategic decision was primarily made to give talks some space for diplomatic discussions. Leaders hope this temporary pause will allow for a peaceful resolution to the conflict between the two nations. Consequently, the global oil market reacted strongly to this unexpected news of de-escalation. Many economic analysts believe that a period of reduced military action could help stabilize the volatile region. The halt in attacks offers a critical opportunity for both nations to engage in constructive dialogue. International observers are expressing cautious optimism about the prospects for achieving lasting peace. Ultimately, the aim is to find a long-term solution that benefits all parties involved and prevents further unrest in the area.

Words and grammar

1. dived (verb): dropped suddenly and quickly, like a sharp fall in value or level, often used for markets or prices.
2. halted (verb): stopped completely or brought to a stop, especially in a formal or official manner, usually an activity or process.
3. give talks some space (phrase): to create an opportunity or a suitable environment for discussions and negotiations to happen without interruption or immediate pressure.
4. pause (noun): a temporary stop or break in an activity, action, or speech, allowing for a moment of rest or reflection before continuing.
5. resolution to the conflict (phrase): a successful way of solving a serious disagreement or problem between opposing groups or countries, leading to an end to hostilities.

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself or on the object of the action, rather than on the performer of the action. It's often used when the agent is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from the context. It is formed with a form of 'to be' + the past participle of the main verb.
Example from the text: "The US government officially stated that its attacks on Iran have been halted."
