Read the story

A big fire started in a bar in Bangkok. The fire happened in the Chatuchak district. Many people were at the bar. At least 27 people were killed. 25 people were critically injured. Firefighters came to the scene quickly. They saw patrons trying to escape the fire. People were running through flames. It was a very dangerous situation. The firefighters helped many people.

Words and grammar

1. killed (verb (past participle)): died because of something bad that happened.
2. injured (verb (past participle)): hurt physically in an accident or attack.
3. firefighters (noun): people who stop fires and help others.
4. scene (noun): the place where something happened.
5. patrons (noun): customers who visit a bar, restaurant, or shop.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. We often add '-ed' to regular verbs. Irregular verbs change their form.
Example from the text: "Firefighters came to the scene quickly."
