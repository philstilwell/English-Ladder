📅 November 26, 2026 - UK and EU Plan to Publish New Agreements
New agreements could make selling food between the UK and EU easier.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The UK government and the EU plan to publish two agreements on Thursday. They agreed on these plans last week. The new deal will reduce checks on food moving between the UK and the EU. UK businesses selling food to the EU will also have less paperwork, such as forms to complete. The government hopes these changes will help customers pay less for food. But customers may not see lower prices in shops. The BBC says the agreements include a special rule for the first ten years. Under this rule, the side ending the deal would pay to rebuild border infrastructure. This means buildings and other things needed for border checks.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. publish (verb): To make information available for people to read.
2. agreements (plural noun): Plans or rules that two sides accept.
3. checks (plural noun): Careful looks at things to see if they follow the rules.
4. paperwork (uncountable noun): Forms and other papers that people need to complete for work.
5. customers (plural noun): People who buy things from a shop or business.
6. lower (comparative adjective): Less than before.

Grammar Focus: “Will” for the future
Use “will” and a verb to talk about the future. The verb stays in its basic form, as in “will reduce”. Use the same form with “I”, “they” and other subjects. To make a negative, use “will not” and a verb.
Example from the text: "The new deal will reduce checks on food moving between the UK and the EU."

A US government lawyer does not answer some questions about Nvidia.

Huang said sorry to Nvidia workers on Tuesday, according to reports. He said his mistakes were very bad.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. questions (plural noun): Things people ask when they want to know something.
2. checking (verb, -ing form): Looking carefully to find out whether something is wrong.
3. leader (noun): The person who guides a group or company.
4. job (noun): Work that someone does to earn money.
5. return (noun): The act of coming back.
6. mistakes (plural noun): Things someone does wrong.

Grammar Focus: Simple past with "did not"
Use "did not" before a verb to say that something did not happen in the past. After "did not", use the verb's basic form, such as "say", not "said". The short form of "did not" is "didn't".
Example from the text: "He did not say whether the government was checking the company."

📅 November 24, 2026 - Don Toliver Moves Two London Shows to Later Dates
Two London concerts have new dates after people had to leave a Manchester concert.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

US rapper Don Toliver has moved two London shows to later dates. An unknown substance, or type of material, was released into the crowd at his Manchester concert. The people at the concert had to leave on Sunday. The equipment is still in Manchester and cannot reach London in time for this week's shows. The Wembley show will now be on 14 December. The O2 show will now be on 15 December. Police said 12,000 people had to leave the arena, a large building for events. One person went to hospital. Police said 89 people had signs of illness after the concert. Fans at the Manchester show will get all their ticket money back.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. later (adjective): After the day or time first planned.
2. crowd (noun): A large group of people in one place.
3. had to (verb phrase): Needed to do something.
4. equipment (uncountable noun): The things people need to do a job.
5. in time (adverb phrase): Early enough to do something.
6. ticket (noun): A paper or phone message that shows you can enter an event.

Grammar Focus: Past need with “had to”
Use “had to” when someone needed to do something in the past. Put the basic verb after “had to”, as in “had to leave”. This verb stays the same for one person or many people. Here, people needed to leave the concert.
Example from the text: "The people at the concert had to leave on Sunday."
