Christiane Amanpour Exits CNN After Four Decades

📅 October 20, 2026 - Christiane Amanpour Exits CNN After Four Decades

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

CNN's chief international anchor, Christiane Amanpour, has announced her impending departure from the organisation next year after more than four decades of service. Following months of contemplation and a few sleepless nights, Amanpour confirmed her intention to separate from the news channel. Amanpour commenced her tenure in 1983 on the international news desk, subsequently becoming one of the most prominent global reporters. Throughout her career, she received numerous challenging assignments, extensively covering the horrors of war across regions such as Rwanda, Afghanistan, Ukraine, and Iran. Furthermore, she has presented her own nightly interview programme on CNN International since 2009. The 68-year-old media veteran expressed that she eagerly anticipates engaging in new endeavours down the line. She also emphasised the importance of maintaining an independent approach, reminding her colleagues to always remain truthful rather than strictly neutral. CNN chairman Mark Thompson lauded her as one of the most trusted and revered media figures anywhere. Thompson acknowledged his sorrow at her exit but noted that he has come to accept this transition as the right step for her both personally and professionally. Reflecting on her legacy, Amanpour praised the camaraderie of her teams as they collectively battled to uncover facts and evidence. She asserted that their unified mission allowed them to transmit the truth to the world, operating entirely without fear nor favour.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. impending (adjective): Approaching or about to happen in the near future.
2. departure (noun): The act of leaving or stepping away from an organisation or position.
3. contemplation (noun): The act of thinking deeply or meditating on a choice.
4. commenced (verb): Formally began or initiated a professional role or activity.
5. tenure (noun): The period of time during which a person holds a particular job or role.
6. prominent (adjective): Important, famous, or highly visible in a particular field.
7. endeavours (noun): Serious and continuous attempts or efforts to achieve new goals.
8. lauded (verb): Expressed high praise and admiration for someone's achievements.
9. revered (adjective): Respected and admired deeply by others in the profession.
10. camaraderie (noun): A feeling of mutual trust, friendship, and solidarity among team members.
11. without fear nor favour (phrase): Acting in a completely impartial way, refusing to be influenced by threats or special treatment.

Advanced Grammar: Present participial phrases for simultaneous action
A present participle clause can introduce a sentence to show an action occurring at the same time as, or providing the context for, the main verb.
Example from the text: "Reflecting on her legacy, Amanpour praised the camaraderie of her teams as they collectively battled to uncover facts and evidence."
