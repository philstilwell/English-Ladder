Bangkok Faces Severe Flooding After Heavy Rains

📅 September 27, 2026 - Bangkok Faces Severe Flooding After Heavy Rains

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

An emergency situation has been announced across Bangkok after heavy rain fell since Thursday. On Sunday, authorities stated that the heavy rains had stopped, but canal water levels continued to be high. Residents were advised to stay at home, and those near canals were told to move their belongings to higher floors. Thunderstorms are common in Thailand, but recent extreme weather has overwhelmed Bangkok's drainage system. This has caused significant traffic chaos on roads already known for being very busy. A local resident, Somkid Pheuk-ngam, described the water as "at my waist level now." So far, no one has been reported injured or killed in Bangkok from this latest flooding. The Thai Meteorological Service expects more heavy rain throughout the week. The city's governor, Chadchart Sittipunt, reported that the eastern part of Bangkok was most affected. He also mentioned that they are trying to pump water out, but the canals are completely full.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. emergency situation (noun): a serious or dangerous event that needs immediate action and attention.
2. authorities (noun): the people or organizations that have the power to make decisions and make sure laws are obeyed.
3. canal (noun): a long, narrow passage of water, either natural or artificial, used for boats or irrigation.
4. belongings (noun): the things that a person owns, especially small, movable items.
5. extreme weather (noun): very unusual or severe weather conditions, such as exceptionally heavy rain, strong winds, or heat.
6. drainage system (noun): a network of pipes, channels, or ditches that removes unwanted water from an area.
7. traffic chaos (noun): a situation on roads where there is complete disorder and confusion, making it very difficult for vehicles to move.
8. Meteorological Service (noun): an official organization that studies the weather and makes predictions about it.
9. governor (noun): the official in charge of a province, state, or city.

Grammar Focus: Reporting Verbs
Reporting verbs are used to tell people what someone else said, thought, or felt. They help us share information without using direct quotes every time. Common reporting verbs include "said," "stated," "reported," "mentioned," and "described." They are often followed by "that" (which can sometimes be omitted).
Example from the text: "On Sunday, authorities stated that the heavy rains had stopped, but canal water levels continued to be high."
