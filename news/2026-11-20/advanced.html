Anthropic's Market Surge Tests Confidence in AI

Advanced · C1+

📅 November 20, 2026 - Anthropic's Market Surge Tests Confidence in AI

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Anthropic began trading on the Nasdaq stock exchange on Thursday against a backdrop of enormous spending on artificial intelligence (AI). The company reached a market value of roughly $1.76 trillion, placing it among the world's most valuable publicly traded businesses. Anthropic's stock market debut created a test for the wider AI industry. Its shares initially climbed to $111.53 before retreating to close at $97.17. The shares moved above their $83 allocation price, the price set for their initial distribution to investors, signalling considerable enthusiasm for AI. The broader Nasdaq Composite Index, however, closed nearly 2.3% lower. Nevertheless, Anthropic, the company behind Claude, faces intense scrutiny over whether AI will justify the resources devoted to it. A handful of major technology companies collectively spent more than $1 trillion on the AI buildout in the previous year alone. The pace of this spending has fuelled fears that prices may be inflated by excessive optimism. Economist Richard Whittle cautioned that bubbles burst when confidence in a positive future weakens, rather than necessarily on the day of a major deal. Ben Barringer, who leads technology research at Quilter Cheviot, expects substantial fluctuations in Anthropic's share price. Barringer warned that the initial exuberance, or unusually strong enthusiasm, would wear off. Investor strategist Neil Wilson said that Anthropic lacks an obvious peer against which its worth can be assessed. New laws governing AI tools are also a possibility. Some US politicians have indicated that they want AI tools to be safe and subject to guardrails, meaning limits intended to prevent harmful use. Donald Trump reiterated his opposition to robust regulation of AI, indicating resistance to substantial rules governing the technology. A bipartisan group of US lawmakers nevertheless formed a caucus, a group organised around a shared policy interest, to address AI regulation and safety. Anthropic executives, including co-founder Dario Amodei, have been outspoken in expressing fears that future AI could outsmart humans and potentially threaten humanity's survival. Despite having described its rapidly developing technology as potentially dangerous, Anthropic has made an exceptionally swift transition from start-up to public listing.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. reiterated (verb (past tense)): Stated a view or position again, often for emphasis; used especially in formal reporting.
2. intense scrutiny (noun phrase): Close, sustained examination that subjects claims or decisions to demanding questions.
3. exuberance (uncountable noun): Strong, energetic enthusiasm that, in financial reporting, can suggest optimism exceeding what the evidence warrants.
4. wear off (phrasal verb): To gradually lose strength or intensity, especially when an initial feeling or effect fades.
5. peer (countable noun): A sufficiently similar company that can provide a useful basis for assessing another company's performance or worth.
6. fluctuations (plural noun): Changes in a price, amount or level as it moves up and down rather than steadily in one direction.
7. guardrails (plural noun): Rules or limits intended to prevent harmful behaviour while allowing an activity to continue.
8. robust regulation (noun phrase): Strong, substantial rules intended to exercise meaningful control over an activity or industry.
9. bipartisan (adjective): Involving members of two political parties, indicating cooperation across a party divide.
10. outspoken (adjective): Expressing views publicly and directly, including views that may attract disagreement.

Advanced Grammar: Concession with despite and a perfect participle clause
Use “despite” with an “-ing” clause to acknowledge a point that makes the main claim seem unexpected. The perfect form “having described” places that action before the main event; its understood subject is Anthropic, also the subject of the main clause.
Example from the text: "Despite having described its rapidly developing technology as potentially dangerous, Anthropic has made an exceptionally swift transition from start-up to public listing."
