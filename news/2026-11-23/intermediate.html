📅 November 23, 2026 - COP31 Climate Deal Fails Over Fuels and Funding

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Countries at COP31 in Turkey failed to reach a major new climate agreement because of deep divisions over fossil fuels and money. The negotiations continued from Friday into Sunday as countries searched for common ground. The EU and Pacific island nations found the final proposal unacceptable because it did not directly mention fossil fuels. Poorer countries also objected because richer nations made no clear financial commitments to help them adapt to rising temperatures. An earlier draft mentioned fossil fuels six times, but oil-producing nations found this unacceptable. At COP30 in Brazil last year, nations agreed to triple the financial support. However, this year's proposal included neither exact financial figures nor a starting amount for measuring that increase. This lack of detail raised concerns that richer nations would have too much freedom in how they met their promise. Although the main agreement failed, some progress was made on support for workers and communities moving towards clean energy. The climate process relies on consensus, which means countries must reach an agreement together. Following the failure, some people questioned whether this process could succeed in a politically divided world. The UN's climate chief, Simon Stiell, said he would seek advice on possible changes to the process.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. negotiations (plural noun): Formal discussions in which people or groups try to reach an agreement.
2. common ground (noun phrase): Ideas or interests that people share despite their differences.
3. unacceptable (adjective): Not good enough for someone to agree to or allow.
4. objected (verb, past simple): Expressed disagreement with something or opposition to it.
5. financial commitments (plural noun phrase): Firm promises to provide money for a particular purpose.
6. adapt (verb): Change how people live or work to manage new conditions.
7. triple (verb): Increase something to three times its earlier amount.
8. consensus (noun): Agreement reached by the members of a group.

Grammar Focus: Past simple passive
Form the past simple passive with “was” or “were” and a past participle. Use it for a completed past action when the action matters more than who performed it. In the main clause, “was made” focuses on progress without naming who made it.
Example from the text: "Although the main agreement failed, some progress was made on support for workers and communities moving towards clean energy."
