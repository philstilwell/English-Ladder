Andrew Bailey: AI Needs Testing, Not Just Rules

Beginner · A1–A2

News archive →
📅 September 30, 2026 - Andrew Bailey: AI Needs Testing, Not Just Rules

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The Bank of England's Governor, Andrew Bailey, spoke about Artificial Intelligence (AI). He said that making rules for AI is not the right first step. Instead, Bailey wants careful testing for AI to find problems. He also said that AI has important risks. But Bailey believes AI should still develop because it has many good benefits. If testing shows problems, it means that testing is necessary.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. Governor (noun): A person who leads an important organization or a region.
2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) (noun phrase): Machines or computer programs that can think and learn like humans.
3. rules (noun): Official instructions that tell people what they can or cannot do.
4. testing (noun): The act of trying something to see if it works well or is safe.
5. risks (noun): Possible dangers or problems that can happen.
6. benefits (noun): Good things that come from something, or advantages.
7. necessary (adjective): Something that must be done or had; very important.

Grammar Focus: Using 'because' to show reason
We use the word 'because' to give a reason for something. It tells us why something is happening or why someone believes something.
Example from the text: "But Bailey believes AI should still develop because it has many good benefits."
