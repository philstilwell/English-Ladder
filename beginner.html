📅 November 09, 2026 - Dormouse Numbers May Be Rising in England and Wales
Dormouse numbers may be going up after years of falling.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Dormouse numbers in England and Wales may be going up after years of falling. A dormouse is a small animal like a mouse. The hazel dormouse is the only kind of dormouse in the UK. The new figures suggest there were almost 10 percent more dormice in 2025 than in 2020. But researchers say the real change could be quite different from what these figures show. There are still almost 60 percent fewer dormice than in 2000. Dormice sleep through the winter in nests on the ground. Scientists think dormice may spend less time in deep sleep in warmer summers. This may leave them with more energy to feed and have young. People who work to save dormice say we still need to protect the woods where they live.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. going up (verb phrase): Becoming larger in number.
2. figures (plural noun): Numbers that show what people found.
3. fewer (determiner): A smaller number of people, animals or things.
4. nests (plural noun): Places animals make to rest or have young.
5. deep sleep (noun phrase): Sleep that is hard to wake up from.
6. energy (noun): The strength to move and do things.
7. protect (verb): To keep someone or something safe.

Grammar Focus: Using “may”
Use “may” when something is possible and you are unsure. Put “may” after the person or thing you are talking about. Then use the verb in its basic form, as in “may spend” or “may help”.
Example from the text: "Scientists think dormice may spend less time in deep sleep in warmer summers."

📅 November 08, 2026 - The Leonid Meteor Shower Starts This Weekend
You may see bright, fast meteors in November.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The Leonid meteor shower starts this weekend. Its meteors look like lines of light that move quickly across the sky. The shower lasts until 30 November. It will be strongest on the night of 17 November. You may see as many as 15 meteors in an hour. Experts say to find a safe, dark place away from street lights. You can see the meteors with just your eyes. Give your eyes about 20 to 30 minutes to get used to the dark. An open space gives you a wide view of the sky. You need clear skies to see the meteors.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. meteor shower (noun phrase): a time when many lines of light can appear in the night sky
2. lasts (verb): continues for some time
3. away from (preposition): not near someone or something
4. street lights (plural noun): lights along a road that help people see at night
5. open space (noun phrase): a place outside with lots of room and few things in the way
6. clear skies (plural noun phrase): a sky with few or no clouds

Grammar Focus: Using “can” to say what is possible
Use “can” to say that it is possible to do something. Put “can” after the person or thing you are talking about. After “can”, use the basic form of the verb, such as “see” in “can see” or “find” in “can find”.
Example from the text: "You can see the meteors with just your eyes."

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. spoke (verb, past simple): Talked about something.
2. found guilty (verb phrase): A court decided that someone broke the law.
3. sadness (noun): The feeling you have when you are unhappy.
4. relief (noun): The feeling you have when you are less worried after something difficult.
5. shared (verb, past simple): Told other people about something.
