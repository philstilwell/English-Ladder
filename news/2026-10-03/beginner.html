Social Media and Concert Surprises

Beginner · A1–A2

News archive →
📅 October 03, 2026 - Social Media and Concert Surprises

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Social media can sometimes spoil big concert surprises for fans. Many people see special moments from concerts online before they go. They might already know the songs and how the stage looks. Some fans try to avoid these spoilers to keep the excitement. Aurélia Collins tries not to see videos about her favorite artists. But other fans like to know everything before they attend a show. Some artists change their shows a little each night for new surprises.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. spoil (verb): to ruin something good, often by telling a secret or making it less fun
2. concert (noun): a live music show, usually with one or more singers or bands
3. online (adverb): on the internet, using a computer or phone to see information
4. moments (noun): special, short times or parts of an event
5. avoid (verb): to keep away from something or someone; to prevent something from happening
6. surprises (noun): things that happen or are found unexpectedly, often making you feel amazed

Grammar Focus: Using 'can' for possibility
We use 'can' to talk about things that are possible or things that are able to happen. It shows that something might happen or is allowed to happen.
Example from the text: "Social media can sometimes spoil big concert surprises for fans."
