📅 November 24, 2026 - Don Toliver Moves Two London Shows to Later Dates

Read the story

US rapper Don Toliver has moved two London shows to later dates. An unknown substance, or type of material, was released into the crowd at his Manchester concert. The people at the concert had to leave on Sunday. The equipment is still in Manchester and cannot reach London in time for this week's shows. The Wembley show will now be on 14 December. The O2 show will now be on 15 December. Police said 12,000 people had to leave the arena, a large building for events. One person went to hospital. Police said 89 people had signs of illness after the concert. Fans at the Manchester show will get all their ticket money back.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. later (adjective): After the day or time first planned.
2. crowd (noun): A large group of people in one place.
3. had to (verb phrase): Needed to do something.
4. equipment (uncountable noun): The things people need to do a job.
5. in time (adverb phrase): Early enough to do something.
6. ticket (noun): A paper or phone message that shows you can enter an event.

Grammar Focus: Past need with “had to”
Use “had to” when someone needed to do something in the past. Put the basic verb after “had to”, as in “had to leave”. This verb stays the same for one person or many people. Here, people needed to leave the concert.
Example from the text: "The people at the concert had to leave on Sunday."
