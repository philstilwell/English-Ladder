📅 October 26, 2026 - New Technology Changes Life on Farms
Farmers use cameras, machines and apps to help with their work.
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

New technology is changing how some farmers work. George McMaster started farming by milking eight cows. He now has 300 cows and watches them through a camera. The camera makes a short video of each cow every day. It can find changes before farmers can see them. One farmer says healthier cows give them more milk. Some cows use a milking machine when they want to. A faster internet connection helped one farmer open a farm shop. A farmer uses an app to help run his farm. In this app, each cow has a page with all her records in one place. The app's developer says farmers should make the decisions. He says the technology helps farmers see the right information at the right time.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. technology (noun): Machines and computer programs that help people do things.
2. machine (noun): Something that uses power to do a job.
3. connection (noun): A way for a phone or computer to use the internet.
4. shop (noun): A place where people buy things.
5. records (plural noun): Written or saved information about something.
6. video (noun): Moving pictures that you can watch on a screen.

Grammar Focus: Can for ability
Use “can” with a verb to say what someone or something is able to do. After “can”, use the verb without “to” or “-s”.
Example from the text: "It can find changes before farmers can see them."

📅 October 25, 2026 - Almost 1,000 Arrested After Mass Protests in France
Police arrested nearly 1,000 people after large protests about the high cost of living happened across France.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Hundreds of thousands of people protested across France on Saturday. They marched because the cost of living is very high. The protests were mostly peaceful during the day. Clashes between police and protesters happened late on Saturday. Police officers used tear gas to stop the crowds. Officials said that police arrested almost 1,000 people. People also protested in other European countries for similar reasons.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. protested (verb): stood together in public to show they disagree with something
2. cost of living (noun): the money you need to pay for everyday things like food and housing
3. peaceful (adjective): quiet, calm, and without violence or fighting
4. clashes (noun): fights between two groups of people
5. tear gas (noun): a special gas used by police that hurts people's eyes
6. arrested (verb): taken by the police because a person might have broken the law

Grammar Focus: Using "because" to give a reason
We use the word "because" to answer the question "why?" or to explain the reason for an action. In this story, "because" explains the reason why the people marched in the streets.
Example from the text: "They marched because the cost of living is very high."
