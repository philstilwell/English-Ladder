Original fictional case: Client Hana and carpenter Ellis review a hall drawing labeled door opening: 900 millimeters. Hana assumes the figure describes clear passage width. The note does not identify a rough or finished opening, and no door has been ordered. Ellis must clarify the ambiguity without inventing a frame allowance or asserting a usable width. Designer Priya can explain which reference the dimension uses. The conversation concerns interpretation and referral, not a construction method or a compliance decision.
Language workshop: What does 900 millimeters describe?
Communication goal: Identify an unclear measurement reference, correct an assumption, and obtain designer clarification before treating the dimension as usable passage.
Language guidance: The only established figure is 900 millimeters in an unclear opening note. Rough opening, finished opening, door size, and clear passage are not interchangeable labels. No conversion or allowance is supplied, and no door has been ordered.
Useful expressions (preserve the supplied facts): The hall drawing labels the door opening as 900 millimeters. / Are you reading that as the clear passage width? / The note does not say rough or finished opening. / The opening reference and the usable passage are not necessarily the same. / What surfaces does this dimension run between? / I am not changing the figure of 900 millimeters. / We do not have a confirmed frame allowance here. / No door has been ordered. / Priya can clarify the measurement reference. / I cannot confirm 900 millimeters of clear passage from this note. / We should not treat this as the order size yet. / Could the clarification identify the opening type? / I will explain that your question concerns usable passage. / This conversation does not establish compliance. / Does 900 refer to the rough opening, finished opening, or another reference? / I will request clarification before presenting the dimension as a confirmed usable width.
Vocabulary: opening dimension: Measurement describing an opening using a stated reference. / rough opening: Structural opening before the door assembly and relevant finishes are installed. / finished opening: Opening measured at the specified completed surfaces. / clear passage: Unobstructed usable space through an opening under the stated conditions. / door leaf: Moving panel of a door. / door frame: Surrounding assembly supporting and locating the door. / jamb: Frame member; side jambs and the head jamb identify different positions. / head: Upper horizontal member or part of an opening. / threshold: Lower part at the base of a doorway. / casing: Trim around a door or window opening. / architrave: Term commonly used for decorative trim around an opening. / millimeter: Metric unit equal to one thousandth of a meter. / dimension line: Drawing line indicating the extent of a measurement. / reference face: Surface from which a dimension is measured. / datum: Stated reference point, line, or level for measurements. / nominal size: Designated size that may differ from an actual measured size. / actual dimension: Measured or specifically defined physical size. / frame allowance: Space allowed for the specified frame arrangement. / clearance: Space between components or around an operating part. / door schedule: Drawing or document listing specified doors and their attributes. / drawing note: Written instruction or information on a drawing. / designer clarification: Explanation from the responsible design professional. / order size: Dimensions used to specify a product for purchase. / dimension reference: Point, surface, or condition a measurement describes.
