Asking about prices

Ask what something costs and clarify the total.

Level ranges are study guidance. Start with the examples and choose your own pace.

Try the practice →Learner workbook PDFTeaching guide PDF

BE

How much is this notebook?

One item takes is.

COST

How much do these tickets cost?

Plural tickets take do.

CLARIFY

Does that include delivery?

Ask what is included in the stated amount.

Understand

The useful distinction

Use How much is it? for one item and How much are they? for plural items. With cost as a verb, use does for one item: How much does it cost? The main verb stays in its base form after does.

Ask what the price includes when you need the total. Price, cost, fee, and fare fit different contexts: a course fee, a bus fare. Do not assume that a displayed amount includes every charge.

Try it

Check your understanding

Write an answer, then compare it with the explanation. Other wording may also be correct. These responses are for self-checking and are not automatically graded.

Compare your answer

are

2. Correct this: How much does it costs?

Compare your answer

How much does it cost?

3. Ask whether the price includes delivery.

Compare your answer

Use it

Share your ideas

See one possible response
