US Attorney General Avoids Questions About Nvidia Investigation

📅 November 25, 2026 - US Attorney General Avoids Questions About Nvidia Investigation

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

US Attorney General Todd Blanche praised Nvidia but refused to say whether the company was under federal investigation. Huang was reinstated less than two weeks after being forced to resign. Huang is due to appear before Congress next week to answer questions about national security concerns. These concerns involve his relationship with an alleged Chinese spy who led Megaspeed, a customer of Nvidia based in Singapore. The US commerce department said it was preparing to blacklist Megaspeed, meaning it planned to place the company under official restrictions. The commerce and justice departments said they began investigating Megaspeed in 2024 over possible violations of rules limiting chip exports. Nvidia's board said it was confident that giving Huang his position back posed no national security risk. Trump said he had spoken to Nvidia's directors and convinced them to bring Huang back. The board said its decision served the best interests of shareholders, employees, partners and customers. Nvidia's share price rose more than 31% on Monday after the news that Huang would lead the company again. The share price closed down nearly 6%, although it remained above its level at the end of the previous week. Huang reportedly apologised to Nvidia employees on Tuesday and said he had made serious mistakes.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. reinstated (verb, past participle): Given back a position that someone had previously lost.
2. alleged (adjective): Described as having done something wrong, although this has not been proved.
3. restrictions (plural noun): Official limits on what a person or company is allowed to do.
4. violations (plural noun): Actions that break rules or laws.
5. confident (adjective): Feeling sure that something is true or will happen.
6. convinced (verb, past tense): Successfully persuaded someone to believe something or take an action.
7. share price (noun phrase): The amount someone pays to buy one unit of ownership in a company.
8. apologised (verb, past tense): Said sorry for something done wrong.

Grammar Focus: Past simple passive
Use "was" or "were" followed by a past participle to describe a completed past action when the receiver is the focus. In "was reinstated", Huang receives the action. We can leave out who performed it when that information is not the focus.
Example from the text: "Huang was reinstated less than two weeks after being forced to resign."
