Asking for and giving advice

Ask a focused question and offer a useful option.

Level ranges are study guidance. Start with the examples and choose your own pace.

Try the practice →Learner workbook PDFTeaching guide PDF

CONTEXT

I need to reach the station by nine.

State the goal and constraint.

QUESTION

Which route would you recommend?

Ask something the listener can answer.

OPTION + REASON

You could take the bus because it stops nearby.

Offer an option with a relevant reason.

Understand

The useful distinction

Give enough context for the listener to understand the problem. State your goal or constraint, then ask a specific question: I have ten minutes. Which route would you suggest? Advice is normally uncountable: some advice, a piece of advice.

When responding, offer an option and a reason. Could can present a possibility; should can make a stronger recommendation. Ask a clarifying question when information is missing. Acknowledge limits rather than giving confident advice on something you do not know.
