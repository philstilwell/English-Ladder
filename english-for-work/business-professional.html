Business and Professional English

A useful business message makes the purpose, evidence and next step clear. These courses help you explain a recommendation, question an assumption, negotiate a constraint and respond professionally when expectations differ.

Choose the work you do

Start with the course that matches your responsibilities. Finance and financial advice cover different audiences; law and legal operations focus on different kinds of work. Consulting, strategy and project management offer practice for cross-functional conversations. Human resources and leadership courses focus on expectations, feedback and participation.

Make the practice practical

Read a case and identify what the speaker can confirm. Give a brief response before looking at the model. Then ask a partner to challenge one assumption or request a clearer next step. Finish with a short written message for the same reader.
