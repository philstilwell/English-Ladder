Lesson 01: Confirming openings and measurement references
Original fictional case: The hall drawing labels a door opening 900 millimeters. The client assumes this is clear passage width. The note does not say rough or finished opening; no door has been ordered. The designer can clarify the reference.
Language workshop: Ask what the dimension describes
Communication goal: Distinguish a client's usable-space requirement from a drawing dimension.
Language guidance: An opening dimension needs units and reference surfaces. Rough opening, finished opening, door-leaf width, and usable clear passage are different quantities. Even a finished opening measurement may not establish passage with the door and hardware present. Ask which requirement matters before discussing an order size.
Useful frames (complete the gaps with case facts): Is ___ measured between ___ or ___? / When you say clear width, do you mean ___? / We have the dimension, but still need ___.
Vocabulary: rough opening: The framed space intended to receive a door or window unit before surrounding finishes are complete. / finished opening: The opening measured between specified completed surrounding surfaces; this is not automatically the usable clear passage with a door installed and open. / jamb: A side or top member of a door or window frame. / datum: An agreed reference point or line from which project dimensions are measured.
Speaking task: Prepare for two minutes. Speak for 45-60 seconds using the case facts, then respond to your partner's question. Switch roles and repeat without reading the model response.
Partner: You are the client. You need the usable passage clarified and have not chosen a door unit.
Guided response task: Complete a workplace dialogue by selecting one of four supplied options. Compare the feedback and say the completed exchange aloud. Do not write an open-ended message.
Optional second-round challenge: The designer confirms a rough opening, but the client still asks whether a large cabinet will fit. Separate the two measurements.
Model for comparison AFTER my attempt, not a response to give me first: The drawing gives 900 millimeters, but it does not identify the measurement surfaces. Do you need that as the clear passage width, or were you referring to the framed opening? I will ask the designer to confirm the datum and jamb allowance before we treat this dimension as an order size.
END REFERENCE
