📅 October 11, 2026 - People Protest New Data Centre Plans

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Hundreds of people are protesting new data centre plans. They worry about the data centre's effects on the local landscape. Developers want to build a large computer complex. This project could create many jobs during construction. About 300 people came to a rally to show their opposition. More than 22,500 people signed a petition against the plan. An opponent said the centre would destroy a beautiful part of the area.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. protesting (verb): showing that you are against something, often in public with many other people.
2. plans (noun): detailed ideas or proposals for how something will be done in the future.
3. data centre (noun): a large building full of computers that store and manage information.
4. landscape (noun): the natural features of a piece of land, like hills, trees, and water.
5. developers (noun): people or companies that buy land and build new buildings or projects on it.
6. jobs (noun): paid work that a person does regularly to earn money.
7. petition (noun): a formal written request signed by many people, asking for something specific.

Grammar Focus: Using "could" for Possibility
We use the word "could" to talk about something that is possible in the future, but not certain. It means it might happen, but it's not guaranteed. For example, 'This project could create many jobs' means it is possible it will create jobs, but it hasn't happened yet.
Example from the text: "This project could create many jobs during construction."
