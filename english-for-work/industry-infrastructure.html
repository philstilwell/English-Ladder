English for Industry and Infrastructure

An operational update needs a clear status, a specific problem and an accurate next action. Practice the language of production meetings, service interruptions, design coordination, shipping delays and other situations where teams depend on shared information.

Choose the work you do

For frontline work, choose delivery driving, warehouse distribution, housekeeping, landscaping, carpentry, electrical work, plumbing, heating and refrigeration, or automotive service. Broader manufacturing, logistics, construction, aviation, energy, environmental and telecommunications courses emphasize team coordination and operational decisions.

Make the practice practical

Give a short update that distinguishes completed work from pending checks. Have a partner introduce a delay or missing record and ask you to clarify its effect. Preserve the stated facts and approval limits. These lessons practice communication and do not replace authorized operating or safety procedures.
