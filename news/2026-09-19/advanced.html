📅 September 19, 2026 - Ed Sheeran Addresses Macklemore Controversy and Israel-Gaza Conflict

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ed Sheeran addressed the recent upheaval caused by rapper Macklemore's removal from his concert tour, speaking about free speech and the Israel-Gaza situation. He stated he had never intended to be an activist musician, but the situation placed him amid a passionate debate on free speech and the world's most complex political issue. During his Philadelphia show, Sheeran described the 7 October 2023 attacks as "horrific" and labeled the events unfolding in Gaza as "unjustifiable." The widespread unease led to several artists withdrawing from the tour, with ticket sales declining and prices dropping for the remaining US leg. Sheeran clarified that the decision to drop Macklemore originated from his promoters, not from him personally. He expressed his belief that his concerts should always be a safe space for everyone, underscoring his commitment to his fans. Emphasizing a humanitarian perspective, Sheeran articulated his distress over the "catastrophic and unjustifiable and disproportionate" events in Gaza. He conveyed profound sorrow for the immense devastation, the loss of civilian lives, and the systemic injustice witnessed in the West Bank. Sheeran also voiced strong objections to the concept of venues "preapproving" performers or their content, a stance met with audience applause. Outside the venue, a few dozen protestors waved flags as ticket holders streamed in, yet the concert proceeded largely unfettered. Attendees interviewed by the BBC shared a common resolve to enjoy the music while simultaneously championing freedom of speech. Macklemore's original performance involved a song dedicated to pro-Palestinian protestors, in which he referred to "genocide" in Gaza, a term Israel disputes. Messina Touring Group indicated that Macklemore's departure was due to certain venues threatening to cancel concerts if he performed. Robert Kraft, owner of Gillette Stadium, subsequently confirmed his decision to bar Macklemore from performing at Sheeran's upcoming shows there.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. upheaval (noun): A violent or sudden change or disruption to something.
2. amid (preposition): In the middle of or surrounded by.
3. articulated (verb): Expressed or stated clearly and coherently.
4. disproportionate (adjective): Too large or too small in comparison to something else; lacking appropriate balance or relation.
5. conveyed (verb): Communicated or made known; transmitted.
6. profound (adjective): Very great or intense; having or showing great knowledge or insight.
7. systemic (adjective): Relating to a system, especially as opposed to a particular part; affecting a whole body or system.
8. objections (noun): Expressions or feelings of disapproval or opposition; reasons for disagreeing.
9. unfettered (adjective): Not bound by chains or shackles; unrestrained or unrestricted, allowing complete freedom.
10. championing (verb): Supporting the cause of; defending or advocating for.

Advanced Grammar: Participial Phrases for Adverbial Modification
Participial phrases, often beginning with an -ing form (present participle) or a past participle (e.g., 'interviewed'), can function as adverbial modifiers at the beginning or end of a sentence. They provide additional information about the main clause, such as cause, effect, purpose, or a related action, and efficiently condense information. In the example, 'Emphasizing a humanitarian perspective' explains the manner in which Sheeran articulated his distress.
Example from the text: "Emphasizing a humanitarian perspective, Sheeran articulated his distress over the "catastrophic and unjustifiable and disproportionate" events in Gaza."
