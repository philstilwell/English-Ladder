Title: Ukrainian Protests Erupt Over Defence Minister's Dismissal

Protests have broken out across Ukrainian cities following President Zelensky's unexplained decision to remove the defence minister, causing significant unrest among citizens and military personnel.

Read the story

Citizens in several Ukrainian cities have begun to stage protests. These demonstrations are a reaction to President Zelensky's recent removal of the defence minister. Mykhailo Fedorov's dismissal has sparked considerable public outrage. Many people are demanding a clear explanation for this unexpected decision. The president, however, has not yet offered any official reason for the change. This lack of transparency is causing great upset among various groups. Both members of the military and broader civil society are expressing their concerns. The situation highlights growing tensions between the government and the public. Such significant changes without justification can undermine public trust. International observers are closely monitoring the developments in Ukraine.

Words and grammar

1. protests (noun): Public expressions of disapproval, often involving demonstrations or marches, against a policy or action.
2. removal (noun): The act of taking someone or something away from their position or place.
3. dismissal (noun): The official act of ending someone's employment or removing them from a position.
4. civil society (noun phrase): The part of society made up of non-governmental organizations and institutions that manifest the will of citizens, such as charities, community groups, and NGOs.
5. great upset (noun phrase): A strong feeling of distress, anger, or sadness caused by a difficult or disappointing situation.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Simple
The Present Perfect Simple is used to talk about actions or states that happened at an unspecified time in the past but have a connection to the present, or actions that started in the past and continue up to now. It often emphasizes the result of an action rather than when it happened.
Example from the text: "The president, however, has not yet offered any official reason for the change."
