📅 August 24, 2026 - Taliban's Rule and Changes in Afghanistan

Read the story

The Taliban returned to power in Afghanistan. This important event happened five years ago. The BBC news team is following senior figures of the Taliban. They want to see how this group runs the country. The Taliban is reshaping Afghanistan's daily life. They control many aspects, including divorce rules. Smartphones and technology are also under their control. BBC reporters are observing the actions of these leaders. The group makes decisions that affect all people. This report shows how the Taliban controls everyday activities.

Words and grammar

1. returned (verb): To come back to a place or a position after being away.
2. group (noun): A number of people or things that are together in one place or are connected in some way.
3. leaders (noun): Important people who guide or direct a group or country.
4. controls (verb): To have the power to make decisions about something and decide what happens.
5. reshaping (verb): To change the form or character of something in a significant way.
6. senior figures (noun phrase): Important and powerful people in an organization or government.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or events that happened at a specific time in the past and are now finished. For regular verbs, we often add '-ed'. For irregular verbs, the form changes.
Example from the text: "The Taliban returned to power in Afghanistan."
