Anthropic Starts Selling Shares on the Stock Market

📅 November 20, 2026 - Anthropic Starts Selling Shares on the Stock Market

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

People can now buy and sell shares in Anthropic on the stock market. Shares are small parts of a company. Anthropic makes Claude, a popular artificial intelligence (AI) tool that can talk with people. The company started just five years ago. Its share price was set at $83 before trading began. The price rose to $111.53, but it fell later that day. Each share cost $97.17 at the end of the day. Some investors, people who put money into companies, said the first day seemed to be a success. The price changes seemed to show that investors were still unsure about the future of AI.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. shares (plural noun): Small parts of a company that people can buy and sell.
2. popular (adjective): Liked or used by many people.
3. rose (verb, simple past): Went up in price.
4. fell (verb, simple past): Went down in price.
5. success (noun): Something that goes well.
6. unsure (adjective): Not certain about something.

Grammar Focus: Simple past
Use the simple past for things that happened before now. Many verbs take “-ed”, as in “started”. Some change differently: “rise” becomes “rose”, and “fall” becomes “fell”. For these three verbs, use the same past form with every subject.
Example from the text: "The company started just five years ago."
