A trial ends without a decision

📅 September 05, 2026 - A trial ends without a decision

Read the story

A judge in Massachusetts has declared a mistrial in the Lindsay Clancy murder case. The jury could not agree after a week of discussions following weeks of testimony. The case concerns the deaths of her three children. Her lawyer tried unsuccessfully to prevent the mistrial. Another hearing is scheduled for later in the month to consider the next steps. The prosecutor said the legal team would discuss whether to seek another trial. Clancy remains held on the existing charges. The legal outcome remains uncertain.

Words and grammar

1. testimony (noun): statements given by witnesses as evidence in court
2. prevent (verb): stop something from happening
3. hearing (noun): a court session where a legal matter is considered
4. prosecutor (noun): a lawyer who presents a criminal case against an accused person
5. charges (plural noun): formal accusations that someone has committed a crime
6. declared (verb): Stated something officially, publicly, or strongly.
7. scheduled (verb): arranged to take place at a particular time
8. seek (verb): to try to obtain or achieve something

Grammar Focus: Remain + adjective
Remains uncertain means continues to be uncertain. It describes the current state, not a future verdict.
Example from the text: "The legal outcome remains uncertain."
