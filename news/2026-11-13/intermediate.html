📅 November 13, 2026 - Nvidia Faces Reported Leadership and Financial Uncertainty

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The supplied report says Jensen Huang stepped down as Nvidia's chief executive after allegations about his personal conduct. It says US politicians have expressed concern about possible national security risks linked to his alleged conduct. According to the report, Nvidia lost $550 billion in market value over five days as the scandal dominated headlines. It identifies Colette Kress as interim chief executive while the company searches for a permanent replacement for Huang. The report says Kress called an emergency meeting of her executive team to discuss the company's future. It also says Nvidia promised an independent inquiry that would consider all available information. According to the report, Nvidia said it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets had been compromised. The report says companies developing artificial intelligence tools suddenly needed large numbers of Nvidia chips to process data. The report explains that Nvidia's chips were originally designed for computer gaming. According to the report, many employees earned millions of dollars by selling their Nvidia shares as the price rose sharply. It says Nvidia represents about 7 percent of the S&P 500 index, which is offered through popular US retirement investment funds. The report explains that retirement account balances rise and fall as the stock market changes.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. stepped down (phrasal verb, past simple): Left an important job or position.
2. allegations (plural noun): Claims that someone did something wrong, which have not been proved.
3. market value (noun phrase): The total value of a company's shares at their current price.
4. interim (adjective): Serving temporarily until someone is chosen for the position permanently.
5. permanent (adjective): Intended to continue rather than last for only a short, planned period.
6. inquiry (noun): An organized process of examining information to find out what happened.
7. compromised (verb, past participle): Put at risk or made less secure.
8. shares (plural noun): Units of ownership in a company that people can buy and sell.

Grammar Focus: Past simple passive
Use “was” or “were” plus a past participle to form the past simple passive. In the clause about Nvidia's chips, “were originally designed” focuses on the chips and their purpose; the designer is not named.
Example from the text: "The report explains that Nvidia's chips were originally designed for computer gaming."
