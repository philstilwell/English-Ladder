Word choice · A2–B1 · 10–15 minutes

Success, succeed, successful, successfully

Use the correct member of a word family.

Level ranges are study guidance. Start with the examples and choose your own pace.

Try the practice →Learner workbook PDFTeaching guide PDF

NOUN

The event was a success.

A success names a successful event.

VERB

We succeeded in finding a room.

In is followed by an -ing form.

ADVERB

We booked the room successfully.

Successfully modifies booked.

Understand

The useful distinction

Success is a noun: The event was a success. Succeed is a verb: We succeeded. Successful is an adjective: The event was successful. Successfully is an adverb: We completed it successfully.

Use succeed in + -ing for achieving an action: succeed in finding a solution. Not every noun use needs an article: hard work can contribute to success. Describe what the success means instead of relying only on a positive label.
