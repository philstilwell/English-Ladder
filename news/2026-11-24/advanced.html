📅 November 24, 2026 - Don Toliver delays London shows after Manchester arena evacuation

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Don Toliver postponed two London performances after his Manchester concert was evacuated on Sunday following the release of an unidentified substance into the audience. Live Nation attributed the London disruption to a logistical constraint: the stage and production equipment remained in Manchester and could not be moved in time for Monday's O2 performance or Tuesday's Wembley Arena show. Wembley Arena's replacement date is 14 December, while The O2 performance has been moved to 15 December. Police said 89 people displayed symptoms believed to be linked to contact with the substance, and one person was taken to hospital. Greater Manchester Police declared a major incident and said 12,000 people were evacuated from the arena. A further 88 people underwent dry decontamination, a procedure for removing contamination without water, and were subsequently accommodated at the Etihad Stadium while the incident was managed, police said. Fans described distressing scenes, while police initially suspected that the substance was tear gas. Police were continuing to test items at the arena to identify the substance, and no arrests had been made. Investigators were seeking to establish the substance's nature, its origin and any associated risk, according to Assistant Chief Constable Tom Harding. The question of whether any criminality had occurred was also under investigation. Some fans questioned security arrangements, complaining about a lack of searches as people entered the Manchester concert. The arena was said to use frictionless screening that detected prohibited items without requiring people to empty their pockets. Murray said many large venues used mass screening technology, and the report also described camera monitoring, security guards and plain-clothed officers at such venues. Murray considered current technology adequate but argued that it needed to become more sophisticated over the next few years. Venues were said to be concentrating on excluding items, such as explosives, that could cause mass casualties. A statement said full refunds for the Manchester show would be processed automatically.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. logistical constraint (noun phrase): A practical limitation involving the organisation or movement of people or resources.
2. dry decontamination (noun phrase): A procedure for removing potentially harmful material from a person without using water.
3. accommodated (verb, past participle): Provided with a place to stay temporarily, without necessarily being given overnight lodging.
4. associated risk (noun phrase): The possibility of harm connected with the substance or circumstances being investigated.
5. criminality (uncountable noun): Conduct that constitutes a criminal offence, rather than merely causing concern or disruption.
6. frictionless screening (noun phrase): Security checking designed to minimise interruptions or actions required from people passing through.
7. prohibited items (plural noun phrase): Objects that the applicable rules forbid people from bringing into a particular place.
8. plain-clothed officers (plural noun phrase): Police officers wearing ordinary clothes rather than uniforms.
9. sophisticated (adjective): Technologically advanced and capable of performing more complex or precise tasks.
10. mass casualties (plural noun phrase): Large numbers of people killed or injured in an incident.

Advanced Grammar: Reduced passive relative clauses with reporting verbs
A past-participle phrase can replace a passive relative clause: "symptoms believed to be linked" means "symptoms that were believed to be linked". This compresses the report while preserving uncertainty about the connection.
Example from the text: "Police said 89 people displayed symptoms believed to be linked to contact with the substance, and one person was taken to hospital."
