Read the story

There is news about Israel and the West Bank. Israel is accused of weaponising archaeology. This means using old sites in a bad way. These sites are ancient and important. Israel is planning large-scale land expropriations. This means taking a lot of land. The land is in historic Palestinian areas. The West Bank is a special place. Many people are worried about these plans. Some say Israel uses archaeology to control land.

Words and grammar

1. accused of (verb phrase): told that you did something wrong or bad.
2. weaponising (verb): using something as a weapon to hurt or control others, not for its original purpose.
3. ancient (adjective): very, very old; from a long time ago.
4. planning (verb): thinking about what you will do in the future and making arrangements for it.
5. land expropriations (noun phrase): when a government takes private land for public use or other reasons, sometimes without agreement.

Grammar Focus: The Verb 'To Be' and Present Continuous
We use 'is' for one thing or person, and 'are' for many things or people. We often use 'is' or 'are' with a verb ending in '-ing' to talk about actions happening now or plans for the future.
Example from the text: "Israel is planning large-scale land expropriations."
