📅 September 19, 2026 - Ed Sheeran Apologises for Tour Mistakes and Speaks About World Issues

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ed Sheeran talked about a problem at his concert. He apologised for 'mistakes' at his concert. Rapper Macklemore was removed from his tour before this show. Other artists also left the tour. Ticket prices went down because of this problem. Sheeran said his promoters, not him, decided to remove Macklemore. He also talked about the attacks on October 7, 2023, and the situation in Gaza. Sheeran said his music is about unity, not politics.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. apologised (verb): said "sorry" for something wrong.
2. mistakes (noun): wrong actions or errors.
3. tour (noun): a trip by a music group to many places to perform.
4. removed (verb): taken away from a group or place.
5. problem (noun): something that is difficult to deal with.
6. prices (noun): the money you pay for things.
7. unity (noun): being together and agreeing.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. It often uses '-ed' at the end of regular verbs or a different form for irregular verbs.
Example from the text: "He apologised for 'mistakes' at his concert."
