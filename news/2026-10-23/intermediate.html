📅 October 23, 2026 - Trump Says Musk Brought Back Ukrainian Internet Access

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

US President Donald Trump claims that Elon Musk has brought back internet access for Ukraine's armed forces. Trump made these comments after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said the satellite network was still limited. The recent speed limit made Ukraine's offensive equipment powerless against Russian attacks. Modern warfare often relies on high-speed internet to effectively direct unmanned vehicles. Trump refused to discuss a report that he personally called Musk to lift the restrictions. The journal reported that the system was working more normally shortly after their conversation. Ukraine relies on the service for various uses, and Elon Musk has repeatedly denied restricting access. Meanwhile, Musk has suggested that the US government ultimately controls this specific network.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. armed forces (noun): a country's military organizations, such as the army, navy, and air force
2. offensive (adjective): used for attacking someone or something, rather than for defending
3. powerless (adjective): lacking the strength or authority to act or to prevent something from happening
4. warfare (noun): the activity of fighting a war, often involving specific weapons or methods
5. effectively (adverb): in a way that is successful and achieves the result that you want
6. restrictions (noun): official rules or laws that limit what people can do or use
7. repeatedly (adverb): happening many times or again and again
8. ultimately (adverb): finally, after a series of things have happened, or at the most basic level

Grammar Focus: Reporting verbs followed by an -ing form (gerund)
Some reporting verbs, like "deny", "admit", or "suggest", are followed by a verb ending in "-ing" (a gerund) rather than a "to" infinitive. In the example, the verb "denied" is followed directly by "restricting" to talk about an action someone claims they did not do.
Example from the text: "Ukraine relies on the service for various uses, and Elon Musk has repeatedly denied restricting access."
