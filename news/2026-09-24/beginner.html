Protein Powder Prices Go Up

📅 September 24, 2026 - Protein Powder Prices Go Up

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Amarpreet has been drinking protein shakes for many years. His protein shakes are now much more expensive. In 2020, his protein cost about £60. Last year, the price went up to £120. More people want protein, but there is not enough. So, the cost of ingredients is higher. Amarpreet will buy a cheaper brand now. Evie will not buy protein powder anymore.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. protein shakes (noun): A drink with a lot of protein, often used by people who exercise.
2. expensive (adjective): Costs a lot of money.
3. cost (verb): To have a certain price.
4. price (noun): The amount of money you need to pay for something.
5. ingredients (noun): Different foods or things you mix to make food or a product.
6. cheaper (adjective): Costs less money; not as expensive.
7. anymore (adverb): Not now; not happening in the future.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or situations that happened at a specific time in the past and are now finished. For example, 'went up' tells us about a price change that already happened.
Example from the text: "Last year, the price went up to £120."
