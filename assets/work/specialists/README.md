# Specialist Profession Illustrations

Eight original transparent illustrations created with OpenAI's native image
generator for this expansion, with the user's approval. No paid external image
service, stock substitution, programmatically drawn replacement, or API fallback
was used. PNG originals are retained here for the books; 384-pixel transparent
WebP derivatives are used on the site. Format conversion creates no new artwork.

## Prompt Directions

All eight prompts used the existing `assets/work/medical/general-practitioners.png`
as a style reference only: polished educational illustration, crisp dark outlines,
dimensional painted shading, blue and white with restrained warm accents, a compact
centered composition readable at 104 pixels, fully visible objects, transparent
margins and no lettering, branding, caption, frame, or watermark.

- `nutrition-dietetics`: nutrition professional with a meal-planning clipboard,
  a plate of vegetables, grains, and protein, and an apple; no weight-loss or pill imagery.
- `sports-medicine`: clinician and adult athlete discussing a knee model beside
  rehabilitation equipment; no emergency or graphic injury imagery.
- `international-law`: globe, balanced justice scales, open legal folio and pen;
  no national flags or political symbols.
- `patent-law`: invention folio with a mechanical sketch, magnifying glass,
  corresponding prototype and justice scales; distinct from generic law imagery.
- `university-professors`: university professor leading a seminar beside a
  lectern and conceptual diagram, with student notebooks; no schoolhouse or graduation cap.
- `investment-management-banking`: portfolio allocation display and transaction
  folders converging toward an agreement, with coins; one combined course illustration.
- `cross-border-ma-integration`: two businesses connected through a board showing
  people, computer systems and process checks, with a cooperation motif.
- `localization-qa`: software/game screen with different text-box widths and a
  highlighted clipping defect, magnifier, globe, VR headset and controller.

The artwork is illustrative, not a clinical, legal, technical, or financial diagram.
Original generations remain in the user's Codex generated-images directory.
