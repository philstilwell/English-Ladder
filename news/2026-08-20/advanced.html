Read the story

Kyiv was subjected to a massive attack on Thursday, marking one of the most intense aerial bombardments the capital has endured recently. Ukrainian officials confirmed that the sustained assault involved a barrage of missiles and drones, overwhelming certain defensive positions. Apartment blocks and various warehouses across the city were directly targeted, resulting in extensive structural damage and civilian casualties. Tragically, at least 13 individuals perished in the onslaught, further exacerbating the human toll of the protracted conflict. Ukraine continues to grapple with critical shortages in its sophisticated air defence systems, a vulnerability acutely exploited by the recent offensive. The ongoing strain on military resources renders the nation susceptible to such destructive incursions, posing a severe challenge to urban safety. Experts suggest that the frequency and scale of these attacks underscore the urgent imperative for accelerated military aid from Western allies. Without substantial reinforcement, Ukraine's dwindling capabilities risk further erosion, potentially altering the strategic landscape. These relentless aerial campaigns aim to destabilize civilian life and exert psychological pressure on the Ukrainian populace. The international community watches closely as Kyiv assesses the full extent of the damage and renews appeals for robust global assistance.

Words and grammar

1. massive attack (noun phrase): A very large-scale, intense, and destructive offensive, typically involving a significant number of military assets or munitions.
2. targeted (verb (past participle)): Singled out or directed at a specific object, person, or area, often with hostile intent or as an objective.
3. grapple with (phrasal verb): To struggle or contend with a difficult problem, situation, or adversary, often implying a prolonged effort to overcome it.
4. imperative (noun): An urgent need or command; something that is absolutely essential or crucial and must be done.
5. dwindling capabilities (noun phrase): A gradual reduction or decline in the capacity, power, or effectiveness of a system, resource, or entity over time.

Advanced Grammar: Participial Phrases (Present Participle)
A present participial phrase, beginning with an -ing verb, acts as an adjective to modify a noun or pronoun, adding descriptive information or explaining the result or consequence of an action. It can often replace a relative clause or an adverbial clause, making sentences more concise and sophisticated.
Example from the text: "Ukraine continues to grapple with critical shortages in its sophisticated air defence systems, a vulnerability acutely exploited by the recent offensive."
