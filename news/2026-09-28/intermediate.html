📅 September 28, 2026 - Trump-Xi Summit: What Was Unsaid Matters Most

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The recent summit between Trump and Xi in Washington was notable for what was not explicitly said. On Monday, China released details of some concrete outcomes from the talks. However, the summit produced no joint statements and few major breakthroughs. Instead, both governments issued separate accounts, suggesting they remain wary of each other. Beijing and Washington agreed to launch a new dialogue on artificial intelligence. They also established a communication channel for AI-related incidents to prevent broader confrontation. Chinese state media confirmed the trade truce has been extended until January 10. However, Beijing had hoped for a much longer extension to this truce, making it a modest gain. China secured lower tariffs on $30bn of goods entering the US, with reciprocal reductions expected. Strikingly, there was no mention of increasing China's rare earth mineral exports to the US. The White House factsheet made no mention of Taiwan, although it was clearly a priority for Xi Jinping. According to Beijing, Xi urged Trump to formally oppose Taiwan independence. The US State Department continues to classify travel to China as a Level 2 advisory, urging increased caution. Ultimately, contentious issues from advanced technology to Taiwan remain unresolved after the summit.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. notable (adjective): Important or interesting; deserving attention.
2. concrete outcomes (noun phrase): Results or achievements that are definite and clearly visible, not just ideas or plans.
3. breakthroughs (noun): Important discoveries or developments that help solve a problem.
4. wary (adjective): Feeling or showing caution about possible dangers or problems.
5. dialogue (noun): A formal discussion between two groups or countries, especially to resolve problems.
6. confrontation (noun): A hostile or argumentative meeting or situation between opposing parties.
7. trade truce (noun phrase): A temporary stop or pause in a trade war or disputes between countries.
8. tariffs (noun): Taxes imposed by a government on imported or exported goods.
9. priority (noun): A thing that is regarded as more important than others.
10. unresolved (adjective): Not solved or settled; still needing an answer or a decision.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Passive
The present perfect passive is used to describe an action that happened at an unspecified time in the past and has a connection to the present, focusing on the action itself rather than who performed it. It is formed with "has/have been" + the past participle of the main verb. For example, in the sentence "The trade truce has been extended," we know the truce is now longer, but the focus is on the extension, not specifically on who extended it.
Example from the text: "Chinese state media confirmed the trade truce has been extended until January 10."
