Lesson 01: Account Opening and KYC
Original fictional case: Application A62 is the first business-account application by Northbank Design at this fictional bank. Its authorized-signatory details are supplied, but the ownership information requested for the applicable customer review is incomplete. Applicant representative Ken asks officer Zoya to open the account immediately and finish the review later. Under the stated local process, this application cannot be approved until the required review is complete. Zoya will provide the specific outstanding-information list through the verified secure channel and arrange a review-status update Thursday at 11:00. Neither complete documents nor that update guarantee approval.
Language workshop: Explain the missing ownership information without promising approval
Communication goal: Clarify an account-opening requirement, distinguish ownership from signing authority, and give a useful next step.
Language guidance: An authorized signatory can operate an account under its mandate without necessarily being its beneficial owner. Supplying signatory details does not automatically answer the ownership question requested in this application.
Useful expressions (preserve the supplied facts): We are reviewing business-account application A62. / We have the authorized-signatory details. / The requested ownership information remains incomplete. / Signing authority and beneficial ownership are different questions. / This application requires the review to be complete before approval. / I understand that you want to begin using the account promptly. / I cannot bypass the required review. / I will provide the outstanding-information list. / Use the verified secure channel for the requested records. / We will identify what remains missing rather than request everything again. / Requirements must be assessed for this customer and application. / Complete documents do not automatically mean approved onboarding. / Any exception requires the applicable authorized review. / We will update the review status Thursday at 11:00. / That is an update appointment, not an approval guarantee. / Send the specified information so the relevant review can continue.
Vocabulary: Know Your Customer (KYC): Processes used to understand and verify relevant customer information. / customer due diligence (CDD): Assessment of customer identity, relationship purpose, and relevant risk under applicable requirements. / ownership structure: The arrangement of ownership interests in an entity. / legal entity: An organization recognized as a distinct person under applicable law. / beneficial owner: An individual meeting the applicable ownership or control definition for the review. / authorized signatory: A person permitted to act or sign for an account under its mandate. / Customer Identification Program (CIP): A U.S. bank program for obtaining and verifying required customer identity information. / control person: An individual meeting the applicable responsibility or control criterion. / account mandate: Instructions defining who may operate an account and under what authority. / identity verification: Checking identity using the applicable evidence and process. / documentary verification: Verification using specified documents. / non-documentary verification: Verification using appropriate methods other than specified identity documents. / nature and purpose: The intended role and use of the customer relationship. / expected activity: The anticipated account use relevant to understanding the relationship. / customer risk profile: The assessed characteristics relevant to the customer's risk context. / source of funds: The origin of money involved in a relationship or transaction. / source of wealth: The origin of a person's or entity's overall accumulated wealth. / onboarding: The process of establishing a new customer relationship. / account application: A request to establish an account with supplied supporting information. / outstanding requirement: A requested or applicable condition that has not yet been satisfied. / verification status: The current stage of checking relevant information. / risk-based procedure: A process calibrated to relevant assessed risks and applicable requirements. / exception review: Authorized assessment of a proposed departure from a stated process. / approval authority: The defined power to approve a specified action or relationship.
Speaking task: Read turns 1-10, distinguishing signatory details from ownership information. Swap roles for turns 11-20; separate document receipt, verification, and approval. Complete the B14 transfer and check the reference, missing document, owner, and update time.
Partner: Ken: Applicant representative / Zoya: Account-opening officer
Guided response task: Select supplied answers for the dialogue gaps. Check the explanations, then read the completed exchange aloud; no original writing is required.
Optional second-round challenge: Application B14 has signatory details but lacks the requested ownership chart. Hana will coordinate the review. The next status update is Monday at 10:00; approval is not guaranteed.
Model for comparison AFTER my attempt, not a response to give me first: Ken: When will I hear from you? An accurate pending status would still help our planning.
Zoya: We will update the account application status Thursday at eleven hundred. That is a communication appointment, not an approval guarantee. We will identify any continuing information gap or review step rather than imply a decision we do not have.
Ken: I will use the verified channel, supply the specified ownership records, and keep the distinction between submission and approval clear internally.
Zoya: Thank you. The eventual decision remains with the relevant approval authority. We can make the process understandable and keep the next contact reliable while preserving the required review and the actual account status.
