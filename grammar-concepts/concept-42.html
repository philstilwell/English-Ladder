CATEGORY

A library is a place where people can borrow books.

Start with a familiar kind of thing.

EXAMPLE

You can borrow a novel and return it later.

Make the activity concrete.

CHECK

How is borrowing different from buying?

Ask about the important distinction.

Understand

The useful distinction

Start with a familiar category, then describe the defining feature and give a concrete example. A commuter is a person who regularly travels between home and work. The example should clarify the definition, not replace it.

Adjust the detail to the listener. Ask what they already know and check understanding with a useful question. Avoid explaining one unfamiliar word through several more unfamiliar words. An analogy helps only if you also state where it differs.

Try it

Check your understanding

Write an answer, then compare it with the explanation. Other wording may also be correct. These responses are for self-checking and are not automatically graded.

1. Which opening is clearer for a beginner: A commuter is a person ... / A commuter is an intermodal participant ...?

Compare your answer

A commuter is a person ...

A familiar category gives the listener a starting point.

2. Explain borrow in one simple sentence.

Compare your answer

Borrow means use something for a time and then return it.

Include the temporary use and expected return.

3. Why might an example alone be insufficient?

Compare your answer

It may not show what all uses have in common.

A short definition gives the general meaning.

Use it

Share your ideas

Explain a familiar service to someone who has never used it.

See one possible response

A delivery locker is a secure place to collect a package. You use a code to open its door.

Self-check: Does your sentence express your intended meaning? Does its form follow the relevant pattern? Can a reader understand the context?
