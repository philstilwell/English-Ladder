Read the story

Overnight, Ukrainian drones carried out strikes against several locations in southern Russia. The primary targets of these attacks were logistics hubs operated by Wildberries, a major Russian online retailer. These crucial facilities, responsible for storing and distributing goods, are located in the Krasnodar and Stavropol regions. Reports indicate that the drones successfully hit more sites connected to the company's operations. This incident marks a significant escalation, focusing on civilian commercial infrastructure rather than purely military targets. Wildberries is a popular online retailer, and its operations have likely been disrupted by these events. The strikes highlight the growing reach and capability of Ukrainian drone technology in the ongoing conflict. Such attacks can have considerable economic consequences, affecting supply chains and consumer access to goods. Authorities in the affected regions are currently assessing the extent of the damage caused by the drone strikes. The specific impact on the retailer's ability to deliver products to customers remains to be fully determined.

Words and grammar

1. logistics hubs (noun phrase): Central points where goods are stored, sorted, and sent out for delivery.
2. hit (verb): To make physical contact with something, often causing damage.
3. infrastructure (noun): The basic physical and organizational structures and facilities needed for the operation of a society or enterprise.
4. operations (noun): The activities or processes of a business or organization.
5. retailer (noun): A person or business that sells goods to the public in relatively small quantities for use or consumption rather than for resale.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Passive
The Present Perfect Passive is used to describe an action that started in the past and has a result or impact in the present, where the focus is on the object that received the action, not who performed it. It is formed with 'has/have been' + the past participle of the main verb.
Example from the text: "Wildberries is a popular online retailer, and its operations have likely been disrupted by these events."
