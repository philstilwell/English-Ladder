While and during

Connect activities that overlap in time.

Level ranges are study guidance. Start with the examples and choose your own pace.

Try the practice →Learner workbook PDFTeaching guide PDF

WHILE + CLAUSE

The phone rang while I was cooking.

A subject and verb follow while.

DURING + NOUN

The phone rang during dinner.

Dinner names the event.

FOR + DURATION

We talked for an hour.

An hour measures the duration.

Understand

The useful distinction

While commonly introduces a clause: while I was cooking. During is a preposition followed by a noun phrase: during dinner. Both can locate something within another period or event.

While cooking is also possible when the understood subject is clear. While does not require a continuous tense in every context: while I am away is natural. During does not say how long an action lasted; use for with a duration.

Try it

Check your understanding

Write an answer, then compare it with the explanation. Other wording may also be correct. These responses are for self-checking and are not automatically graded.

1. I took notes ___ the meeting.

Compare your answer

during

The meeting is a noun phrase.

2. Correct this: During I was reading, it began to rain.

Compare your answer

While I was reading, it began to rain.

I was reading is a clause.

3. Which means a duration: during the lesson or for an hour?

Compare your answer

for an hour

During identifies the surrounding event; for gives the length.

Use it

Share your ideas

Describe an interruption during an activity.

See one possible response

A friend called while I was making lunch.

Self-check: Does your sentence express your intended meaning? Does its form follow the relevant pattern? Can a reader understand the context?
