They function by omitting the relative pronoun (e.g., 'which,' 'that') and the 'to be' verb, resulting in a more economical and sophisticated sentence structure common in formal writing. This compression helps to maintain a fluent and professional tone while conveying complex details.
Example from the text: "President Donald Trump recently declared the United States would significantly downsize the exercises conducted jointly with South Korea."
