Robot Boats Help Search for Sea Mines

Beginner · A1–A2

News archive →
📅 November 11, 2026 - Robot Boats Help Search for Sea Mines

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The conflict in the Gulf gives the US a chance to test robots that look for sea mines. Sea mines are bombs in the water that can explode and badly damage passing ships. In the past, about 80 sailors worked on each special ship to find mines. Robots now do more of this work to keep people safer, while people watch them from land. Some robot systems may need only about 20 people working on land. One type of robot boat uses sound to find mines under the water. US robots can destroy a mine or mark its place so ships can avoid it. These robot systems cost less than the special ships used to find mines. The article says robots can reduce danger, but they cannot show that there are no mines.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. look for (phrasal verb): Try to find something.
2. sailors (plural noun): People who work on ships.
3. land (noun): The ground, away from the sea.
4. destroy (verb): Damage something so badly that it cannot work anymore.
5. avoid (verb): Stay away from someone or something.
6. cost (verb): Have a price that people must pay.

Grammar Focus: Can for ability
Use “can” before a verb to say what someone or something is able to do. Use the verb without “to”, and do not add “s” after “he”, “she” or “it”. Use “cannot” to say that someone or something is not able to do something.
Example from the text: "US robots can destroy a mine or mark its place so ships can avoid it."
