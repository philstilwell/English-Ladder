Read the story

French President Emmanuel Macron says Russian 'hybrid attacks' are intensifying against Europe. He has asked his government to protect important places. These important places are critical infrastructure and defence industry sites. France was targeted by these hybrid attacks in the past few weeks. Other European leaders also warn that Russia may be preparing attacks. A 'hybrid attack' uses military force with non-military methods like cyberattacks. It can also include spreading misinformation.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. intensifying (verb): Getting stronger or happening more often.
2. government (noun): The group of people who control a country or state.
3. protect (verb): To keep someone or something safe from harm.
4. critical infrastructure (noun phrase): Very important systems like roads, power, and water that a country needs.
5. targeted (verb): To aim at a specific person or place; to choose as a goal.
6. preparing (verb): Getting ready for something.
7. cyberattacks (noun): Attacks on computer systems, often to steal information or cause problems.

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice (Simple Past)
We use the passive voice to talk about an action when we do not know who did it, or when the action is more important than who did it. We form it with "was" or "were" + the past participle of the main verb.
Example from the text: "France was targeted by these hybrid attacks in the past few weeks."
