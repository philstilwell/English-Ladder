📅 October 25, 2026 - Mass Protests Across France Lead to Nearly 1,000 Arrests

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Hundreds of thousands of people took part in mass demonstrations across France on Saturday. While many marches were calm, violent conflict erupted between protesters and riot police in Paris. Some protesters built makeshift barricades and threw projectiles at officers, while masked youths smashed windows. In response, law enforcement used water cannon to disperse crowds in several cities. Despite the violence, officials have so far managed to avoid the extreme disorder seen last weekend. Officials stated that nearly 1,000 arrests were made following the protests. Two demonstrators explained that they feel abandoned by the government. Similar issues have triggered mass protests in several other European cities. These recent events follow weeks of escalating tension over rising fuel prices.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. demonstrations (noun): Public events where large groups of people meet or march to show support or opposition for something.
2. erupted (verb): Started suddenly and often violently.
3. makeshift (adjective): Made to be used temporarily when nothing better is available.
4. projectiles (noun): Objects that are thrown at a target, often as weapons.
5. disperse (verb): To make a crowd of people leave an area and spread out in different directions.
6. disorder (noun): A situation where people behave in a noisy, disorganized, or violent way in public.
7. triggered (verb): Caused a reaction or series of events to start happening.
8. escalating (adjective): Becoming greater, more serious, or worse over time.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Tense
We use the present perfect tense ("have" or "has" + past participle) to describe an action that happened at an unspecified time in the past and has a result or connection to the present. In this sentence, the issues "have triggered" protests, showing that a past event is closely connected to the current situation.
Example from the text: "Similar issues have triggered mass protests in several other European cities."
