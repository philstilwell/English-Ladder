Read the story

Meta is a very big company. It owns Facebook and Instagram. Meta was fined a lot of money. This fine is for problems with child safety. A court made a ruling against Meta. The new fine is 567 million dollars. Meta is a social media giant. Before, Meta was fined 375 million dollars for the same case. Now, the total money Meta must pay is 942 million dollars. This is a very large amount of money for a company to pay.

Words and grammar

1. fined (verb (past tense)): To make someone pay money as a punishment for breaking a rule.
2. child safety (noun phrase): Keeping children safe from harm or danger.
3. ruling (noun): An official decision made by a court or judge.
4. social media giant (noun phrase): A very big and powerful company that owns many social media websites or apps.
5. total (noun): The whole amount or number of something, when everything is added together.
6. company (noun): a business that sells goods or provides services

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or events that happened completely in the past. We often add '-ed' to regular verbs. For irregular verbs like 'to be', it changes to 'was' or 'were' when talking about one person or thing.
Example from the text: "Meta was fined a lot of money."
