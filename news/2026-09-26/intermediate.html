📅 September 26, 2026 - Ed Sheeran Concerts Cancelled Due to Severe Weather Warnings

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ed Sheeran's US concerts in Massachusetts were cancelled this weekend. This cancellation was due to severe weather warnings across the New England area. He had been scheduled to perform two shows at Gillette Stadium near Boston. A statement from the concert promoter confirmed the difficult decision to cancel the events. The decision was made with the safety of fans and staff as the top priority. Weather warnings for high winds were active until the early hours of Sunday. Ticketholders who purchased tickets through Ticketmaster will be automatically refunded. The tour had previously been in the news for a different controversy. This involved dropping support act Macklemore after he made pro-Palestinian comments on stage. Stadium owners had lobbied for Macklemore's removal from the shows.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. due to (prepositional phrase): because of; as a result of something.
2. perform (verb): to entertain an audience by singing, dancing, acting, or playing music.
3. promoter (noun): a person or company that organizes and often finances large public events, such as concerts or sports matches.
4. confirmed (verb): stated that something is definitely true or will happen.
5. priority (noun): something that is more important than other things and needs to be dealt with first.
6. active (adjective): currently in operation or progress; functioning.
7. purchased (verb): bought something; acquired something by paying for it.
8. controversy (noun): a public argument or disagreement, especially one that involves different opinions about something important.

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action or the result of the action, rather than who performed it. It's formed with a form of 'to be' (e.g., is, was, were) and the past participle of the main verb. It is often used in news reporting when the agent of the action is less important, unknown, or obvious.
Example from the text: "The decision was made with the safety of fans and staff as the top priority."
