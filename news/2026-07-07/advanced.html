📅 July 07, 2026 - Title: Zelensky's Urgent Appeal to Nato for Enhanced Air Defence Amidst Intensified Russian Strikes

Read the story

President Volodymyr Zelensky is poised to make a fervent appeal to Nato members for advanced air defence systems. This urgent request comes in the wake of relentless and intense Russian strikes targeting Ukrainian infrastructure and cities. The Ukrainian leader specifically intends to press Nato for additional interceptor missiles, deemed crucial for safeguarding civilian lives and strategic assets. He will leverage an upcoming Nato meeting in Turkey as a platform to articulate Ukraine's pressing security requirements. Kyiv has consistently highlighted the existential threat posed by persistent aerial assaults, necessitating robust countermeasures. The objective is to significantly enhance Ukraine's ability to defend its airspace and effectively intercept incoming projectiles. This diplomatic offensive underscores the ongoing escalation of hostilities and the critical need for international solidarity. For Zelensky, securing these vital assets is imperative to mitigate further damage and and prevent humanitarian catastrophe. The discussions at the summit are expected to focus on both immediate military aid and long-term strategies for bolstering Ukraine's defence capacity. Securing advanced air defence remains a cornerstone of Ukraine's broader strategy to repel aggression and protect its sovereignty.

Words and grammar

1. fervent appeal (noun phrase): An intense, passionate, and heartfelt request or plea.
2. escalation (noun): A rapid increase in intensity, seriousness, or scope, often referring to conflicts or crises.
3. bolstering (verb (present participle)): The act of supporting, strengthening, or reinforcing something that is weakening or needs improvement.
4. underscores (verb): Emphasizes, highlights, or draws particular attention to a fact, idea, or situation.
5. aerial assaults (noun phrase): Attacks conducted using aircraft or missiles from the air.
6. countermeasures (plural noun): actions intended to oppose or reduce another action or threat
7. humanitarian (adjective): Concerned with or seeking to promote human welfare, especially by providing aid and support to people in need.
8. sovereignty (noun): Supreme power or authority; the full right and power of a governing body over itself, without external interference.
9. aggression (noun): Hostile or violent behaviour or attitudes toward another; readiness to attack or confront.
10. imperative (adjective): urgently necessary or extremely important

Advanced Grammar: Nominalization
Nominalization is the process of forming a noun from another part of speech, such as a verb or adjective. It is frequently employed in formal English, especially in news reporting and academic writing, to create more concise and objective sentences by focusing on the event or concept rather than the actor or action. This stylistic choice enhances density and formality.
Example from the text: "This diplomatic offensive underscores the ongoing escalation of hostilities and the critical need for international solidarity."
