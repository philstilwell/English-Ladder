Keep durable private metadata/receipts, not transcript text. Stay quiet for unchanged, non-actionable results; report newly published lessons or a new blocker.

1. Run `node student-portal/codex-handoff-client.mjs nightly-discover student-portal/private/codex-nightly/queue.json`. This performs real Zoom discovery, including all search pages, delayed-note overlap, completed-meeting lookup and retained-cache cleanup. It performs no AI processing. Read the resulting private queue. It excludes ignored and completed jobs. If discovery fails partway through, call `queue` to recover the saved discovery reservation and any notes already found; retain its `discovery.recheckAt` and use the same single follow-up search. 2. For each eligible job, use `node student-portal/codex-handoff-client.mjs nightly-prepare <job-id> student-portal/private/codex-nightly/<job-id>-prepared.json`. Handle duplicate/skipped results without authoring. `waiting:true` gives an exact `retryAt`: try that job once more at or after that time, with waits no longer than 60 seconds. The server allows only an initial check and one delayed-source recheck, 15 minutes apart, per job per nightly date. Temporary Zoom lookup failures (`zoom_unavailable`) and a missing meeting reference (`meeting_reference_missing`) use this same single recheck. Follow-up discovery may supply the missing meeting details; it does not reset the number of checks. This survives restart and midnight. `deferred:true` means stop for this run. Failed matching, a required Zoom reconnection, paused/deleted accounts and oversized sources remain blocked. Never guess a date or a student. A missing meeting reference may be retried only at its returned `retryAt`; preparation still verifies the actual completed meeting and active student before authoring. Do not retry a `deferred:true` result just because the follow-up discovery ran. Known empty transcripts stay deferred until Zoom reports a source change. A failed recheck leaves the lesson queued, without an extra publisher or AI fallback. Do not use ordinary `prepare` to bypass readiness limits; it remains available for explicitly authorized catch-up or revalidating a genuinely changed source/history snapshot during an allowed author/revision cycle. After the discovery recheck, prepare newly found jobs under these same limits.
