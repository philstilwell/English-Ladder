📅 September 03, 2026 - A weather warning

Read the story

The UN has warned that El Niño could be unusually strong. This natural event begins in the Pacific Ocean and affects weather in other regions. Changes in winds allow warmer water to spread eastwards and release heat into the air. Some regions may face dry conditions, while others receive heavier rain and flooding. It could cause disruption to economies around the world. Countries are preparing by strengthening warning systems and arranging medical support. The WMO is working to share forecasts with communities that may be affected. These risks come on top of the effects of long-term global warming.

Words and grammar

1. release (verb): let something move out or become available
2. conditions (plural noun): the circumstances or physical state in a place
3. strengthening (verb): making something stronger or more effective
4. forecasts (plural noun): statements about what is likely to happen in the future
5. global warming (noun phrase): the long-term rise in average temperatures around the world
6. eastwards (adverb): towards the east
7. medical support (noun phrase): health care and supplies provided to people who need them
8. on top of (prepositional phrase): in addition to something that already exists

Grammar Focus: Could for possibility
Could cause describes a possible result. It does not say that the result is certain.
Example from the text: "It could cause disruption to economies around the world."
