Read the story

The Bank of England has kept interest rates the same. This is the sixth time in a row that rates have stayed steady. But interest rates are likely to rise in the future. This is because high energy prices may continue. A war in the Middle East has disrupted global energy supplies and caused high energy prices. The Bank of England uses interest rates to help control inflation. Inflation means that prices for many things are rising. If interest rates rise, borrowing money becomes more expensive. But people with savings accounts can get more money.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. kept (verb): did not change something; held it.
2. rates (noun): the amount of money banks charge you to borrow or pay you to save.
3. likely (adjective): probably true or going to happen.
4. energy prices (noun phrase): the cost of fuel like gas and electricity.
5. help control (verb phrase): to guide or limit something, but not always completely.
6. inflation (noun): when prices for many things go up.
7. borrowing (verb): taking money from a bank that you must pay back later.
8. savings (noun): money you keep in a bank, usually to use later.

Grammar Focus: If clauses for conditions and results
We use the word "if" to talk about a condition. The main part of the sentence shows the result of that condition. For example, 'If it rains, I take my umbrella.'
Example from the text: "If interest rates rise, borrowing money becomes more expensive."
