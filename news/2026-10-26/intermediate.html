AI Helps Farmers Care for Cows and Save Time

Read the story

George McMaster began his farming career by milking eight cows and checking their health by eye. Today, he monitors a herd of 300 cows through an AI camera at his farm near Islandmagee. The camera's software identifies each cow, analyses a short daily video and highlights changes that people may not yet see. The farmer says the camera helps identify cows with walking problems earlier than daily observation alone. The farmer believes healthier cows produce more milk, bringing benefits for both animals and farmers. Cows enter a robotic milking machine whenever they choose, instead of being gathered at fixed times. Better broadband has allowed the farmer to open a farm shop that accepts electronic payments. The farmer has used the FarmDrive app to run his business for four years. The app lets him enter information immediately, instead of writing it down in both the cattle building and the office. It also allows him to prepare reports for inspections, including details about animal medicines and feed. Each cow can have a profile that keeps all her information together, with the option of adding a photo. The developer sees AI as an adviser that helps farmers find useful information and work more efficiently. He believes farmers should remain responsible for decisions, rather than letting AI make those decisions for them.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. monitors (verb, present simple): Regularly watches or checks something to notice changes.
2. highlights (verb, present simple): Draws attention to something so that it is easier to notice.
3. observation (noun): The activity of watching carefully to learn about something.
4. immediately (adverb): At once, without waiting.
5. inspections (plural noun): Official checks to see whether rules and standards are being followed.
6. profile (noun): A page or section in an app containing information about one person or animal.
7. adviser (noun): Someone or something that offers information and suggestions to help with decisions.
8. efficiently (adverb): In a way that uses time and effort well, with little waste.

Grammar Focus: Present perfect with a period of time
Use the present perfect to describe an activity that began in the past and continues now: “The farmer has used the FarmDrive app to run his business for four years.” Form it with “has” or “have” plus the past participle, as in “has used”. Use “for” with a period of time, such as “for four years”.
Example from the text: "The farmer has used the FarmDrive app to run his business for four years."
