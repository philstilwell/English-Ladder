Trump Says Musk Restored Starlink Internet in Ukraine

Beginner · A1–A2

📅 October 23, 2026 - Trump Says Musk Restored Starlink Internet in Ukraine

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ukraine uses an internet system called Starlink. The military needs this fast internet to operate drones. Recently, the military had a problem with slow speeds. The slow internet made it hard to stop attacks. Donald Trump said that Elon Musk restored the access. Now, reporters say the system is working normally again.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. system (noun): A group of connected things that work together to do a job.
2. military (noun): The armed forces of a country, like the army and navy.
3. operate (verb): To control or use a machine or vehicle.
4. drones (noun): Small flying machines that do not have a pilot inside them.
5. attacks (noun): Violent actions meant to hurt people or damage places.
6. restored (verb): Brought something back to normal or made it work again.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that are finished. Regular past tense verbs end in "-ed", like "restored". Irregular verbs change their spelling, like "said" instead of "say".
Example from the text: "Donald Trump said that Elon Musk restored the access."
