📅 November 21, 2026 - New Hunger Games Stars Find Support in Each Other

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Joseph Zada, Mckenna Grace and Whitney Peak have the leading roles in the Hunger Games prequel Sunrise on the Reaping. A prequel tells an earlier part of a story, and this film is set 24 years before the original Hunger Games. Before the release, the three actors showed excitement and worry as they began a demanding series of press interviews. Zada, who plays Haymitch Abernathy, said the actors stayed close and made sure everyone felt supported. Grace is the most experienced of the three, with a career beginning in childhood that includes television series and recent Ghostbusters films. However, she said that joining the Hunger Games series was unlike anything she had experienced before. Peak said she and Zada turned to Grace when confused during film promotion, and Zada described Grace as a mentor. Zada plays a teenage version of Haymitch, the role played by Woody Harrelson in the original films. To develop his performance, Zada spent months studying Harrelson's acting in the first four Hunger Games films and taking notes. He used this research as a starting point while adding his own ideas to the role. Peak said filming could quickly become overwhelming, so the actors tried to keep the mood light between difficult scenes. Grace explained that sharing physically and emotionally difficult work helped the cast form close relationships.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. leading roles (plural noun phrase): The main acting parts in a film or play.
2. prequel (noun): A film or book about events that happen before those in an earlier film or book.
3. demanding (adjective): Needing a lot of effort, time or energy.
4. supported (adjective): Receiving help and encouragement from other people.
5. experienced (adjective): Having knowledge and skill from doing something before.
6. mentor (noun): A more experienced person who gives someone advice and help.
7. performance (noun): The way an actor plays a part in a film or show.
8. overwhelming (adjective): Causing feelings so strong that a situation becomes difficult to manage.

Grammar Focus: Relative clauses with “who” for extra information
Use “who” after a person to add information about them. Put commas around this information when the person is already identified. The main sentence remains complete if you remove the part beginning with “who”.
Example from the text: "Zada, who plays Haymitch Abernathy, said the actors stayed close and made sure everyone felt supported."
