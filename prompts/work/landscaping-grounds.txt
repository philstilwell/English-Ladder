Lesson 01: Confirming the booked grounds work
Original fictional case: Oak Court's work order includes front-lawn mowing and path leaf clearance. Resident asks for rear-hedge shaping. Hedges are excluded from today's booking; office can review a separate request, with no price or date confirmed.
Language workshop: Name what the visit includes
Communication goal: Clarify an extra task without dismissing the customer's request.
Language guidance: A recurring service does not automatically include every grounds task on every visit.
Useful frames (complete the gaps with case facts): Today's work order includes ___. / ___ is outside this visit's listed work. / Would you like the office to review ___ separately?
Vocabulary: work order: The job record stating the grounds work requested or agreed for a visit. / service area: The specified part of a property included in a grounds job. / exclusion: A task or location expressly left outside the agreed work. / recurring visit: A grounds-service appointment that repeats on an agreed schedule.
Speaking task: Prepare for two minutes. Speak for 45-60 seconds using the case facts, then respond to your partner's question. Switch roles and repeat without reading the model response.
Partner: Resident: you assumed a regular grounds visit included the hedge; ask for a separate review.
Guided response task: Complete a workplace dialogue by selecting one of four supplied options. Compare the feedback and say the completed exchange aloud. Do not write an open-ended message.
Optional second-round challenge: The resident asks whether requesting the extra task guarantees it today. Distinguish request from booking.
Model for comparison AFTER my attempt, not a response to give me first: Today's work order covers the front lawn and leaf clearance from the path. Rear-hedge shaping isn't included in this visit. Is that an additional request you would like the office to review? I'll keep the new hedge request separate from the booked work so we don't imply that its price or timing has already been agreed.
