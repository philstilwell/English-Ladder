Read the story

Iceland had a special event. People voted about joining the EU. The EU is the European Union. Many countries are members of the EU. The government wanted to restart talks. But most people said "no" to this. About 52.8% of voters rejected the idea. They voted against resuming discussions. A broadcaster called RUV reported this news. So, Iceland will not talk about joining the EU now.

Words and grammar

1. voted (verb): To choose something in an election or meeting by casting a ballot.
2. joining (verb): Becoming a member of a group or organization, like a club or a union.
3. EU (noun): The European Union, a group of many European countries that work together.
4. rejected (verb): To refuse to accept something; to say 'no' to an idea or a proposal.
5. restart (verb): To begin something again after it has stopped, like starting a game over.

Grammar Focus: The Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. For regular verbs, we often add '-ed' to the base form. For irregular verbs, the form changes completely. It tells us something happened at a specific time before now.
Example from the text: "About 52.8% of voters rejected the idea."
