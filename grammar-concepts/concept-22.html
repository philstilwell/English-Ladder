Measurements and shapes

Describe size with an appropriate unit and pattern.

Level ranges are study guidance. Start with the examples and choose your own pace.

Try the practice →Learner workbook PDFTeaching guide PDF

The cable is three meters long.

The measurement describes the cable.

BEFORE THE NOUN

It is a three-meter cable.

The unit stays singular in this compound adjective.

SHAPE

The table is rectangular.

Rectangular describes shape, not size.

Understand

The useful distinction

Use an adjective after the measurement: The table is two meters long. Use a noun with of: a length of two meters. In a compound adjective before a noun, the unit is singular: a two-meter table.

Choose the dimension you mean: long for length, wide for width, high or tall for height, and deep for depth. Tall is common for people and upright objects. Use square, round, or rectangular for shape. Always include the unit when a number alone is unclear.
