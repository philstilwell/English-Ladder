Read the story

Ukraine's leader, Mr. Zelensky, went to Washington. He wants to talk with Mr. Trump. Mr. Zelensky hopes to get more air defences. These are systems to protect Ukraine from missiles. Russia has escalated its attacks on Ukraine. These attacks have been very dangerous and caused deadly attacks. Ukraine needs strong commitments from the US. These commitments mean the US promises to help. Mr. Zelensky wants to make sure the help is firm. So, Ukraine can protect its people better.

Words and grammar

1. leader (noun): A person who guides or commands a group.
2. air defences (noun phrase): Systems that protect a country from attacks by planes or missiles in the sky.
3. commitments (noun): Strong promises to do something or give something.
4. escalated (verb (past simple)): Became more intense, larger, or more serious.
5. deadly attacks (noun phrase): Violent actions that can cause death or serious harm.

Grammar Focus: Simple Present Tense for Wants and Hopes
We use the Simple Present tense to talk about things we want or hope for. It shows a desire or intention. For example, 'He wants help' or 'She hopes to visit.' Remember to add '-s' for 'he', 'she', 'it' forms, like 'wants' or 'hopes'.
Example from the text: "He wants to talk with Mr. Trump."
