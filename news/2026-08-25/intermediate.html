📅 August 25, 2026 - Iran 'Fully Prepared' to Counter New US Economic Sanctions

Read the story

New and widened economic sanctions have recently been announced against Iran by the United States. These measures are intended to significantly increase pressure on the Iranian economy. US officials have dramatically described these as an "economic D-Day", indicating their severity. However, Iran's government has declared itself fully prepared to counter these new financial restrictions. Tehran claims it possesses a specific plan to address the impact of the increased pressure effectively. The Iranian leadership asserts that these punitive actions will not achieve their intended effect. They believe their strategies will help mitigate any severe economic damage to the country. This ongoing tension highlights the complex relationship between the two nations. Both countries continue to pursue their respective national interests through various means. The international community is watching closely to see how these latest developments unfold.

Words and grammar

1. widened (adjective): Made larger or more extensive in scope or area.
2. unfold (verb): To develop or happen over a period of time; to become clear or revealed.
3. mitigate (verb): To make something less severe, serious, or painful; to alleviate or soften the impact of an adverse condition.
4. significantly (adverb): to a degree that is important or noticeable
5. restrictions (plural noun): rules that limit what people can do
6. officials (noun): People who hold a position of authority or trust in an organization or government.
7. declared (verb): Stated something officially, publicly, or strongly.
8. increase (verb): to make something greater in amount, strength or level

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself and the object that receives the action, rather than the person or thing performing the action. It is formed using a form of 'to be' (e.g., is, are, was, were, has been) followed by the past participle of the main verb. This structure is useful when the doer is unknown, unimportant, or when you want to emphasize the action or its receiver.
Example from the text: "New and widened economic sanctions have recently been announced against Iran by the United States."
