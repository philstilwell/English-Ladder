📅 September 06, 2026 [0 days, 12 hours old] - Weather and air travel
Could changing weather mean more problems for travelers?

Intermediate ESL: Weather and travel

Before you read

Read the title. What do you think the story is about?

Read the story

Air travel faces hotter conditions and stormier skies as the climate changes. The report asks whether passengers will increasingly have to accept disruption. That disruption may include delays. Some planes might need to change their routes.

Words and grammar

1. disruption (noun): a problem that interrupts normal activity
2. delays (plural noun): times when something happens later than planned
3. routes (plural noun): the ways used to travel from one place to another

Grammar Focus: Might for possibility
Might need to means a change is possible. It does not say every plane will change its route.
Example from the text: "Some planes might need to change their routes."

Intermediate ESL: Courts · mentions deaths of children

Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Before you read

Read the title. What do you think the story is about?

Read the story

A mistrial has been declared in the Lindsay Clancy murder case after the jury could not agree. The case concerns the deaths of her three children. The supplied summary leaves open whether she will be held criminally responsible. The legal outcome remains uncertain.

Words and grammar

1. mistrial (noun): a trial that ends without a valid final decision
2. jury (noun): a group of people who decide facts in a trial
3. uncertain (adjective): not known or not decided

Grammar Focus: Remain + adjective
Remains uncertain means continues to be uncertain. It describes the current state, not a future verdict.
Example from the text: "The legal outcome remains uncertain."

📅 September 04, 2026 [2 days, 12 hours old] - A series of suspected attacks
Read carefully when responsibility is uncertain.

Intermediate ESL: International affairs · suspected attacks

Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Before you read

Read the title. What do you think the story is about?

Read the story

The report describes a growing series of suspected attacks in Europe. Germany has blamed Russia for an attack on Leipzig airport. Other suspicious incidents have occurred elsewhere. Russia is the main suspect in the report.

Words and grammar

1. blamed (verb): said that someone was responsible
2. suspicious (adjective): making people think something may be wrong
3. suspect (noun): a person or party thought to be responsible

Grammar Focus: A claim with a source
In the report limits the statement to what this source says. Suspect does not mean proven responsible.
Example from the text: "Russia is the main suspect in the report."

Read the story

Rescuers in Nepal are trying to reach workers trapped at a hydropower site. They have used explosives in their effort to get through the hillside. The saved report describes a difficult rescue after flooding. Families are waiting for news.

Words and grammar

1. rescuers (plural noun): people who try to bring others to safety
2. trapped (adjective): unable to get out
3. waiting (verb): staying until something happens

Grammar Focus: An action happening now
Are waiting describes an activity in progress. It does not tell us when the wait will end.
Example from the text: "Families are waiting for news."
