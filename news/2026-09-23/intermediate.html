📅 September 23, 2026 - Pornhub's Age Checks Face Scrutiny from UK Regulator

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ofcom has launched an investigation into Aylo, Pornhub's owner, to see if the site's new age checks effectively prevent children from seeing adult material. The government body stated that Pornhub began using a new age verification process for some users, relying on Apple as a third party. Ofcom expressed concerns that Aylo might not have completed enough due diligence and testing before putting this new process into action, questioning its effectiveness. In a detailed statement, Aylo strongly defended its age verification method and promised full cooperation with the inquiry. Under the UK's Online Safety Act, websites hosting adult material must use highly effective age assurance to confirm users are over 18. As part of its investigation, Ofcom will examine how Aylo applies its age verification processes and if it carried out the necessary due diligence before implementation. However, Aylo claims that after trying various age-check methods in different markets, Apple's approach offers much stronger protection than any other currently available in the UK. The Online Safety Act gives Ofcom the authority to impose penalties on companies that don't comply, potentially up to £18 million or 10% of their annual global income.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. launched (verb): started something, often a large or important activity or project.
2. inquiry (noun): an official process to find out the truth about something.
3. prevent (verb): to stop something from happening or to stop someone from doing something.
4. effectively (adverb): in a way that produces a desired or intended result.
5. due diligence (noun phrase): the careful research or actions someone must do before entering an agreement or making an important decision.
6. defended (verb): protected someone or something from attack, or explained why something was right when others disagreed.
7. cooperation (noun): the act of working together with someone or doing what they ask to achieve a shared goal.
8. assurance (noun): a statement or promise that something will certainly happen or is true; confidence in something.
9. third party (noun phrase): a person or group involved in a situation in addition to the two main ones.
10. impose (verb): to force a rule, tax, punishment, or burden on someone or something.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Simple
The Present Perfect Simple is used to talk about actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past but have a connection to the present, or for actions that started in the past and continue now. It is formed with "have" or "has" followed by the past participle of the main verb. For example, in "Ofcom has launched an investigation," the launching happened in the past, but the investigation is ongoing or its results are relevant now.
Example from the text: "Ofcom has launched an investigation into Aylo, Pornhub's owner, to see if the site's new age checks effectively prevent children from seeing adult material."
