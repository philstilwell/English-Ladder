📅 November 18, 2026 - Waymo Stops US Freeway Taxi Trips for Now

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Waymo has stopped its driverless taxis from using US freeways for now. Driverless taxis carry passengers without a human driver. Freeways are large roads for fast traffic. California banned the taxis from its freeways after several traffic problems. Some Waymo cars entered areas where people were working on the road. California officials said the cars were a danger to people in these areas. Waymo said there were no reports of anyone being hurt in the incident near San Francisco. Waymo said it was working with state officials to start freeway trips again. The taxis can still carry passengers on normal city streets.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. driverless (adjective): Without a human driver.
2. passengers (plural noun): People who travel in a car, bus or train but do not drive it.
3. freeways (plural noun): Large roads for fast traffic.
4. banned (verb, past tense): Said officially that something was not allowed.
5. entered (verb, past tense): Went into a place.
6. again (adverb): One more time.

Grammar Focus: “Can” for permission
Use “can” to say that someone is allowed to do something. Put the verb after “can” without “to”. Do not add “-s” or “-ing” to that verb. This pattern stays the same with one taxi or several taxis.
Example from the text: "The taxis can still carry passengers on normal city streets."
