A trial ends without a decision

📅 September 05, 2026 - A trial ends without a decision
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

A judge has declared a mistrial in the Lindsay Clancy murder case. The jury could not agree after discussing the case for a week. The case concerns the deaths of her three children. Her lawyer tried to stop the mistrial, but the attempt failed. Another court meeting is planned for later in the month. The legal outcome remains uncertain.

Words and grammar

1. mistrial (noun): a trial that ends without a valid final decision
2. jury (noun): a group of people who decide facts in a trial
3. uncertain (adjective): not known or not decided
4. judge (noun): a person who leads a court and makes legal decisions
5. lawyer (noun): a person trained to help with legal matters
6. attempt (noun): an effort to do something

Grammar Focus: Remain + adjective
“Remains uncertain” means continues to be uncertain. It describes the current state, not a future verdict.
Example from the text: "The legal outcome remains uncertain."
