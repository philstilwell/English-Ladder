📅 October 17, 2026 - Brazil Amazon Deforestation Drops Ahead of Presidential Election
Brazil reports a record drop in Amazon deforestation before a major presidential run-off.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Deforestation rates in the country's Amazon and savanna regions have fallen to record lows. President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva pledged to end deforestation in the Amazon when he assumed office. Satellite data shows that the deforestation rate fell by 41.8%, which is the biggest drop since 1988. During his first year, Lula tightened up environmental monitoring and penalties for logging. During the previous presidency of Jair Bolsonaro, the region lost nearly 60% more in forest lands. The former president had promoted mining in indigenous lands, and forest clearances soared. Lula will face Bolsonaro's son Flavio in a presidential run-off on 25 October. Tropical forests absorb vast quantities of carbon dioxide to help keep the Earth cool. An expert said this achievement shows that it is possible to curb greenhouse gas emissions with enough political will.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. pledged (verb): Formally promised to do or provide something.
2. assumed office (phrase): Officially started a leadership position, such as a president or governor.
3. monitoring (noun): The act of observing and checking the progress or quality of something over time.
4. penalties (noun): Punishments given for breaking a rule, law, or agreement.
5. indigenous (adjective): Belonging to a place from the earliest known times, rather than arriving from somewhere else.
6. soared (verb): Increased rapidly to a high level.
7. run-off (noun): A final election held to decide the winner between the top candidates.
8. curb (verb): To control or limit something that is harmful or unwanted.

Grammar Focus: Non-defining relative clauses
We use non-defining relative clauses with "which" to add extra information about a specific noun or an entire situation. They are separated from the main sentence by commas. In the example, ", which is the biggest drop since 1988" adds extra historical context about the 41.8% fall.
Example from the text: "Satellite data shows that the deforestation rate fell by 41.8%, which is the biggest drop since 1988."

📅 October 16, 2026 - Record High Ship Strikes Slow Oil Recovery Near Hormuz
The number of vessels struck near the Strait of Hormuz has reached a record high, slowing regional oil shipments.
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

A recent analysis of maritime data shows that strikes on vessels near the Strait of Hormuz have reached a record high. Twelve vessels were targeted in just one week, which is the highest number reported since the current conflict began. As a result of these frequent incidents, the recovery of oil shipments traveling through this important waterway has slowed down. According to analysts, Iran is attempting to assert greater authority over the waterway while the US military escorts commercial traffic. Many Gulf Arab states have been using a shuttle system, where smaller fleets carry cargo through the strait before transferring it to larger ships. However, Iran claims that these smaller fleets are using an unauthorised route along the coast of Oman. Furthermore, Iranian forces have started widening their operations by targeting ships near the coast of Gulf Arab states. A risk analyst explained that the primary aim of these operations is to stop the ships and to deter others. Despite these significant challenges, the US military insists that commercial trade continues to flow with coordinated protection.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. vessels (noun): Large ships or boats used for carrying people or goods across the sea.
2. targeted (verb): Chosen and attacked by a weapon or hostile force.
3. incidents (noun): Unexpected events that are often dangerous, violent, or cause problems.
4. assert (verb): To behave in a strong and confident way so that people recognize your power or authority.
5. escorts (verb): Travels with another vehicle or person to protect them or provide security.
6. unauthorised (adjective): Done or used without official permission or legal approval.
7. widening (verb): Increasing the size or range of an activity so it covers a larger area.
8. deter (verb): To prevent or discourage someone from doing something by making them afraid of the consequences.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect for Recent Changes
We use the present perfect tense (have or has + past participle) to describe a recent action that has an important result in the present. In this news story, “have reached” shows that the number of strikes recently increased, and this record-high number still affects the shipping industry right now.
Example from the text: "A recent analysis of maritime data shows that strikes on vessels near the Strait of Hormuz have reached a record high."

Campaigners argue that the centres could undermine climate targets and put more pressure on renewable electricity supplies.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. behind the scenes (adverbial phrase): Out of public view; here, doing the computing that makes online services work.
2. massive scale (noun phrase): on a very large size or extent, much larger than usual.
3. explosion (noun): a sudden and rapid increase in something, like a quick growth.
4. scrambling (verb): moving or trying to do something quickly and with difficulty, often in a hurry.
5. hyperscale (adjective): describing something, especially a data centre, that is extremely large in size and capacity.
6. vital (adjective): extremely important or necessary for something to exist or be successful.
7. moratorium (noun): a temporary ban or stopping of an activity, usually by official order.
8. undermine (verb): to make something weaker or less effective, often gradually or secretly.

Reading: Major bridges in Kyiv have been affected by Russian drone strikes for four consecutive days. Two people were injured after the Pivnichnyi Bridge was struck on Sunday morning. The latest assault took place during German Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s unexpected visit to Kyiv. Russia's military announced it would increase attacks on Kyiv and other Ukrainian regions. Video of Sunday’s incident showed a drone crashing into Pivnichnyi Bridge, causing an explosion as cars crossed it. Other videos showed a noticeable hole in a traffic lane on the bridge. The two impacted bridges are still standing, but damage has caused significant traffic problems in parts of the capital. While emergency services worked on the incidents, Germany's Merz held a meeting with President Zelensky in Kyiv.

Vocabulary and grammar: Words and grammar 1. affected (verb): To have an influence on someone or something, often causing a change or impact. 2. strikes (noun): Attacks, especially military ones, that involve hitting a target with force. 3. consecutive (adjective): Following one after another without interruption. 4. assault (noun): A strong verbal or physical attack on someone or something. 5. unexpected (adjective): Not anticipated or predicted; happening suddenly without prior warning. 6. increase (verb): To become greater in amount, number, or degree; to make something larger. 7. incident (noun): An event or occurrence, often one that is unpleasant or unusual. 8. explosion (noun): A sudden burst of energy or force, often with a loud noise, causing destruction. 9. noticeable (adjective): Easily seen or observed; prominent enough to attract attention. 10. significant (adjective): Sufficiently great or important to be worthy of attention; noteworthy. Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice The passive uses a form of “be” + a past participle. Here, “have been affected” puts the bridges first because the report focuses on what happened to them. The “by” phrase still identifies the cause. Example from the text: "Major bridges in Kyiv have been affected by Russian drone strikes for four consecutive days."
