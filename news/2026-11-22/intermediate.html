📅 November 22, 2026 - Fossil Fuel Dispute Blocks COP31 Climate Agreement

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Negotiations, or formal discussions, at the COP31 climate meeting in Turkey continued more than a day beyond the planned finish. Countries had still not agreed on a global climate agreement, so the discussions were suspended, or stopped temporarily, for the night. The EU and Pacific island nations opposed a draft agreement, an unfinished version, because it did not mention reducing fossil fuel demand. Ministers from several countries and the EU wanted the final agreement to include a commitment to move away from oil, gas and coal. Fossil fuel producers, led by Saudi Arabia, had pushed to remove that commitment from the agreement. The Africa Group wanted commitments, or firm promises, to provide more money for adaptation, which means preparing for climate change's effects. The EU had previously said it would not support this request for more adaptation money. An earlier draft included a target of $120 billion for adaptation by 2035, but this amount was later removed. Some teams were moving their Sunday flights to Monday, suggesting that an agreement was still some distance away. If countries do not reach consensus, meaning agreement among all participants, the negotiations could be suspended. In that case, the meeting would end without an agreement and continue next year. However, the UK's chief negotiator remained hopeful and said her team was still trying to reach an agreement. Another risk was that too many teams might leave, leaving too few countries to approve a global agreement.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. negotiations (plural noun): Formal discussions in which people or groups try to reach an agreement.
2. suspended (verb, past participle): Stopped temporarily, with the possibility of continuing later.
3. draft agreement (noun phrase): An unfinished version of an agreement that people can still discuss and change.
4. demand (noun): The amount of something that people need or want to use.
5. move away from (phrasal verb): Gradually stop using or depending on something.
6. commitments (plural noun): Firm promises to take particular actions.
7. adaptation (noun): Changes that help people prepare for and manage the effects of climate change.
8. consensus (noun): Agreement among all the people or groups taking part in a discussion.

Grammar Focus: First conditional with “could”
Use “if” with the present simple to describe a possible future condition. Use “could” with the base form of a verb in the main clause to show a possible result. This pattern presents the result as possible rather than certain.
Example from the text: "If countries do not reach consensus, meaning agreement among all participants, the negotiations could be suspended."
