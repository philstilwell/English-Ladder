Read the story

The United States has recently launched several heavy military strikes on Iran. These actions came after an attempted attack on American troops, escalating tensions in the region. Prior to this, there had been a short period of relative calm, creating hope for de-escalation. During that time, hostilities were paused, indicating a temporary halt in the conflict between the two nations. However, this lull has now ended, with both sides resuming their aggressive postures. Reports confirm that both the US and Iran have restarted missile attacks, making the situation more volatile. The US justified its strikes as a necessary defensive measure following the threat to its personnel. International bodies are expressing concern about the renewed conflict and its potential impact on regional stability. This rapid return to conflict follows a brief moment where de-escalation seemed possible. The world is closely observing these developments, hoping for a peaceful resolution despite the recent events.

Words and grammar

1. launched (verb): To start or set in motion something, often a large-scale project or an attack.
2. heavy (adjective): Referring to something intense, significant, or powerful in its impact, like a strong attack.
3. strikes (noun): Military attacks, often by air, against a specific target.
4. short period of relative calm (phrase): A brief time when a difficult situation becomes less active or intense, suggesting a temporary peace.
5. hostilities were paused (phrase): The fighting or aggressive actions between two groups stopped temporarily.

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is a grammatical construction used when the focus is on the action or the result of the action, rather than on the agent (who or what performs the action). It is formed using a form of the verb 'to be' (e.g., is, are, was, were) followed by the past participle of the main verb. It's often used in news to report events objectively without naming the specific actor, or when the actor is less important or unknown.
Example from the text: "hostilities were paused"
