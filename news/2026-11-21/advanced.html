New Hunger Games Cast Confront Pressure Through Mutual Support

📅 November 21, 2026 - New Hunger Games Cast Confront Pressure Through Mutual Support

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Sunrise on the Reaping places Joseph Zada, Mckenna Grace and Whitney Peak at the centre of the latest Hunger Games instalment. Set 24 years before the original film, it adapts Suzanne Collins’ 2025 novel of the same name. The sixth film joins a series whose worldwide earnings have exceeded $3.3 billion since 2012. Beginning a gruelling press run, the trio showed a mixture of anticipation, anxiety and excitement surrounding the release. Grace brings substantial screen experience, including appearances in The Handmaid’s Tale and two recent Ghostbusters films. Nevertheless, she described joining the franchise as deeply unsettling and unlike anything her previous work had prepared her for. Peak joked that she and Zada repeatedly sought out Grace when they felt stuck or confused while promoting the film, while Zada described her as a mentor. Zada plays the teenage Haymitch, a District 12 participant portrayed by Woody Harrelson in the original films. Reviews have been generally positive, with several outlets praising Zada while acknowledging the difficulty of stepping into Harrelson's shoes. Having spent months studying Harrelson’s earlier portrayals, Zada used his research as a foundation for his own interpretation. When they met halfway through filming, Harrelson recommended Cool Hand Luke, which Zada said helped ground his portrayal, and Harold and Maud, which he described as an enjoyable watch. Jesse Plemons regarded Zada’s role as a substantial undertaking and expressed admiration for his stamina and composure. An ensemble cast including Ralph Fiennes, Maya Hawke, Kieran Culkin and Elle Fanning returns audiences to a brutal dystopian world during the fiftieth Hunger Games. Within that fictional society, the ruling Capitol organises annual games involving Panem’s twelve districts as punishment for rebellion. Peak described the work as intensely stressful at times, making lighter moments between difficult scenes particularly valuable. Grace linked the cast’s close bonds to the days they spent sharing physically strenuous and emotionally intense work.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. gruelling (adjective): Requiring prolonged, exhausting physical or mental effort.
2. anticipation (noun): An expectant feeling about an approaching event, often involving excitement or uncertainty.
3. sought out (phrasal verb, past simple): Deliberately approached a particular person to obtain help, advice or reassurance.
4. stepping into Harrelson's shoes (idiomatic verb phrase, -ing form): Taking over a role associated with Harrelson, with the expectations that come from succeeding an established actor.
5. interpretation (noun): An actor’s particular understanding and presentation of a role through creative choices.
6. ground (verb, base form): To give an artistic portrayal a convincing basis that makes it feel believable.
7. undertaking (noun): A substantial task or responsibility requiring considerable effort and commitment.
8. stamina (noun): The capacity to sustain physical or mental effort over an extended period.
9. ensemble cast (noun phrase): A group of actors whose collective participation is a significant feature of a production.
10. dystopian (adjective): Depicting an imagined society characterised by oppression, suffering or severely restricted freedom.

Advanced Grammar: Perfect participle clauses with a shared subject
Use “having” followed by a past participle to present an action as completed before the main action. The understood subject of the participle clause must match the main-clause subject; here, Zada both studied the portrayals and used the research.
Example from the text: "Having spent months studying Harrelson’s earlier portrayals, Zada used his research as a foundation for his own interpretation."
