Anthropic Prepares for Major Stock Market Launch

Intermediate · B1–B2

📅 November 19, 2026 - Anthropic Prepares for Major Stock Market Launch

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Anthropic is valued at up to $1.5 trillion ahead of its planned stock market launch on Thursday. Anthropic makes the artificial intelligence (AI) chatbot Claude and plans to price its shares at $83 each on Nasdaq, a New York stock exchange. This will make Anthropic the first US company focused entirely on AI to go public, or offer shares on the stock market. The report says demand was more than ten times the number of shares available before the public listing. If demand is as high as reported, Anthropic's market value could rise above its target when trading begins. Anthropic expects to raise a total of $94 billion through its stock market launch and additional private investments. However, some investors are concerned about whether the company can generate revenue, or income from its business, and make profits. Financial analyst Yang Wang questioned whether Anthropic's business could keep pace with large payments due over the next two years. The company has made safety a central part of its approach to developing AI. It has called for slower AI development, warning that advanced systems could cause serious harm without suitable safeguards, or safety measures. Despite these concerns, Anthropic wants to expand quickly and gain access to more money through the public markets. There are also concerns about the wider AI industry's business model, which depends on expensive computer chips that are in short supply.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. valued at (verb phrase, past participle form): Estimated to have a particular financial value.
2. stock exchange (noun): A market where people and businesses buy and sell company shares.
3. go public (verb phrase): To offer a company's shares for people to buy on the stock market.
4. demand (uncountable noun): The amount of something that people want to buy.
5. raise (verb): To obtain money for a business or purpose.
6. revenue (uncountable noun): Money a business receives from its activities before costs are taken away.
7. keep pace (verb phrase): To develop quickly enough to meet changing needs or demands.
8. safeguards (plural noun): Measures intended to prevent harm or reduce risks.
9. in short supply (adjective phrase): Not available in large enough amounts to meet people's needs.

Grammar Focus: Conditionals with “could” for possible future results
Use “if” plus the present simple to describe a possible future condition. In the result clause, use “could” plus the base form of a verb to show a possible result rather than a certain prediction.
Example from the text: "If demand is as high as reported, Anthropic's market value could rise above its target when trading begins."
