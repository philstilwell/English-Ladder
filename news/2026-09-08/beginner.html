📅 September 08, 2026 - Nigella Lawson Joins Bake Off as New Judge

Read the story

Nigella Lawson is a new judge on The Great British Bake Off. Many critics praised her new role. The show returns to TV later this month. She is taking over from Dame Prue Leith. New contestants are confirmed for the 17th series. A doctor and a hairdresser are among the contestants.

Words and grammar

1. judge (noun): A person who decides who wins in a competition or show.
2. praised (verb, past simple): Said very good things about someone or something.
3. returns (verb, simple present): Comes back again to a place or screen.
4. taking over (verb phrase): Starting a new job or role after someone else leaves.
5. contestants (noun): People who take part in a competition or a game.
6. confirmed (verb, past participle): Made sure that something is true or settled.

Grammar Focus: Simple Present Tense (Facts)
We use the simple present tense to talk about facts or things that are generally true. The verb form stays the same for 'I', 'you', 'we', 'they', but we add “-s” or “-es” for 'he', 'she', or 'it'.
Example from the text: "Nigella Lawson is a new judge on The Great British Bake Off."
