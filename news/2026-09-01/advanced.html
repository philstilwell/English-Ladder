Read the story

The report concerns people forced to return to Afghanistan from Pakistan and Iran. The headline frames the experience as having to start again under the Taliban. Its quoted statement challenges the assumption that everyone returning has previously lived there. Some people have never been to Afghanistan.

Words and grammar

1. forced (verb): made to do something they did not choose
2. return (verb): go back to a place
3. never (adverb): not at any time

Advanced Grammar: Have never been
Have never been describes an experience that has not happened before now. Never means not at any time.
Example from the text: "Some people have never been to Afghanistan."
