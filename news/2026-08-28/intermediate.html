Read the story

Recent preliminary investigations by scientists have revealed the likely cause of the severe floods. These initial studies have pointed to a collapsed glacier as the main reason for the disaster. The event resulted in devastating floods across certain areas of Nepal and Tibet. This incident clearly highlights the concerning issue of rapidly melting ice in the Himalayan region. Experts are increasingly worried about how climate change impacts these crucial ice formations. The sudden influx of water caused significant damage to infrastructure and local communities. Such events demonstrate the urgent need for better disaster preparedness in vulnerable mountain regions. Scientists believe warmer temperatures contribute significantly to glacial instability. Understanding these geological processes is essential for mitigating future risks to human settlements. The global scientific community is calling for more attention to the environmental changes occurring in high-altitude areas.

Words and grammar

1. preliminary investigations (noun phrase): Initial studies or examinations done to discover facts or details about something before a full analysis.
2. pointed to (phrasal verb): Indicated or suggested something as the cause, reason, or evidence for a situation.
3. collapsed glacier (noun phrase): A large mass of ice that has suddenly broken apart, fallen down, or lost its structural integrity.
4. devastating floods (noun phrase): Extremely destructive and damaging overflows of water that cause widespread harm.
5. rapidly melting (adverb + verb): Dissolving or turning into liquid very quickly, often due to increased temperatures.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Tense
The Present Perfect tense connects past actions to the present moment. It describes an action that started in the past and continues to the present, or a past action whose result is relevant now. It is formed with 'has/have' followed by the past participle of the main verb.
Example from the text: "Recent preliminary investigations by scientists have revealed the likely cause of the severe floods."
