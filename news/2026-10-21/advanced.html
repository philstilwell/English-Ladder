Russia Releases American Without Conditions Amid Diplomatic Tensions

📅 October 21, 2026 - Russia Releases American Without Conditions Amid Diplomatic Tensions

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Russia has released a 74-year-old American who was jailed for purportedly fighting on behalf of Ukraine. The Russian president confirmed during an early Wednesday meeting that Stephen Hubbard had been transported to Pulkovo Airport. Donald Trump stated that the release followed a direct request he made to the Russian leader last week. He added that the release did not require any reciprocal concessions from the United States. American news outlets reported that the elderly man was in poor health and expected to be flown to Texas for medical care. Trump defended his move to pause impending sanctions against Russia, arguing that enforcing them would have brought diplomatic discussions to a complete halt. Steve Witkoff thanked Putin for a deal struck with Washington earlier this month to import Russian diesel. This diesel agreement infuriated Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, triggering a tumultuous period of deteriorating relations. Witkoff also urged Putin to consider resuming trilateral peace talks, seeking an end to a conflict that grinds on into its fifth year. Meanwhile, Kyiv accused Washington of withholding vital intelligence, a claim the US firmly rejected as scandalous. However, Zelensky confirmed that intelligence sharing had resumed after constructive talks with American officials in Oslo.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. purportedly (adverb): As appears or is stated to be true, though not definitively proven.
2. reciprocal (adjective): Given, felt, or done in return; involving mutual exchange.
3. impending (adjective): About to happen; looming or fast approaching.
4. infuriated (verb): Made someone extremely angry and impatient.
5. tumultuous (adjective): Characterised by intense disorder, turbulence, or confusion.
6. deteriorating (adjective): Becoming progressively worse over a period of time.
7. trilateral (adjective): Involving three separate parties, countries, or groups.
8. grinds on (phrasal verb): Continues for a long time in a slow, tedious, and destructive way.
9. scandalous (adjective): Causing outrage by a perceived offence against morality or established norms.
10. constructive (adjective): Serving a useful purpose; tending to build up or improve relations.

Advanced Grammar: Participial phrases expressing result
A present participle like "triggering" can introduce a phrase that describes the direct consequence of the action in the main clause. This structure offers a sophisticated way to link a cause to its immediate effect without starting a new sentence.
Example from the text: "This diesel agreement infuriated Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, triggering a tumultuous period of deteriorating relations."
