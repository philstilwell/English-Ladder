Time and verbs · A2–B1 · 10–15 minutes

Past events and background actions

Put a finished event in its time context.

Level ranges are study guidance. Start with the examples and choose your own pace.

Try the practice →Learner workbook PDFTeaching guide PDF

EVENT

The bus arrived at six.

A completed event.

BACKGROUND

I was waiting at the stop.

An activity in progress.

TOGETHER

I was waiting when the bus arrived.

The arrival happened during the wait.

Understand

The useful distinction

The past simple presents a finished event or a past state: I arrived at six; the room was quiet. The past continuous presents an activity in progress around a past time: I was waiting at six.

Combine them to show background and an event: I was cooking when the phone rang. This does not always mean the first activity stopped. Add then, before, or after when the order needs to be explicit.

Try it

Check your understanding

Write an answer, then compare it with the explanation. Other wording may also be correct. These responses are for self-checking and are not automatically graded.

1. Choose: At eight yesterday, I ___ dinner. (was cooking / cook)

Compare your answer

was cooking

An activity was in progress at a past time.

2. Which happened during the walk: I was walking when it began to rain?

Compare your answer

It began to rain.

The walk is the background activity.

3. Does was reading when the phone rang prove the reading stopped?

Compare your answer

No. The sentence does not state that.

The reader may have continued.

Use it

Share your ideas

Write two sentences about a past event and its background.

See one possible response

I was working at home yesterday. A neighbor knocked on the door.

Self-check: Does your sentence express your intended meaning? Does its form follow the relevant pattern? Can a reader understand the context?
