Wild Dogs Travel Far to Find Mates

Beginner · A1–A2

📅 September 14, 2026 - Wild Dogs Travel Far to Find Mates

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

A pack of African wild dogs completed a record-breaking journey. They traveled nearly 2,500 miles across Africa. Scientists say this offers hope for wildlife. Three wild dog brothers made this journey across Zambia. They avoided cities and other human areas. African wild dogs are very rare predators. They need to find mates outside their own group. These journeys help connect different wild dog populations.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. pack (noun): A group of wild animals, like dogs, that live and hunt together.
2. completed (verb): Finished doing something; brought to an end.
3. journey (noun): A trip, or traveling from one place to another, usually over a long distance.
4. hope (noun): A feeling that something good will happen in the future.
5. wildlife (noun): Wild animals, birds, and plants that live in nature and are not tamed by humans.
6. avoided (verb): Kept away from something or someone; stayed out of the way or did not go near.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense for completed actions
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. For regular verbs, we often add "-ed" to the base form. For irregular verbs, the word changes completely.
Example from the text: "A pack of African wild dogs completed a record-breaking journey."
