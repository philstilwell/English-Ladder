US Moves Bombers from UK Base After Threat

Beginner · A1–A2

📅 October 06, 2026 - US Moves Bombers from UK Base After Threat

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The US moved its bombers from England. President Trump said this was because of 'some threats'. The Pentagon confirmed the bombers went back to America. Mr Trump said they moved the planes to avoid danger. Police arrested several men during an investigation linked to the base. Police were investigating an incident linked to RAF Fairford, the base in England. US official Marco Rubio said he still trusted the security of US bases. The arrested men were later released on bail.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. moved (verb): To change the position of something.
2. bombers (noun): Large military aircraft that carry bombs.
3. threats (noun): Possible dangers or bad things that might happen.
4. Pentagon (proper noun): The US government department responsible for the armed forces; also the name of its main building.
5. confirmed (verb): Said that something was true or definite.
6. danger (noun): The possibility of something bad happening, like harm or injury.
7. arrested (verb): Police caught and held someone because they thought they did something wrong.
8. incident (noun): An event or happening, often something unpleasant.
9. bail (noun): Permission for an arrested person to leave custody while the case continues, usually with conditions.

Grammar Focus: Using "because of"
Use “because of” before a noun or noun phrase to give a reason: “because of some threats”. Use “because” before a clause: “because there were threats”.
Example from the text: "President Trump said this was because of 'some threats'."
