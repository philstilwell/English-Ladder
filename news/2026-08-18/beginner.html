Read the story

Russia warns the United Kingdom. The UK supplied drones to Ukraine. Russia says the UK will pay for this action. British-made drones were used in the conflict. Russia says the UK is making the war worse. Russia accuses the UK of this dangerous action. The UK Ministry of Defence responded to Russia. The MoD says it stands against Russian aggression. The UK supports Ukraine against Russian actions. This is a big international disagreement.

Words and grammar

1. warns (verb): Tells someone about a possible danger or problem.
2. supplied (verb): Gave something to someone; provided something.
3. will pay (phrase): Will suffer consequences or face punishment for an action.
4. accuses (verb): Says someone did something wrong or is responsible for something bad.
5. aggression (noun): Angry or violent behavior towards another country or person.

Grammar Focus: Simple Present Tense
We use the simple present tense for facts, habits, and things that are true now. It shows actions that happen regularly or are generally true, like 'The sun rises in the east' or 'I drink coffee every day'.
Example from the text: "Russia warns the United Kingdom."
