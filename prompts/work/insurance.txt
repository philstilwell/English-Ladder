Lesson 01: Underwriting and Risk Selection
Original fictional case: Broker Lena submits an application for Atlas Services, describing its activity only as maintenance. The application omits operating locations and the requested three-year loss history. Underwriter Theo does not know whether the work is performed at a workshop, at customer premises, or both. No quote, binder, or coverage confirmation has been issued. Lena will obtain the activity breakdown, location details, and loss records, and give Theo a status update Monday at 11:00. Atlas wants cover urgently, but neither the requested start date nor a complete submission automatically establishes acceptance or coverage.
Language workshop: An incomplete submission cannot establish the risk
Communication goal: Request material missing information while preserving the difference between review, quotation, and binding.
Language guidance: A complete submission is not the same as an acceptable risk, a quote, or bound insurance. Preserve the stage of the process. The urgency of a requested inception date does not itself create coverage.
Useful expressions (preserve the supplied facts): We still need the operating locations and three-year loss history. / Does maintenance mean workshop work, customer-site work, or both? / A blank loss-history field does not mean no losses. / Please supply the requested three-year records. / Include the loss run's valuation date. / These details are needed to assess the risk presented. / Completing the submission does not guarantee acceptance. / No quote, binder, or coverage confirmation has been issued. / I understand that the requested start date is important. / A requested inception date is not a confirmed inception date. / Lena will obtain the activity and location breakdown. / We will update the submission status Monday at 11:00. / That is an update time, not a promise of bound cover. / Any decision must follow the applicable underwriting authority. / We have not established where all the work takes place. / Send the missing records with their period and source clearly identified.
Vocabulary: underwriting: Assessment and selection of risk under an insurer's applicable process. / submission: Information supplied for an insurance assessment or quotation. / applicant: The person or organization requesting insurance. / business activity: The operations performed by the applicant. / risk appetite: The types and characteristics of risk an insurer is prepared to consider. / exposure: A condition or measure associated with the possibility of insured loss. / operating location: A place where the business carries out its activities. / premises: Buildings or land occupied or used for an activity. / off-site work: Work performed away from the business's own location. / loss history: A record of prior losses or claims over a stated period. / loss run: A report listing claims and related information for a defined account and period. / valuation date: The date at which reported amounts or claim statuses are evaluated. / risk selection: Deciding which risks to accept under the relevant criteria. / referral: Escalation to a person or level with the required authority. / underwriting authority: The defined power to make specified underwriting decisions. / quote: Proposed insurance terms subject to their stated conditions and status. / binder: A document or agreement providing temporary insurance as applicable to its terms. / inception date: The date on which insurance begins under the applicable agreement. / subjectivity: A stated requirement attached to a proposed underwriting arrangement. / material information: Information relevant under the applicable decision or disclosure framework. / occupancy: The use of premises relevant to a property-risk assessment. / risk engineering: Technical assessment of risk conditions and controls. / declination: A decision not to offer the requested insurance. / submission completeness: Whether the requested submission information has been supplied.
Speaking task: Read turns 1-10 and emphasize the activity, location, and loss-record requests. Swap roles for turns 11-20; distinguish the requested start date from bound cover. Complete the Nova transfer and check the missing items, owner, and update time.
Partner: Lena: Insurance broker / Theo: Underwriter
Guided response task: Select supplied answers for the dialogue gaps. Check the explanations, then read the completed exchange aloud; no original writing is required.
Optional second-round challenge: The Nova application lacks two operating addresses and the requested two-year loss record. Priya will obtain the information. A status update is due Thursday at 10:00; no binder has been issued.
Model for comparison AFTER my attempt, not a response to give me first: Lena: If the clarified activities require a more senior decision, I would rather know the next review step than make a promise to the client.
Theo: We can explain the relevant referral status when applicable. Keep the distinction between a review route and a decision. Neither an escalation nor a request for information is automatically a declination.
Lena: My client update will list the requested facts, Monday's status contact, and the absence of any coverage confirmation. It will avoid a guaranteed outcome.
Theo: Good. Any eventual decision must respect underwriting authority and the applicable process. Clear information helps that decision, but it does not substitute for the required authorization or the actual terms offered.
END REFERENCE

The caller, Ben, has provided contact details and photographs, but the event date is unknown and the repair estimate has not been received. The date the damage was discovered is 6 June; that is not necessarily the date the event occurred. An adjuster has not completed the coverage or amount review. Mina will request the known timeline and supporting documents through the approved channel. An internal reserve, if established under the applicable process, is an estimate for claim accounting and management, not a promise of payment to Ben.
Language workshop: Open the file without inventing the loss details
