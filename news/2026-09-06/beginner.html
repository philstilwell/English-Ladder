Read the story

Air travel faces hotter weather and more storms. Climate change may cause more disruption to journeys. Passengers might face more delays. Some planes might need to change their routes.

Words and grammar

1. disruption (noun): a problem that interrupts normal activity
2. delays (plural noun): times when something happens later than planned
3. routes (plural noun): the ways used to travel from one place to another

Grammar Focus: Might for possibility
Might need to means a change is possible. It does not say every plane will change its route.
Example from the text: "Some planes might need to change their routes."
