Read the story

The report follows people forced to return to Afghanistan as Pakistan and Iran intensify expulsions. The measures affect undocumented migrants, but the report also describes registered Afghans facing removal from Pakistan. Some people have never been to Afghanistan. Nazia, born in Pakistan, crossed the border with her children into a country unfamiliar to her. Aid agencies have established temporary shelters near the crossing for families arriving without accommodation. The shelters offer only a few nights of refuge, leaving families to confront longer-term housing needs. Interviewees described limited financial resources and few immediate opportunities to earn a living. Reductions in international aid are adding pressure to the response. One mother also voiced concern about the future of girls under the Taliban’s restrictions on education. The UN refugee agency maintains that returns should be voluntary and should safeguard people’s safety and dignity.

Words and grammar

1. expulsions (plural noun): acts of officially forcing people to leave a country or place
2. undocumented (adjective): without the official documents required for residence or entry
3. registered (adjective): officially recorded on a list or in a system
4. temporary (adjective): intended to last for a limited time
5. refuge (noun): shelter or protection from danger or hardship
6. voluntary (adjective): done by free choice rather than through force
7. financial resources (noun phrase): money and other funds available to meet needs
8. safeguard (verb): to protect something important against harm or loss
9. restrictions (plural noun): rules or measures that limit what people can do
10. intensify (verb): to make an activity or effect stronger or more extensive

Advanced Grammar: Have never been
Have never been describes an experience that has not happened before now. Never means not at any time.
Example from the text: "Some people have never been to Afghanistan."
