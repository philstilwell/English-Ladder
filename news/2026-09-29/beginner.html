📅 September 29, 2026 - Celine Dion's Surprise Performance at Paris Fashion Week

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Celine Dion opened Paris Fashion Week. She gave a surprise performance. She sang at a show for L'Oreal. Celine Dion is a famous Canadian singer. Other famous people like Viola Davis joined her on the runway. Paris Fashion Week shows new clothes and designs. Many big fashion brands show their collections there.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. opened (verb): to start an event or something new.
2. surprise (noun): an event or thing that is not expected.
3. performance (noun): when a singer or actor entertains people.
4. famous (adjective): very well known by many people.
5. joined (verb): to come together with other people or things.
6. runway (noun): a long, narrow stage for models to show clothes.
7. designs (noun): new styles or looks for clothes.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. We often add "-ed" to regular verbs. Some verbs have different forms.
Example from the text: "Celine Dion opened Paris Fashion Week."
