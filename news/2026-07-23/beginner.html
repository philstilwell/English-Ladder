Read the story

The United States signed an important paper. This is a nuclear deal with Saudi Arabia. It is a new agreement for both countries. The US Department of Energy says this is important. They said it is for "peaceful" co-operation. US companies can now have "great access" to Saudi Arabia. They can join Saudi Arabia's nuclear energy program. This program helps Saudi Arabia make power. The agreement will help US companies too. Both countries will work together on this project.

Words and grammar

1. nuclear deal (noun): An official agreement about how to use nuclear power safely or for peaceful reasons.
2. agreement (noun): A formal decision or promise made by two or more people or groups to do something.
3. co-operation (noun): The act of working together with others to achieve a shared goal or to help each other.
4. access (noun): The ability or right to enter, use, or get something, like a place or an opportunity.
5. nuclear energy (noun): Power that is created when atoms are split apart, often used to produce electricity.

Grammar Focus: Simple Present Tense for facts
We use the Simple Present tense to talk about things that are always true or happen regularly. It is used for facts and general statements that do not change.
Example from the text: "The US Department of Energy says this is important."
