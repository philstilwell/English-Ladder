📅 September 21, 2026 - Trump Announces 'AI Force' Amid Technology Warnings

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

US President Donald Trump announced he will form an "AI Force" and appoint an artificial intelligence tsar. He stated his administration would not "hinder or stifle the growth" of the industry. This position comes amid warnings about the emergent technology and calls to slow its development until better safeguards are put in place. Many warnings about AI's potential dangers have led to demands for coordinated action on its development. Trump offered no details or a timeline for his plan to create this new "AI Force". He called AI the "next Industrial Revolution," predicting it would be even larger and more impactful. AI is now central to the US-China rivalry as both nations seek to gain an advantage in advanced technology. Researchers have suggested a significant chance that the technology could "endanger humanity". Major AI companies are also developing "super intelligence", which is theoretical technology smarter than humans. Some industry leaders have called for AI development to be regulated, including independent monitoring. However, Sam Altman of OpenAI suggested people should have more faith in AI firms to keep the technology safe.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. appoint (verb): to choose someone for an official job or position
2. tsar (noun): a person appointed by a government to advise on or coordinate a particular area of activity
3. hinder (verb): to make it difficult for someone to do something or for something to happen
4. stifle (verb): to prevent something from happening, developing, or continuing
5. safeguards (noun): measures taken to protect someone or something or to prevent something undesirable
6. coordinated (adjective): organized so that all the parts work together effectively
7. impactful (adjective): having a powerful effect on something or someone
8. rivalry (noun): a situation in which people or groups are competing with each other
9. advantage (noun): a condition or circumstance that puts one in a favorable or superior position
10. theoretical (adjective): based on ideas or concepts rather than practical experience or proof

Grammar Focus: Reporting Verbs
Reporting verbs are used to tell someone what another person said or asked without using their exact words. Common reporting verbs include "said," "told," "stated," "announced," "claimed," or "suggested." They often introduce a clause that explains the content of the reported speech.
Example from the text: "He stated his administration would not "hinder or stifle the growth" of the industry."
