Read the story

President Trump talked about the situation. He said a ceasefire is "over". A ceasefire means stopping a fight. The US and Iran had strikes before this. Strikes are military actions or attacks. President Trump was at a Nato summit. A summit is a meeting of important leaders. US negotiators can keep talking with the Iranians. Negotiators try to find solutions. They can talk if Iran wants to.

Words and grammar

1. ceasefire (noun): when fighting stops between two sides.
2. over (adjective): finished; not happening anymore.
3. strikes (noun): attacks, often by military forces.
4. summit (noun): a very important meeting, usually for leaders.
5. negotiators (noun): people who talk to find an agreement.

Grammar Focus: The word 'can'
We use 'can' to say that something is possible or someone has the ability to do something. It shows possibility or permission.
Example from the text: "US negotiators can keep talking with the Iranians."
