📅 July 29, 2026 - Over 100 aftershocks hit Japan after a deadly earthquake
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

Japan had a powerful earthquake recently. This earthquake killed 13 people. After the main quake, there were many smaller quakes. These smaller quakes are called aftershocks. More than 100 aftershocks hit the country. Officials say these aftershocks could be strong. Some aftershocks might reach magnitude 7. People are urged to stay alert. Staying alert means being ready for danger. It is important to stay safe in affected areas.

Words and grammar

1. earthquake (noun): A sudden strong shaking of the ground.
2. killed (verb (past simple)): Caused someone to die.
3. aftershocks (noun): Smaller earthquakes that happen after a big one.
4. officials (noun): People in important positions, often in the government.
5. alert (adjective): Watching and ready for danger or something important.
6. magnitude (noun): A measure of the size or power of an earthquake.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that happened and finished in the past. We often add '-ed' to regular verbs. For irregular verbs, the form changes.
Example from the text: "More than 100 aftershocks hit the country."
