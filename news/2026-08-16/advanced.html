Read the story

A fresh spate of aerial assaults perpetrated by Russia has resulted in the tragic deaths of five individuals across various Ukrainian regions. These renewed attacks underscore the escalating intensity of the conflict and its devastating human cost. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has vehemently accused Russian forces of intentionally targeting critical civilian infrastructure. He asserts that such actions constitute egregious violations of international law and humanitarian principles. Conversely, Moscow has proffered an alternative narrative, stating that its strikes were aimed at a specific warehouse. This stark discrepancy in accounts highlights the ongoing challenge of verifiable reporting amidst active combat. International observers are closely monitoring the situation to ascertain the objective truth behind these latest events. The continuous bombardment exacerbates the already dire humanitarian crisis faced by millions within Ukraine. Such persistent aggression by Russia draws widespread condemnation from the global community. Calls for a lasting and diplomatic resolution to the devastating conflict are growing increasingly urgent.

Words and grammar

1. aerial assaults (noun phrase): Attacks conducted using aircraft or missiles from the air.
2. perpetrated (verb (past participle)): Carried out or committed (a harmful, illegal, or immoral action).
3. vehemently accused (verb phrase): Strongly and intensely criticized or blamed someone for an action, often with strong feelings.
4. egregious violations (noun phrase): Extremely bad or shocking breaches of rules, laws, or principles.
5. proffered an alternative narrative (verb phrase): Offered a different version or account of events, typically to contradict a previously presented one.

Advanced Grammar: Participial Phrases for Consequence or Manner
Participial phrases (present or past participle) can be used to add extra information about the main clause, often describing the manner in which something is done, a consequence, or providing further context, creating more sophisticated sentence structures.
Example from the text: "Conversely, Moscow has proffered an alternative narrative, stating that its strikes were aimed at a specific warehouse."
