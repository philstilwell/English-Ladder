Read the story

An important religious leader in Iran died. Many senior officials went to his funeral. But Iran's supreme leader was absent. The supreme leader is named Mojtaba Khamenei. He has not been seen in public since an attack. This attack killed his father. The attack happened when a war started. This war is between the US, Israel, and Iran. People noticed the supreme leader did not attend the funeral. His absence is a topic of discussion.

Words and grammar

1. supreme leader (noun phrase): The most important leader of a country or group.
2. senior officials (noun phrase): Important people who work for the government or an organization.
3. absent (adjective): Not present; not in a place where someone should be.
4. attend (verb): To go to an event, a meeting, or a place.
5. funeral (noun): A ceremony for burying or burning a dead person.
6. attack (noun): a violent act to hurt or damage someone or something

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that happened and finished in the past. We often add "-ed" to regular verbs, or use special forms for irregular verbs. This tense tells us that an action is complete.
Example from the text: "An important religious leader in Iran died."
