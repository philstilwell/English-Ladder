Read the story

New technology is changing how some farmers work. George McMaster started farming by milking eight cows. He now has 300 cows and watches them through a camera. The camera makes a short video of each cow every day. It can find changes before farmers can see them. One farmer says healthier cows give them more milk. Some cows use a milking machine when they want to. A faster internet connection helped one farmer open a farm shop. A farmer uses an app to help run his farm. In this app, each cow has a page with all her records in one place. The app's developer says farmers should make the decisions. He says the technology helps farmers see the right information at the right time.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. technology (noun): Machines and computer programs that help people do things.
2. machine (noun): Something that uses power to do a job.
3. connection (noun): A way for a phone or computer to use the internet.
4. shop (noun): A place where people buy things.
5. records (plural noun): Written or saved information about something.
6. video (noun): Moving pictures that you can watch on a screen.

Grammar Focus: Can for ability
Use “can” with a verb to say what someone or something is able to do. After “can”, use the verb without “to” or “-s”.
Example from the text: "It can find changes before farmers can see them."
