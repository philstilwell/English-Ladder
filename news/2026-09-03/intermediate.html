A weather warning

Notice the difference between a warning and a certainty.

Before you read

Read the title. What do you think the story is about?

Read the story

The UN has warned about an unusually large El Niño. The source describes El Niño as a natural weather event. Countries are preparing for its possible effects. It could cause disruption to economies around the world.

Words and grammar

1. warned (verb): told people about a possible problem
2. preparing (verb): getting ready
3. disruption (noun): a problem that stops normal activity

Grammar Focus: Could for possibility
Could cause describes a possible result. It does not say that the result is certain.
Example from the text: "It could cause disruption to economies around the world."
