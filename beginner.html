📅 August 28, 2026 [0 days, 0 hours old] - Collapsed Glacier Causes Devastating Floods in Nepal-Tibet
A broken glacier in the mountains caused very bad floods in Nepal and Tibet.

Beginner ESL: Glaciers, Floods, and Climate Change in the Himalayas

I. The News Brief

A big glacier collapsed in the mountains. This happened near Nepal and Tibet. The collapsed glacier likely caused devastating floods there. Many scientists looked into this problem. Scientists say the floods were very bad. The floods destroyed many things. Ice in the Himalayan mountains is melting fast. This melting Himalayan ice is a serious issue. Scientists are still studying what happened. They want to understand why the glacier fell.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. collapsed glacier (noun phrase): A large piece of ice that broke into pieces and fell down from a mountain.
2. likely caused (verb phrase): Probably made something happen or was the main reason for an event.
3. devastating floods (noun phrase): Very destructive overflows of water that cause great harm and damage.
4. scientists say (verb phrase): Researchers or experts share their findings or give their professional opinions.
5. melting Himalayan ice (noun phrase): Frozen water in the high mountains of the Himalayas that is turning into liquid water.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or events that started and finished at a specific time in the past. For regular verbs, we often add '-ed', but many verbs have special past forms.
Example from the text: "The collapsed glacier likely caused devastating floods there."

📅 August 25, 2026 [2 days, 14 hours old] - Iran Responds to US Sanctions

Beginner ESL: US-Iran Economic Tensions

I. The News Brief

The United States announced new economic sanctions on Iran. These special rules affect Iran's money system. The US wants to put pressure on Iran's economy. The US called its latest sanctions an "economic D-Day." But Iran says it is fully prepared to face them. Iran's leaders have a plan to address these challenges. They want to counter the negative effects of the US actions. Iran states it has ways to manage the new money rules. This situation creates tension between the two countries. Iran aims to protect its financial system and people.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. sanctions (noun): Official orders to stop trade or money with a country.
2. economy (noun): The system of how a country makes, spends, and manages money and goods.
3. fully prepared (adjective): Completely ready for something to happen.
4. address (verb): To deal with a problem or situation.
5. counter (verb): To act against something to reduce its effect or stop it.

Grammar Focus: The Verb "To Be" (Simple Present)
The verb "to be" helps describe who or what someone or something is, or its state. In the simple present, we use "is" for singular subjects like "Iran" and "it". We use "are" for plural subjects and "am" for "I."
Example from the text: "Iran says it is fully prepared to face them."
