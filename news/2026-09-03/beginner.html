📅 September 03, 2026 - A weather warning

Read the story

The UN has warned about a very strong El Niño. This natural weather event starts in the Pacific Ocean. It can bring less rain to some places and more rain to others. It could cause disruption to economies around the world. Countries are preparing by improving weather warnings. Some are also getting medical help ready for people who may need it.

Words and grammar

1. warned (verb): told people about a possible problem
2. preparing (verb): getting ready
3. disruption (noun): a problem that stops normal activity
4. natural (adjective): existing or happening in nature
5. weather (noun): conditions such as rain, heat and wind
6. improving (verb): making something better

Grammar Focus: Could for possibility
Could cause describes a possible result. It does not say that the result is certain.
Example from the text: "It could cause disruption to economies around the world."
