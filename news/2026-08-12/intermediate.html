📅 August 12, 2026 - Rescuers Search for Survivors After Deadly Colombia Earthquake

Read the story

A powerful earthquake recently struck Colombia, causing immense destruction and tragic loss of life. Emergency rescue teams are currently scrambling to find and free individuals. Initial reports confirm that at least 180 people have died as a direct result of the disaster. Many buildings and infrastructure have collapsed, covering areas with vast amounts of rubble. The focus remains on locating survivors who might be trapped beneath the debris. On Tuesday, the affected region experienced a number of significant aftershocks. These subsequent tremors caused further instability and complicated the ongoing rescue operations. Government agencies have mobilised extensive resources to aid in the immediate emergency response. Specialized teams are working around the clock, desperately searching for any signs of life. International organizations are also preparing to offer essential assistance to Colombia during this critical period.

Words and grammar

1. scrambling (verb): Moving quickly and with difficulty, often using hands as well as feet, or acting urgently to do something.
2. mobilised (verb): To bring together and prepare resources or people for a particular purpose, especially in an emergency.
3. struck (verb): hit or affected a place suddenly and forcefully
4. infrastructure (noun): basic systems and facilities, such as roads, water supplies and electricity networks
5. assistance (noun): help given to someone who needs it
6. operations (plural noun): planned activities carried out for a purpose, such as rescue work or military action
7. collapsed (verb): fell down or gave way because a structure could no longer stand
8. preparing (verb): getting ready

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself rather than who or what is performing it. It's formed with a form of 'to be' and the past participle of the main verb. We often use it when the agent (the one doing the action) is unknown, unimportant, or obvious, or when we want to emphasize the receiver of the action.
Example from the text: "The focus remains on locating survivors who might be trapped beneath the debris."
