UK and EU Plan to Publish New Agreements

Beginner · A1–A2

📅 November 26, 2026 - UK and EU Plan to Publish New Agreements

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The UK government and the EU plan to publish two agreements on Thursday. They agreed on these plans last week. The new deal will reduce checks on food moving between the UK and the EU. UK businesses selling food to the EU will also have less paperwork, such as forms to complete. The government hopes these changes will help customers pay less for food. But customers may not see lower prices in shops. The BBC says the agreements include a special rule for the first ten years. Under this rule, the side ending the deal would pay to rebuild border infrastructure. This means buildings and other things needed for border checks.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. publish (verb): To make information available for people to read.
2. agreements (plural noun): Plans or rules that two sides accept.
3. checks (plural noun): Careful looks at things to see if they follow the rules.
4. paperwork (uncountable noun): Forms and other papers that people need to complete for work.
5. customers (plural noun): People who buy things from a shop or business.
6. lower (comparative adjective): Less than before.

Grammar Focus: “Will” for the future
Use “will” and a verb to talk about the future. The verb stays in its basic form, as in “will reduce”. Use the same form with “I”, “they” and other subjects. To make a negative, use “will not” and a verb.
Example from the text: "The new deal will reduce checks on food moving between the UK and the EU."
