Accuse and blame

Distinguish an allegation from an assignment of responsibility.

Level ranges are study guidance. Start with the examples and choose your own pace.

Try the practice →Learner workbook PDFTeaching guide PDF

ACCUSE OF

They accused him of sharing the file.

They claimed he did something wrong.

BLAME FOR

They blamed the delay on a missed delivery.

This assigns a cause.

OBSERVE

The file arrived after the deadline.

This states an observable event.

Understand

The useful distinction

Accuse someone of something means say that the person did something wrong: accuse someone of lying. Blame someone for something assigns responsibility: blame the supplier for a delay.

An accusation is a claim, not proof. Use alleged, said, or according to when reporting an unestablished claim. In a practical discussion, describing an observed problem and asking for an explanation can be more useful than assigning blame.
