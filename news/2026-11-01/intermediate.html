📅 November 01, 2026 - Rare Beetle Found Again in Wales After 60 Years

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

A rare beetle has been discovered in Pembrokeshire after being missing from Wales for 60 years. In the past, habitat loss had brought the Short-Necked Oil Beetle close to extinction, meaning it could have disappeared completely. Last year, a survey at Castlemartin found only dead adult beetles, raising concerns about the species' future there. This year, after three years of searching, experts discovered larvae, or young beetles at an early stage of development, in a small area. Anne Hobbs from the Bumblebee Conservation Trust said the evidence suggested that the species was breeding in Wales again. The beetles are dependent on bees to complete their life cycle and need a particular habitat, or type of natural environment. They need thick plants for shelter and plenty of flowers in their habitat. They are usually seen from March to June in coastal grassland and sand dunes, especially on warm, sunny days. Because they need such specific conditions, only a handful of sites in the UK are suitable for them. Castlemartin Range is currently the only known site for the species in Wales, and wildlife protection workers regularly survey it. Hobbs said protection work needed to consider the needs of several species to be effective. She thanked the project's partners and volunteers for continuing their support, despite two years without any sightings of larvae.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. habitat loss (noun phrase): The damage or disappearance of natural places where animals and plants live.
2. raising concerns (verb phrase): Making people worry about a possible problem.
3. evidence (uncountable noun): Facts or signs that help people judge whether something is true.
4. breeding (verb, present participle): Producing young animals.
5. dependent on (adjective phrase): Needing someone or something in order to live or develop.
6. shelter (uncountable noun): Protection provided by a place where an animal can stay safely.
7. a handful of (quantity phrase): A small number of people or things.
8. suitable (adjective): Having the right features for a particular need or purpose.

Grammar Focus: Present perfect passive
Use "has" or "have" followed by "been" and a past participle to report an event connected to the present. In "has been discovered", the focus is on the beetle and the discovery; the sentence does not identify who discovered it.
Example from the text: "A rare beetle has been discovered in Pembrokeshire after being missing from Wales for 60 years."
