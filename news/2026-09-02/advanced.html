Keep an accusation separate from a proven fact.

Read the story

The EU and Nato have vowed to increase pressure on Russia following what the headline calls a new escalation in Germany. Berlin has blamed Russia for a failed drone attack on Leipzig airport. The supplied source reports an attribution of responsibility; it does not provide the evidence behind it. Russia is accused of acting more recklessly.

Words and grammar

1. blamed (verb): said that someone was responsible
2. failed (adjective): did not succeed
3. accused (verb): said to have done something wrong

Advanced Grammar: Reporting an accusation
Is accused of reports a claim about wrongdoing. It does not establish that the claim is true.
Example from the text: "Russia is accused of acting more recklessly."
