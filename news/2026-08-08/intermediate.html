Read the story

Russian forces carried out fresh missile attacks in areas surrounding Kyiv overnight, causing significant destruction. Tragically, three civilians were killed in these strikes, and a young child was identified among the victims. The attacks intensified concerns about the ongoing conflict and its severe human cost. President Volodymyr Zelensky had previously issued a stark warning regarding Ukraine's dwindling supplies of crucial missile interceptors. He emphasized that without sufficient aerial defense systems, Russia could launch more devastating aerial assaults. The President's statement underscored the urgent need for international support to bolster Ukraine's defensive capabilities. These overnight attacks demonstrate Russia's continued aggression and disregard for civilian lives. Rescuers quickly responded to the affected sites, working tirelessly to search for survivors and manage the aftermath. The international community has widely condemned the escalating violence and the targeting of non-combatants. Ukraine continues to appeal for more advanced defensive weaponry to counter these persistent onslaughts.

Words and grammar

1. dwindling supplies (noun phrase): Becoming smaller or less in amount as time passes; gradually decreasing availability.
2. aerial assaults (noun phrase): Attacks carried out using aircraft, missiles, or drones from the sky rather than from the ground.
3. counter (verb): To act in opposition to something; to take action in response to an attack or threat to defend yourself.
4. onslaughts (noun): A fierce or destructive attack, often sustained and overwhelming in its intensity.
5. condemned (verb, past participle): Expressed strong disapproval of something, especially on moral or ethical grounds; officially declared wrong.

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself and what is being acted upon, rather than who or what is performing the action. It's formed with a form of the verb 'to be' (e.g., is, are, was, were) followed by the past participle of the main verb. News reports often use the passive voice to maintain objectivity and highlight the event.
Example from the text: "Tragically, three civilians were killed in these strikes, and a young child was identified among the victims."
