📅 November 22, 2026 - Countries Cannot Agree at Climate Talks
Countries disagree about fuel and money at climate talks in Turkey.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Nearly 200 countries are trying to agree on a new world climate plan. The talks are in Turkey, but the countries have not agreed on a plan. Teams stopped talking for the night after the meeting ran more than a day late. Some countries want a promise to use less fossil fuel, such as oil, gas and coal. However, the latest plan says nothing about using less fossil fuel. African countries also want promises of more money to prepare for climate problems. Some teams are changing their flights from Sunday to Monday while they wait for a deal. All countries must agree before they can make a deal. The talks could stop if the countries cannot agree. If the talks end without a deal, the countries will meet again next year.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. talks (plural noun): Meetings where people discuss something and try to agree.
2. plan (noun): Ideas about what to do and how to do it.
3. promise (noun): Words saying that you will do something.
4. prepare (verb): Get ready for something.
5. flights (plural noun): Journeys by plane.
6. deal (noun): A plan that two or more people or countries accept.

Grammar Focus: “Could” for possibility
Use “could” with a verb like “stop” or “meet” to say that something is possible. It does not mean that it will happen for sure. Put the verb straight after “could”, without “to”, and do not add “s” or “ed”.
Example from the text: "The talks could stop if the countries cannot agree."

Three young actors share their feelings about making a new Hunger Games film.

Read the story

Grace worked as an actor when she was a child. Peak and Zada asked Grace for help when they felt confused while talking about the film. One actor says they are thankful to have each other. The film is now in cinemas.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. nervous (adjective): Worried about what may happen.
2. character (noun): A person in a film or story.
3. notes (plural noun): Short pieces of writing that help you remember things.
4. prepare (verb): To do things before an activity so you are ready.
5. thankful (adjective): Happy that someone has helped you or that something good has happened.
6. cinemas (plural noun): Places where people go to watch films.

Grammar Focus: Simple past
Use the simple past for finished actions before now. Many verbs add “-ed”, so “watch” becomes “watched”. Some verbs change differently: “make” becomes “made”. These forms stay the same with “I”, “he” and “they”.
Example from the text: "He watched the earlier films and made notes to prepare for his part."

People can now buy and sell shares in the company behind Claude.

Anthropic makes Claude, a popular artificial intelligence (AI) tool that can talk with people. The company started just five years ago. Its share price was set at $83 before trading began. The price rose to $111.53, but it fell later that day. Each share cost $97.17 at the end of the day. Some investors, people who put money into companies, said the first day seemed to be a success. The price changes seemed to show that investors were still unsure about the future of AI.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. shares (plural noun): Small parts of a company that people can buy and sell.
2. popular (adjective): Liked or used by many people.
3. rose (verb, simple past): Went up in price.
4. fell (verb, simple past): Went down in price.
5. success (noun): Something that goes well.
