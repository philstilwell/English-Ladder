Read the story

President Trump made a new announcement. The United States will reduce its military drills with South Korea. South Korea stayed out of the Iran war. This was one reason for the President's choice. President Trump said he has a very good relationship with North Korea's leader. He also cites this friendship. It is another reason for the change. The decision is to downsize these exercises. This means the joint training will become smaller. The countries will still work together, but less often.

Words and grammar

1. reduce (verb): To make something smaller or less.
2. military drills (noun phrase): Practice exercises for soldiers and armies.
3. very good relationship (noun phrase): When two people or countries are good friends.
4. cites (verb): To give a reason for something or to mention something.
5. downsize (verb): To make something smaller.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. For regular verbs, we often add '-ed'. Some verbs are irregular and change their form, like 'stay' becoming 'stayed'.
Example from the text: "South Korea stayed out of the Iran war."
