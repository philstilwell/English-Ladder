Countries fail to agree on major climate plan

📅 November 23, 2026 - Countries fail to agree on major climate plan

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Countries at COP31 in Turkey failed to agree on a major plan to fight climate change. Countries disagreed about fossil fuels and money. Fossil fuels include coal, oil and gas. The talks were planned to end on Friday, but they continued until Sunday. The final plan did not mention fossil fuels. Some countries rejected the plan because it did not mention fossil fuels. Many poorer countries wanted clear promises of money from richer countries. They wanted help to deal with the effects of rising temperatures. The next climate talks are planned for November 2027 in Ethiopia.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. climate change (noun phrase): Changes in the world's usual weather over a long time.
2. fossil fuels (plural noun): Coal, oil and gas that people burn for energy.
3. talks (plural noun): Meetings where people discuss a problem or plan.
4. rejected (verb, past simple): Decided not to accept something.
5. promises (plural noun): Things that people say they will certainly do.
6. deal with (phrasal verb): Take action to help with a problem.

Grammar Focus: Giving reasons with because
Use "because" to explain why something happens or happened. Put the reason after "because". The reason needs a subject and a verb. You can put the action first and the reason second.
Example from the text: "Some countries rejected the plan because it did not mention fossil fuels."
