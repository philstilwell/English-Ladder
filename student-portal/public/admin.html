    HOW YOUR STUDENT PORTAL WORKS

    Technical notes

    A reference for the current rules, timing, privacy measures, and limits.

    Specifications updated October 15, 2026. These notes describe the system’s behavior; student details and passwords belong on individual account pages.

    CEFR estimates

    First estimate
    After two distinct usable, redacted transcripts have been saved. The review uses the latest two sessions.
    Monthly review
    No sooner than one calendar month after the previous review. It uses the latest four available sessions within the retention period. If only two or three remain, it uses those. Fewer than two means it waits.
    Evidence and limits
    CEFR means Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. The estimate covers conversational language visible in transcripts, from A1 to C2. It is not a formal test result or an independent assessment of pronunciation, pace, listening, reading, or writing.
    What keeps the level stable
    Every selected transcript is read in full, with learner quotations checked against each session. Teacher examples and quiz scores do not establish the level. Unchanged evidence does not trigger another review. Failed or inconclusive monthly reviews keep the earlier estimate.

    Estimates and supporting examples appear only in the administrator interface. Each account shows its next eligible review date.

    Transcript privacy

    Before saving
    Speaker identities are matched first. Names, contact information, and identifying personal details are then redacted and separately reviewed. A failed privacy review prevents storage and publication from that transcript.
    Speaker roles
    Student and Instructor labels stay distinct. Other speakers receive separate anonymous labels. Timestamps and the remaining wording are preserved, including language mistakes.
    Purpose
    Saved transcripts are used for language assessment. Personal lives, work performance, beliefs, and health are not assessed.

    Automated redaction cannot guarantee complete anonymity. Raw Zoom responses and the name-to-role mapping are not saved by the portal.

    Storage and retention

    Where copies live
    In the website’s private server storage, separated by student account. Full cached transcripts are not sent to browser pages or saved in browser storage.
    Three-month limit
    Nightly cleanup deletes cached copies older than three calendar months, using Eastern lesson dates. It includes all saved versions and paused students, and still runs when publishing is disabled or Zoom is disconnected.
    Records that remain
    Feedback, assessments, brief CEFR evidence, quizzes, and first-choice answer history remain. This cleanup does not delete original Zoom records.

    Account deletion removes that account’s cached transcripts and learning records together. The retired student address cannot be reused.

    Nightly updates and Zoom

    Schedule
    1 a.m. New York time, with daylight saving handled automatically. The website’s server does the work; your Mac and Codex can be off.
    Finding a lesson
    The job checks Zoom for new or changed notes, matches an exact configured speaker name, and confirms the meeting date. It requires the complete transcript and allows time for a newly finished meeting to settle.
    Reusing saved copies
    Completed lessons are skipped. Pending retries use the reviewed server copy while the source and speaker mapping are unchanged. A changed source is fetched again; a pending copy without a source revision is refreshed after seven days.

    Monthly CEFR reviews can run from saved copies even if Zoom is unavailable. Historical lessons are imported separately; a software update does not automatically backfill old recordings.

    Feedback and quizzes

    Each eligible lesson
    Produces reviewed language feedback, an updated assessment, and seven new practice questions when the evidence is sufficient. Failed generation keeps the existing useful pages.
    Original answers
    The first choice is kept even after a successful retry. Teacher previews do not count as student attempts. Older quiz wording and saved attempts are preserved.
    Student-facing language
    Instructions use short, clear English. This reading style and quiz performance are not treated as proof of a CEFR level. Study plans suggest independent practice.

    Accounts and access

    Private administration
    Full names, contact details, course records, and account management stay in the administrator area. Student pages use first names and chosen nicknames.
    Student Account page
    Students can change their nickname and password from Account in their main menu. A password change requires the current password and ends their existing sessions on all devices. Passwords remain case-insensitive, and Phil’s private login-details panel can retrieve the updated password. Teacher previews cannot submit student settings.
    Activities choices
    Only the administrator chooses the tools in each student’s Activities dropdown. Open Edit account, check the tools under Student activities, and save changes. The same checkboxes are available when registering a student. The available tools are English Road Practice, Grammar practice, and Business terms. All three are checked by default for new students. Existing selections, including an empty selection, are preserved. The student menu refreshes when opened or the page reloads. Students can change their nickname and password, but cannot change these selections. Hiding a tool keeps its saved practice progress.
    Activity overview
    Each activated student’s account loads a private summary of all three Activities tools. English Road shows sets started/completed, saved answers and correct answers, and the latest set. Grammar practice and Business terms show items marked finished, questions to discuss, and the saved place. Last saved activity uses existing server dates from answers, item choices, navigation bookmarks, and filter changes. Simply opening a page or using a teacher preview creates no activity record. Refresh checks for new saved changes; hidden tools and paused accounts keep their history. These records are practice information, separate from lesson quizzes and CEFR estimates. No new tracking service or paid AI call is used.
    Paused accounts
    Student access and new lesson/CEFR updates stop. Saved learning history remains, and transcript retention cleanup continues.
    Sessions and passwords
    Sign-in sessions last up to eight hours. Changing a student password ends that student’s existing sessions. Signing out clears loaded private information from the page.
    Course records
    Course end dates and hours are manually maintained; they do not automatically pause an account or decrease after a Zoom meeting.

    English Road Practice

    Question source
    A reviewed copy of 4,200 English Road questions across 35 topics and six practice bands. Mixed sets use 25 distinct topics; focused sets use 20 questions. Updates are imported deliberately, without fetching English Road during student practice.
    Saving and review
    Starting a set and checking each answer saves it to private storage for that student. Original choices, question wording, explanations, and server dates are preserved. Students can resume on another device. Ended and completed sets remain available in the English Road Practice panel on the administrator’s student account page.
    Separation and privacy
    Practice is separate from lesson quizzes and CEFR estimates. Teacher previews cannot submit student answers. Answers are not saved in browser storage or sent to English Road. Unsubmitted choices are not saved. Account deletion removes practice records; the three-month transcript rule does not delete practice history.
    Cost and limits
    No AI calls or new paid service are required. Practice uses existing private storage and hosting. Each account is limited to 90 practice changes per minute. Future hosting charges depend on total usage and the account’s allowances.

    Grammar practice

    Question source
    A copy of the 900 ranked items from the template spreadsheet’s Grammar tab, imported October 15, 2026. The original order, questions, choices, and answer key are preserved. Student comments, learned-item checkboxes, and AI responses are not imported. The sheet is not fetched during practice; future content changes need a new import.
    Simple self-study
    Students see ten items at a time and can jump to any group. Checking “Show answer” replaces the blanks with the source answer and shows the completed sentence in the logo’s blue. Unchecking restores the original item and normal text color. This activity has no score or answer submission. Teacher previews can use it too.
    Finished and Questions
    Each item has two compact buttons, with text and dimensions 30% smaller than standard action buttons. Finish changes to Finished and gives it a grey background. The yellow Questions button changes to Saved to Questions and adds it to the student’s Questions list, with links back to the exact items. Both choices are saved privately and can be undone independently. The same list is available in the Grammar practice panel on the administrator account page, including for paused students. Teacher views can read choices but cannot change them. Grammar choices are separate from Business terms.
    Hide finished items
    This checkbox shows only unfinished items, keeping their original numbers and order. Navigation skips finished items. The Questions list still includes finished items; opening one shows all items again. A finished item disappears only after its choice is saved. If all items are finished, the page explains how to show them again. Each student’s checkbox choice is saved privately for this activity and restored on return, including on another device. Opening a finished question temporarily shows all items without changing the saved preference. Teacher preview changes are temporary. Failed saves keep the prior choice and show a retry message.
    Return to practice
    Each student’s last group is saved privately and restored when they return, including on another device. If it is hidden by the finished-items filter, the page opens the next available group, or the last available one if none follows. An all-finished page keeps the saved place. Answer reveals stay temporary. Teacher previews do not change the student’s place. Failed saves offer a retry; a change from another page is not replaced until the student chooses to save again.
    Privacy and cost
    Finished and Questions choices, the Hide finished items preference, the last viewed place, update times, and save revisions are stored, alongside existing menu visibility preferences. Answers and answer reveals are not saved. Loaded content clears when leaving, reloading, signing out, or the session expires. Saved choices remain until changed or the account is deleted. No records go to browser storage, Google, or AI, and these choices do not affect CEFR or lesson quizzes. No new paid service or AI call is required; ordinary hosting and storage allowances apply.

    Business terms

    Question source
    Imported October 15, 2026 from the template spreadsheet’s New BTs tab. The activity uses 2,958 complete exercises, preserving their original order and item numbers. Each has three sentences, six numbered blanks, and six alphabetical options. Repeated words stay repeated, and the answer key keeps the correct capitalization.
    Items held for correction
    Three of the 2,961 source exercises are incomplete: item 18, “street”; item 155, “molecule”; and item 1563, “privacy”. The first two have only five filled answers. “Privacy” also has an unfinished sentence. They are omitted from student practice until corrected and imported again. The original spreadsheet is unchanged.
    Self-check and navigation
    One exercise appears at a time. Students think through the placements and check one “Show answers” box to fill all three sentences. Only the six inserted answers appear in logo blue; the surrounding words keep their normal color. Unchecking restores the numbered blanks. Previous and Next follow the source order and skip incomplete items; students can also jump to an item number. A note explains that later questions are harder.
    Finished and Questions
    Finish changes to Finished and gives the exercise a grey background. The yellow Questions button changes to Saved to Questions and adds it to a shared Questions list for discussion during lessons. Students can undo either choice. Both choices are saved privately by student and item; they return on the next visit. The administrator account page has a Questions list panel with the same exercises and answer reveals, including for paused students. Teacher views can read these choices but cannot change them.
    Hide finished items
    This checkbox shows only unfinished items, keeping their original numbers and order. Navigation skips finished items. The Questions list still includes finished items; opening one shows all items again. A finished item disappears only after its choice is saved. If all items are finished, the page explains how to show them again. Each student’s checkbox choice is saved privately for this activity and restored on return, including on another device. Opening a finished question temporarily shows all items without changing the saved preference. Teacher preview changes are temporary. Failed saves keep the prior choice and show a retry message.
    Return to practice
    Each student’s last item is saved privately and restored when they return, including on another device. If it is hidden by the finished-items filter, the page opens the next available item, or the last available one if none follows. An all-finished page keeps the saved place. Answer reveals stay temporary. Teacher previews do not change the student’s place. Failed saves offer a retry; a change from another page is not replaced until the student chooses to save again.
    Privacy and cost
    Finished and Questions choices, the Hide finished items preference, the last viewed place, update times, and save revisions are stored. No answers, scores, or revealed states are saved. Choices are kept separate from CEFR and lesson quizzes. Student notes, questions, completion checkboxes, and other sheet tabs are not imported. Loaded content clears when leaving the activity, reloading, signing out, or the session expires; saved choices remain until changed or the account is deleted. No records go to browser storage, Google, or AI. No new paid service or AI call is required; ordinary hosting and storage allowances apply.

    AI explanation prompts

    Copy and paste
    Things to practice items in Assessments, Feedback items in Things to practice, Useful language, and A few minutes of practice, the Quiz under Overview, Grammar practice, Business terms, and English Road Practice have a compact yellow Copy AI prompt button for each item. English Road includes it in answer review too. After a successful copy, it reads AI prompt copied. Very small ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot icons sit beside the button. Each opens the regular AI chat in a new tab, where students paste the copied prompt. The icons have names on hover and for screen readers. Failed copying shows a retry message.
    What the prompt contains
    The exercise, its choices, and a request for a simple explanation, two new examples, and one follow-up question. Assessment practice prompts contain only the topic, model sentences, and practice suggestion; student evaluations and lesson evidence are excluded. Feedback prompts use only the selected item: its topic, model sentence, and explanation; its word or phrase, meaning, and example; or its practice task and example answer. They exclude lesson remarks, dates, and source evidence. Grammar and Business terms include the source answer key. Quiz prompts include the authored question, choices, answer key, explanation, and model sentence, with no source lesson details. English Road includes its key and explanation only after the question has been answered. Prompts exclude student profiles, chosen answers, scores, saved choices, and lesson transcripts.
    Saving and cost
    Copying does not change progress or saved choices, store the prompt in the browser, open an AI site, or send an AI request. The icon links open only the chat site, without sending the prompt or student details, copying again, or submitting anything. The copied label is temporary. Teacher previews can copy Assessment practice, Feedback, Quiz, Grammar, and Business terms prompts without changing student records. Copying makes no paid AI call; students choose whether to paste and submit the prompt in their own AI service.

    AI costs and spending safeguards

    The server uses Cloudflare Workers AI with the gpt-oss-120b model. New transcript privacy processing uses two calls. Ordinary lesson authoring and review use two calls. A due CEFR review has its own two calls.

    Ordinary lesson updates
    At most two attempts per student per UTC day and twelve per calendar month.
    CEFR reviews
    A separate allowance of one attempt per student per UTC day and two per calendar month, including retries. It does not use up the lesson-update allowance.
    Estimated additional AI cost
    About $0.01–$0.05 to redact and review a new transcript, and $0.02–$0.10 per CEFR review, before free allowances. Actual cost depends on transcript length. Failed attempts count toward limits; these limits are not a fixed dollar cap.

    UTC is the time standard used for spending counters; the nightly schedule and monthly CEFR eligibility use New York dates. Stored copies are expected to fit the existing storage allowance. Simply opening these pages does not trigger paid AI work.
