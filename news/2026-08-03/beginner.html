Read the story

The news is about Iran and the United States. Donald Trump said new talks would start. He said talks would begin on Monday. But Iran denies this claim. Iran says no new talks are taking place with the US. However, Iran is in negotiations with Oman. These negotiations are about access. They are discussing the Strait of Hormuz. This strait is an important sea area. Iran wants to keep access to this area.

Words and grammar

1. denies (verb): To say that something is not true.
2. talks (noun): Meetings or discussions to agree on something.
3. claim (noun): A statement that someone says is true, but others might not agree.
4. negotiations (noun): Formal discussions between people to reach an agreement.
5. access (noun): The right or opportunity to use or enter something.

Grammar Focus: Using 'would' for reported speech or future in the past
We use 'would' to talk about future actions when speaking about the past. It is also used when reporting what someone said about the future. For example, if someone said 'The talks will start,' you can report it as 'He said the talks would start.'
Example from the text: "Donald Trump said new talks would start."
