📅 September 22, 2026 - Rare Harper Lee Book Found in Oxfam Shop

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

A rare book was found in an Oxfam shop in London. It is a first edition of the novel 'To Kill a Mockingbird' by Harper Lee. The book was donated to the charity's bookshop. A shop manager found this special book and knew it was important. The outside cover was old, but the pages inside were in beautiful condition. Now, the book is part of the national collection at the British Library. This discovery reminds people about treasures found in second-hand shops. Oxfam's 'Second Hand September' campaign encourages buying used items.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. rare (adjective): Not common; not seen or found often.
2. found (verb (past simple)): Discovered by chance; saw or learned about something for the first time.
3. shop (noun): A place where you can buy things.
4. donated (verb (past simple)): Gave something, like money or goods, to a charity or a person who needs help.
5. manager (noun): A person who is in charge of a business or a part of a business.
6. special (adjective): Better or more important than usual; unique.
7. condition (noun): The state of something, how good or bad it is.
8. campaign (noun): A series of actions planned to achieve a goal, like selling a product or helping a cause.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or events that happened in the past and are now finished. For example, 'was found' means someone found it before now.
Example from the text: "A rare book was found in an Oxfam shop in London."
