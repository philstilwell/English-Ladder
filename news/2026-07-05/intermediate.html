Read the story

Senior Iranian officials recently gathered for the funeral of an influential ayatollah, drawing international attention. However, Iran's supreme leader was notably absent from this important public event, sparking various discussions. This significant absence has led to numerous speculations among political observers and analysts. Separately, Mojtaba Khamenei, who is a prominent figure in Iran, has not been seen publicly for some time. His disappearance began following an attack that tragically killed his father, a significant event. This incident occurred at the very outset of a major conflict involving the US and Israel in relation to Iran. The ongoing conflict has significantly impacted the political landscape and regional stability. Such unusual public absences of key individuals often suggest underlying shifts or important internal events within a government. Many are observing closely for any further official announcements or new developments regarding the situation. The complex situation highlights the deep political complexities and continued tensions within the Middle East.

Words and grammar

1. absent (adjective): Not present in a place or at an event; missing.
2. officials (noun): People who hold a position of authority or trust in an organization or government.
3. ayatollah (noun): A high-ranking cleric in the Shia Islamic religious hierarchy, often with significant religious and political influence.
4. outset (noun): The beginning or start of something, especially a process or event.
5. conflict (noun): A serious disagreement or argument, often involving prolonged fighting or hostility between groups or nations.

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice (Present Perfect)
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself or the object receiving the action, rather than the person or thing performing the action. The Present Perfect Passive is formed with 'has/have been' + the past participle of the main verb. It is often used in news to report events where the agent is unknown, unimportant, or the focus is on the result.
Example from the text: "Mojtaba Khamenei, who is a prominent figure in Iran, has not been seen publicly for some time."
