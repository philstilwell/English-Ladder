Nature & city life

Can trees cool a city?

Explain how trees help and suggest a place that needs shade.

Beginner · A1–A2 / About 10–15 min

Read the story ↓

3 useful words · 5 questions · Your turn to speak

📅 September 05, 2026 - Can trees cool a city?

Read the story

Find two ways trees help cool a place. Then notice what young trees need.

Trees can help cool a city. Their leaves give people shade on hot days. Trees also release water into the air, and this helps cool it. A tree near a building can reduce the need for air conditioning. People need to choose trees that suit the local climate. Young trees need care after people plant them.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. shade (noun): a place where something blocks direct sunlight
2. reduce (verb): to make something smaller or less
3. climate (noun): the usual weather in a place over many years

Grammar Focus: Can + verb
Use can with the basic form of a verb to describe what is possible.
Example from the text: "Trees can help cool a city."

Make the English yours

Your turn to speak

Try these three short activities with a partner, or say both parts aloud on your own. Start with the sentence help; then try without it.

  1. Say what a tree can do

    Use can to say two things trees do. Check your ideas against the story.

    Trees can ___. They can also ___.

    See an example

    Trees can give people shade. They can also help cool the air.

  2. Choose a shady place

    Imagine a school playground and a bus stop with no shade. Choose one place for more trees. Tell a partner why. Your partner chooses a place too.

    I choose ___. People can ___ there.

  3. Remember the care

    Your friend wants to plant a tree. Tell your friend two things from the story to remember.

    Choose a tree that ___. Young trees need ___.

    See an example

    Choose a tree that suits the local climate. Young trees need care.

  4. One last try, from memory

    Explain how trees help and suggest a place that needs shade. Close the examples and say your answer aloud.
