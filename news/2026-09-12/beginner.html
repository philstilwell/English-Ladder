Museum Thefts Are Becoming Bolder in Europe

📅 September 12, 2026 - Museum Thefts Are Becoming Bolder in Europe

Read the story

Museum thefts are becoming bolder in Europe. Thieves recently stole four paintings from a French museum. These paintings were by artist Renoir. They were worth millions of euros. Museums are often in old buildings, so they are hard to protect. Experts say museums need more time to stop thieves. Some museums are adding more security, like enhanced video surveillance.

Words and grammar

1. thefts (noun): The acts of stealing something; taking things that are not yours.
2. bolder (adjective): More brave or not afraid to do something risky.
3. stole (verb): Took something that belonged to another person or place without permission (past tense of steal).
4. worth (adjective): Having a value of a certain amount of money.
5. protect (verb): To keep someone or something safe from danger or harm.
6. security (noun): Actions or systems put in place to keep a place or person safe from danger or crime.
7. artist (noun): A person who creates art, like paintings or sculptures.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished at a specific time in the past. For example, 'stole' is the past tense of 'steal'.
Example from the text: "Thieves recently stole four paintings from a French museum."
