Read the story

Kyiv, a big city in Ukraine, had a very serious attack. This attack happened on Thursday. At least 13 people were killed. Apartment blocks in the city faced damage. Warehouses also faced damage from the attack. Officials said it was a "massive attack." Many buildings were targeted during the attack. Ukraine currently has problems with air defence systems. They need more protection from such attacks in the future. The situation in Kyiv is very sad for many people.

Words and grammar

1. attack (noun): A strong, harmful action against a place or people.
2. killed (verb): To take away someone's life.
3. officials (noun): Important people who work for the government or a company.
4. targeted (verb): Chosen as a place or thing to hit or hurt.
5. air defence (noun): Systems or weapons that protect a country from attacks from the sky.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. It often tells us "when" something happened, like an event that is now over.
Example from the text: "This attack happened on Thursday."
