📅 November 27, 2026 - Zelensky Wants More US Help for Ukraine

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Ukraine's president, Volodymyr Zelensky, spoke to the BBC in Kyiv. He said the US was paying too much attention to the Middle East. He wants the US to push Russia harder during peace talks. The aim of these talks is to end the war between Russia and Ukraine. The latest peace talks made no clear progress. Zelensky said he expected talks to start again in December. Some businesses in Kyiv have closed because power cuts and attacks have made their costs too high. Some people have already left the city for the winter. Zelensky said Ukraine would try to defend its capital.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. attention (noun): Careful thought about someone or something.
2. peace talks (plural noun): Meetings where people try to agree on ending a war.
3. start again (verb phrase): Begin one more time after stopping.
4. power cuts (plural noun): Times when homes and other buildings have no electricity.
5. closed (verb, past participle): Stopped being open for business.
6. capital (noun): The city where a country's government works.

Grammar Focus: Giving a reason with "because"
Use "because" to give a reason. Put it before the part of the sentence that explains why something happens. The other part gives the result. You can use "because" with present or past verbs.
Example from the text: "Some businesses in Kyiv have closed because power cuts and attacks have made their costs too high."
