📅 November 11, 2026 - Robot Boats Help Navies Search for Sea Mines

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The conflict in the Gulf gives the US an opportunity to test robot systems designed to find sea mines. Modern sea mines rest on the seabed and explode when they detect sound or magnetic signals from a passing ship. Even the threat of these weapons can stop a group of tankers from sailing. Traditionally, specialist mine-hunting ships have carried crews of around 80 people. To keep people away from danger, autonomous systems that can operate on their own are monitored from shore. Some systems may need only 20 people, who work in shifts on land. A Thales robot boat carries sensors that detect mines using sonar, a technology that uses sound to find objects. It pulls equipment that produces a detailed three-dimensional picture of the seabed, using artificial intelligence to improve the image. Compared with conventional mine-hunting ships, autonomous systems are cheaper and can be transported by air with their control centres. The report says US robot minehunters can either blow up a mine or mark its location as a place to avoid. The report also explains that robots can reduce the risk but cannot prove that no mines are present. Neil Roberts, who speaks for marine insurers, says uncertainty about how many mines may be present affects how people feel.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. conflict (noun): Fighting between countries or armed groups.
2. threat (noun): The possibility that something dangerous or harmful could happen.
3. specialist (adjective): Designed or trained for a particular kind of work.
4. autonomous (adjective): Able to operate without a person controlling every action.
5. monitored (verb, past participle): Watched or checked over time to see what is happening.
6. in shifts (adverbial phrase): In separate working periods, with different groups taking turns.
7. conventional (adjective): Following the usual or traditional way of doing something.
8. uncertainty (noun): A situation in which something is not known or certain.

Grammar Focus: Present simple passive
Use the present simple passive to describe a regular process when the action matters more than who does it. Form it with “am”, “is” or “are” plus a past participle. Here, “are monitored” focuses on what happens to the systems.
Example from the text: "To keep people away from danger, autonomous systems that can operate on their own are monitored from shore."
