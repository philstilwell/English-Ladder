Read the story

Odesa, a vital Ukrainian port city, is facing a severe threat from Russia's intensified Black Sea strikes. Recent aerial attacks have caused widespread destruction along Ukraine's southern coastline. Images show many blackened ships and badly smashed docks after the bombardments. These strikes represent a significant surge in Russia's military campaign in the region. The port infrastructure, crucial for Ukraine's economy, has been severely damaged. Residents and international observers are concerned about the impact on maritime trade. Grain exports, vital for global food security, are particularly at risk due to these attacks. The repeated targeting of port facilities demonstrates a clear strategy to disrupt Ukrainian shipping. Despite defenses, no area in Odesa seems completely safe from these aerial assaults. The international community continues to monitor the situation, calling for an end to the aggression.

Words and grammar

1. intensified (adjective): Made stronger or more extreme in degree, force, or amount.
2. blackened (adjective): Made dark or dirty, often by smoke, fire, or corrosion, indicating damage.
3. smashed (adjective): Violently broken into many pieces or severely damaged by forceful impact.
4. surge (noun): A sudden, powerful, and often large increase or movement of something.
5. impact (noun): The powerful effect that something has on a situation, person, or object.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Passive Voice
The Present Perfect Passive Voice is used to describe an action that started in the past and continues to the present, or a past action with a present result, where the focus is on the action itself or the receiver of the action, not necessarily who performed it. It is formed with 'has/have been' + the past participle of the main verb.
Example from the text: "The port infrastructure, crucial for Ukraine's economy, has been severely damaged."
