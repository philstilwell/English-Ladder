📅 August 31, 2026 - A difficult rescue in Nepal
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

Rescuers in Nepal are trying to reach trapped workers. Floods filled tunnels at their power sites with mud. The rescuers have used explosives to break through a hillside. Engineers are sending air into the tunnels. Teams from other countries are helping with the rescue. Families are waiting for news.

Words and grammar

1. rescuers (plural noun): people who try to bring others to safety
2. trapped (adjective): unable to get out
3. waiting (verb): staying until something happens
4. tunnels (plural noun): long passages under the ground
5. explosives (plural noun): materials that can burst with great force
6. engineers (plural noun): people who plan and build machines or structures

Grammar Focus: An action happening now
Are waiting describes an activity in progress. It does not tell us when the wait will end.
Example from the text: "Families are waiting for news."
