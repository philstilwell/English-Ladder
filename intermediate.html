US President Trump has reportedly canceled planned strikes against Iran following requests from Middle Eastern countries, as an agreement's perimeters were discussed.

Intermediate ESL: International Relations, Diplomacy, US Foreign Policy, and Middle East Conflicts

I. The News Brief

US President Donald Trump recently announced he cancels planned military actions against Iran. This decision follows a period of escalating tensions in the Middle East region. The President claims that both Iran and other Middle Eastern nations approached Washington. They reportedly asked the United States to hold off on any potential strikes. Trump indicated that these countries requested a delay because the general perimeters of a diplomatic deal had been discussed. The President suggested that an initial agreement's boundaries were already laid out. This diplomatic effort aims to de-escalate the conflict and prevent further military engagement. International observers are closely monitoring the situation as discussions continue. The sudden cancellation highlights the complexities of foreign policy and international negotiations. It also suggests a willingness from various parties to seek a resolution through dialogue.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. cancels (verb): To decide or announce that something arranged will not happen.
2. strikes (noun): Military attacks, usually from the air.
3. claims (verb): To state something is true, often without proof.
4. hold off (phrasal verb): To delay doing something or to prevent something from happening.
5. perimeters (noun): The outer limits or boundaries of an area or situation.

Grammar Focus: Past Perfect Passive
The Past Perfect Passive is used to talk about an action that was completed before another past action, and the focus is on the action itself rather than who performed it. It's formed with 'had + been + past participle.'
Example from the text: "the general perimeters of a diplomatic deal had been discussed."

📅 August 01, 2026 [1 day, 0 hours old] - EU Tensions Rise Over Migrant Crossings in Ceuta
Spain is criticizing some EU countries, especially Italy, for their reactions to recent migrant crossings into Ceuta, leading to a temporary suspension of the Schengen arrangement.

Intermediate ESL: EU Migration Crisis and Diplomatic Tensions

I. The News Brief

Significant diplomatic tensions have recently escalated between Spain and some other European Union countries. These disagreements stem from the recent increase in migrant crossings into Ceuta, a Spanish territory bordering Morocco. Spain has publicly criticized what it views as a "selfish response" from certain EU nations regarding this humanitarian situation. Italy, in particular, has reacted strongly, with its Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni describing the scenes in Ceuta as "shocking." In a significant diplomatic move, Italy has temporarily suspended the Schengen arrangement with Spain. This means that regular passport-free travel between Italy and Spain will be interrupted, and border controls will be reinstated for a period. The Schengen arrangement typically allows for free movement of people across the internal borders of its participating member states. Spain stresses the importance of a unified and cooperative response from all EU members when addressing complex migration issues. The Spanish government believes that showing solidarity is crucial during such challenging times. This ongoing dispute highlights the deep-seated challenges and differing perspectives within the EU concerning shared responsibilities for border management and migration policies.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. selfish (adjective): Thinking only about yourself and not considering other people's needs or feelings.
2. response (noun): An answer or reaction to something that has been said or done.
3. migrant crossings (noun phrase): The act of people moving from one country or area to another, often by crossing a border or stretch of water.
4. temporarily suspended (verb phrase): Stopped or interrupted for a limited period of time; not permanently ended.
5. Schengen arrangement (noun phrase): An agreement among many European countries that allows people to travel freely between them without passport checks at internal borders.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Tense
The Present Perfect tense is used to describe an action that began in the past and continues into the present, or an action completed in the past with a result that affects the present. It is formed with 'has' or 'have' followed by the past participle of the main verb.
Example from the text: "Italy has temporarily suspended the Schengen arrangement with Spain."

📅 July 31, 2026 [1 day, 23 hours old] - Massive Migrant Influx Overwhelms Spanish Enclave of Ceuta
About 49,000 migrants entered the Spanish territory of Ceuta in just 24 hours, significantly exceeding half of the city's total population.

Intermediate ESL: Migration to Spanish territories

I. The News Brief

Spanish officials have reported an unprecedented influx of migrants into the territory of Ceuta. In a single 24-hour period, approximately 49,000 individuals entered the Spanish enclave from Morocco. This massive movement represents a significant challenge for the local authorities and aid organizations. The city of Ceuta, situated on the North African coast, is home to about 83,600 residents. Consequently, the estimated number of arrivals constitutes more than half of the city's total population. Many migrants undertook dangerous journeys, including swimming around breakwaters to reach the territory. Spanish border forces and emergency services were quickly overwhelmed by the sheer scale of the situation. This rapid influx has prompted urgent discussions between Spain and Morocco regarding border security. Humanitarian groups are now providing essential aid and support to the thousands of people who have arrived. The situation clearly highlights the complex and ongoing issues surrounding migration routes into European territories.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. influx (noun): A large number of people or things arriving at a place or situation at the same time.
2. territory (noun): An area of land that is controlled by a particular country or ruler.
3. enclave (noun): A small area or group that is different from the larger area or group surrounding it.
4. constitutes (verb): To be considered to be something, or to form or make up something.
5. overwhelmed (adjective): Unable to deal with something because it is too much or too difficult to manage.

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when we want to focus on the action itself and the person or thing affected by it, rather than the person or thing performing the action. It is formed using a form of the verb 'to be' followed by the past participle of the main verb. It is often employed when the agent (the one doing the action) is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from the context.
Example from the text: "Spanish border forces and emergency services were quickly overwhelmed by the sheer scale of the situation."

📅 July 30, 2026 [2 days, 23 hours old] - US Launches 'Heavy' Strikes on Iran Amid Renewed Hostilities
The United States has launched significant military strikes against Iran following a period of calm, as both nations have resumed missile attacks.

Intermediate ESL: Geopolitical conflict and renewed military actions in the Middle East.

I. The News Brief

The United States has recently launched several heavy military strikes on Iran. These actions came after an attempted attack on American troops, escalating tensions in the region. Prior to this, there had been a short period of relative calm, creating hope for de-escalation. During that time, hostilities were paused, indicating a temporary halt in the conflict between the two nations. However, this lull has now ended, with both sides resuming their aggressive postures. Reports confirm that both the US and Iran have restarted missile attacks, making the situation more volatile. The US justified its strikes as a necessary defensive measure following the threat to its personnel. International bodies are expressing concern about the renewed conflict and its potential impact on regional stability. This rapid return to conflict follows a brief moment where de-escalation seemed possible. The world is closely observing these developments, hoping for a peaceful resolution despite the recent events.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. launched (verb): To start or set in motion something, often a large-scale project or an attack.
2. heavy (adjective): Referring to something intense, significant, or powerful in its impact, like a strong attack.
3. strikes (noun): Military attacks, often by air, against a specific target.
4. short period of relative calm (phrase): A brief time when a difficult situation becomes less active or intense, suggesting a temporary peace.
5. hostilities were paused (phrase): The fighting or aggressive actions between two groups stopped temporarily.

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is a grammatical construction used when the focus is on the action or the result of the action, rather than on the agent (who or what performs the action). It is formed using a form of the verb 'to be' (e.g., is, are, was, were) followed by the past participle of the main verb. It's often used in news to report events objectively without naming the specific actor, or when the actor is less important or unknown.
Example from the text: "hostilities were paused"

📅 July 29, 2026 [3 days, 23 hours old] - Japan Struck by Powerful Quake and Over 100 Aftershocks
Following a powerful earthquake that killed 13 people, over 100 aftershocks have hit Japan, leading officials to urge residents to remain vigilant against further seismic activity.

Intermediate ESL: Natural Disasters: Earthquakes and Aftershocks in Japan

I. The News Brief

Japan recently experienced a powerful earthquake which tragically resulted in 13 fatalities. The initial tremor was followed by more than 100 aftershocks, causing ongoing concern among residents. Authorities have since issued warnings, urging the public to remain vigilant. These aftershocks have the potential to reach a significant magnitude, possibly up to 7. People in the affected regions are advised to stay alert for any further seismic activity. The country's officials are closely monitoring the situation to ensure public safety. Residents have been asked to prepare for potential emergencies and understand evacuation routes. Buildings and infrastructure are being carefully inspected for damage caused by the tremors. Many families are facing displacement and disruption to their daily lives due to the ongoing quakes. The government emphasizes the importance of following safety guidelines during this critical period.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. earthquake (noun): A sudden violent shaking of the ground, often causing great destruction, as a result of movements within the Earth's crust.
2. aftershocks (noun): Smaller earthquakes that follow the main shock of a large earthquake and originate at or near the same location.
3. vigilant (adjective): Keeping careful watch for possible danger or difficulties; alert and watchful.
4. magnitude (noun): A measure of the size or power of an earthquake.
5. seismic (adjective): Relating to or caused by earthquakes or other vibrations of the Earth's crust.

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action or the receiver of the action, rather than the doer. We often use it when the agent (the person or thing doing the action) is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from the context. It is formed using a form of 'to be' + past participle (e.g., was followed, are advised).
Example from the text: "The initial tremor was followed by more than 100 aftershocks, causing ongoing concern among residents."

Ukraine's President Zelensky will meet with former President Trump in Washington to strengthen US support for anti-ballistic missile defenses amidst intensified Russian attacks.

I. The News Brief

Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky is visiting Washington with a key objective. He aims to press former US President Donald Trump on strengthening air defence systems. Improved anti-ballistic missile defences are desperately needed to protect Kyiv's cities. Russia has recently escalated its military actions, leading to more deadly attacks on Ukraine. Zelensky is particularly hoping to firm up commitments from the United States. These commitments are crucial for bolstering Ukraine's ability to counter Russian aggression.
