📅 October 09, 2026 - Thieves Steal Many Wine Bottles in Italy
A large number of expensive wine bottles were stolen from a winery in Italy.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Thieves stole 30,000 wine bottles in Italy. These bottles are worth about €5m. The theft happened at a winery near Florence. The winery is called Marchesi Antinori. Thieves used forklifts to load trucks. The company hopes police can find the wine. Staff discovered the theft on Monday morning.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. stole (verb): took something from a person or place without permission.
2. bottles (noun): glass or plastic containers with a narrow neck, often used for liquids.
3. worth (adjective): having a certain value in money or importance.
4. winery (noun): a place where wine is made, often from grapes grown there.
5. company (noun): a business organization that makes or sells goods or services.
6. hopes (verb): wants something good to happen in the future.
7. theft (noun): the act of stealing something.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished at a specific time in the past. For regular verbs, we often add '-ed'. For many common verbs, the form changes (e.g., 'steal' becomes 'stole').
Example from the text: "Thieves stole 30,000 wine bottles in Italy."

📅 October 08, 2026 - US and Lebanon Protect Wanted Syrian General
A BBC report says the US and Lebanon are protecting a former Syrian general in return for information.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The US and Lebanon are protecting a former Syrian general, a BBC report says. His name is Bassam al-Hassan. He is wanted over serious crimes. Mr. Hassan is in Lebanon. He is protected because he gives information to the US. This information is about an American journalist, Austin Tice, who disappeared in Syria. The BBC says Hassan was involved when Tice was taken by force in 2012. Syria’s new government wants Hassan to return and face a trial in court. But US authorities want him to stay in Lebanon. A spokesperson for Tice’s family said the reported protection was wrong.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. general (noun): An officer with a high position in an army.
2. protected (verb, past participle): Kept safe from danger or harm.
3. wanted (verb, past participle): Being looked for by the authorities over a possible crime; this does not mean a court has found the person guilty.
4. information (noun): Facts or details about something.
5. journalist (noun): A person who writes news stories for others to read or watch.
6. disappeared (verb, past tense): Went missing; could not be found.

Grammar Focus: Using 'because' to show reason
We use 'because' to explain why something happens or why someone does something. It connects an action or a situation to its reason.
Example from the text: "He is protected because he gives information to the US."

📅 October 07, 2026 - Students Protest for Better Schools in France
Students and other people protested across France to ask for better schools and an end to police actions.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Teenagers in France asked for more resources to improve their schools. They also said the police were using too much force. The protests spread to parents, university students and unions, which are groups that speak for workers. Police clashed with protesters in Lyon. Police used tear gas in Paris and Marseille. The government said 215 students and 85 school staff had been hurt since the protests began. Alissa, a student, said schools were in very poor condition. Her friend Sacha said school ceilings leaked and these poor conditions made studying difficult. Sacha said they would keep protesting until leaders took action.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. protests (noun): events where people show they are unhappy about something, often in a public place
2. resources (noun): things that are available and can be used, like money, staff, or materials for schools
3. police (noun): the official organization that makes sure people follow the law and catches criminals
4. spread (verb): to move and cover a larger area, or to become known by more people
5. clashed (verb): had a fight or argument with someone, often between groups of people
6. hurt (verb): to cause pain or injury to a person or animal
7. conditions (noun): the state of something, especially regarding its quality or health

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
The simple past describes a finished action. Regular verbs usually add “-ed”: “ask” becomes “asked”. Some irregular verbs keep the same form: “spread” is both present and past. The other words in a sentence help show the time.
Example from the text: "Teenagers in France asked for more resources to improve their schools."
