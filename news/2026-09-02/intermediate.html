📅 September 02, 2026 - Europe responds to an accusation
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

Germany has blamed Russia for a failed drone attack on Leipzig airport. Russia denies that it tried to attack a Ukrainian cargo plane there. A robot was used to make an explosive drone safe. The airport is an important Nato hub used by Ukrainian transport aircraft. The EU and Nato have promised to increase pressure on Russia. Nato’s leader has also expressed support for Germany. European ministers are discussing stronger enforcement of sanctions and further visa restrictions. Russia is accused of acting more recklessly.

Words and grammar

1. denies (verb): says that a claim is not true
2. cargo (noun): goods transported by an aircraft, ship or vehicle
3. hub (noun): a central place where transport or other activities connect
4. sanctions (plural noun): official restrictions used to put pressure on a country, organisation or person
5. restrictions (plural noun): rules that limit what people can do
6. expressed (verb): communicated a feeling, opinion or position
7. increase (verb): to make something greater in amount, strength or level
8. transport (noun): the movement of people or goods from one place to another

Grammar Focus: Reporting an accusation
“Is accused of” reports a claim about wrongdoing. It does not establish that the claim is true.
Example from the text: "Russia is accused of acting more recklessly."
