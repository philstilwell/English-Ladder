1. Choose four useful terms from the reference. For each, give a plain-English meaning in this field, two natural word combinations (collocations), one realistic example and a likely misuse or contrast. Professional roles: Client / Advisor / Financial planner

Client: So I cannot be aggressive?

Client: The house funds are needed next year; the retirement funds have a much longer horizon.
Financial planner: That distinction belongs in the plan. We will assess liquidity needs and potential loss separately for each goal.
Vocabulary in this exchange: Time horizon: How long the client expects to invest before needing funds for a goal. / Risk tolerance: The client's emotional willingness to accept investment volatility or loss. / Risk capacity: The client's financial ability to absorb loss without derailing goals.
