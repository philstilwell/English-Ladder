📅 November 19, 2026 - Anthropic Prepares to Sell Shares to the Public
Anthropic plans to sell shares to help its business grow.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Anthropic makes Claude, a computer program that people can talk to. The company started just five years ago. Shares are small parts of a company that people can buy. On Thursday, people will be able to buy its shares on a stock market. Each share will cost $83 when trading starts. The company is now worth up to $1.5 trillion. Anthropic wants to grow, so it is looking for more money. Some people who put money into the company worry about its future profits. Profits are the money a business keeps after paying its costs.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. started (verb, past tense): Began.
2. shares (plural noun): Small parts of a company that people can buy.
3. worth (adjective): Having a value of a certain amount of money.
4. grow (verb): Become bigger.
5. worry (verb): Feel that something bad may happen.
6. profits (plural noun): Money a business keeps after paying its costs.

Grammar Focus: Future with “will”
Use “will” before a verb to talk about the future. Use the verb form you see in “to buy”, but leave out “to”. For example, use “will buy”. Do not add “s” to the verb after “will”.
Example from the text: "Each share will cost $83 when trading starts."

📅 November 18, 2026 - Waymo Stops US Freeway Taxi Trips for Now
Learn why Waymo has stopped some taxi trips and where its taxis can still go.

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Waymo has stopped its driverless taxis from using US freeways for now. Driverless taxis carry passengers without a human driver. Freeways are large roads for fast traffic. California banned the taxis from its freeways after several traffic problems. Some Waymo cars entered areas where people were working on the road. California officials said the cars were a danger to people in these areas. Waymo said there were no reports of anyone being hurt in the incident near San Francisco. Waymo said it was working with state officials to start freeway trips again. The taxis can still carry passengers on normal city streets.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. driverless (adjective): Without a human driver.
2. passengers (plural noun): People who travel in a car, bus or train but do not drive it.
3. freeways (plural noun): Large roads for fast traffic.
4. banned (verb, past tense): Said officially that something was not allowed.
5. entered (verb, past tense): Went into a place.
6. again (adverb): One more time.

Grammar Focus: “Can” for permission
Use “can” to say that someone is allowed to do something. Put the verb after “can” without “to”. Do not add “-s” or “-ing” to that verb. This pattern stays the same with one taxi or several taxis.
Example from the text: "The taxis can still carry passengers on normal city streets."

Robert Pattinson will play James Bond in a new film.

Read the story

Robert Pattinson will play James Bond after Daniel Craig. The British actor is known for Twilight and The Batman. Amazon MGM Studios announced the news on social media on Monday. Pattinson is 40 and will be the seventh actor to play Bond. The next Bond film is expected in 2028. Denis Villeneuve will direct the film, and Steven Knight will write it. The film's title and exact release date have not been announced yet. Pattinson said he was excited to work with the team.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. actor (noun): A person whose job is to play people in films or plays.
2. play (verb): To be someone else in a film or play.
3. announced (verb, past simple): Shared some news with many people.
4. title (noun): The name of a film, book or song.
5. excited (adjective): Very happy about something that is happening or will happen.
6. team (noun): A group of people who work together.

Grammar Focus: Talking about the future with “will”
Use “will” and the basic verb form to talk about the future. The verb stays the same after “will”, so use “will play”, not “will plays”. Use this pattern for future actions, such as making a new film.
Example from the text: "Robert Pattinson will play James Bond after Daniel Craig."
