📅 November 08, 2026 - The Leonid Meteor Shower Starts This Weekend

Read the story

The Leonid meteor shower starts this weekend. Its meteors look like lines of light that move quickly across the sky. The shower lasts until 30 November. It will be strongest on the night of 17 November. You may see as many as 15 meteors in an hour. Experts say to find a safe, dark place away from street lights. You can see the meteors with just your eyes. Give your eyes about 20 to 30 minutes to get used to the dark. An open space gives you a wide view of the sky. You need clear skies to see the meteors.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. meteor shower (noun phrase): a time when many lines of light can appear in the night sky
2. lasts (verb): continues for some time
3. away from (preposition): not near someone or something
4. street lights (plural noun): lights along a road that help people see at night
5. open space (noun phrase): a place outside with lots of room and few things in the way
6. clear skies (plural noun phrase): a sky with few or no clouds

Grammar Focus: Using “can” to say what is possible
Use “can” to say that it is possible to do something. Put “can” after the person or thing you are talking about. After “can”, use the basic form of the verb, such as “see” in “can see” or “find” in “can find”.
Example from the text: "You can see the meteors with just your eyes."
