A series of suspected attacks

Read the story

The report describes more suspected attacks on defence sites in Europe. Germany has blamed Russia for an attack on Leipzig airport. Russian officials say this claim is wrong. There have also been suspicious fires at other defence sites. Some cases are still being investigated, and some fires may have been accidents. Russia is the main suspect in the report.

Words and grammar

1. blamed (verb): said that someone was responsible
2. suspicious (adjective): making people think something may be wrong
3. suspect (noun): a person or party thought to be responsible
4. officials (plural noun): people with responsible jobs in a government or organisation
5. claim (noun): a statement that someone says is true
6. accidents (plural noun): harmful events that happen without being planned

Grammar Focus: A claim with a source
“In the report” limits the statement to what this source says. “Suspect” does not mean proven responsible.
Example from the text: "Russia is the main suspect in the report."
