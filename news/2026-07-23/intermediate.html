Read the story

The United States and Saudi Arabia have recently signed a landmark nuclear deal. This significant agreement focuses on peaceful nuclear co-operation between the two nations. The US Department of Energy announced the details of this important pact. Officials stated that the deal aims to support Saudi Arabia's development of nuclear energy. Crucially, it will provide US firms with significant access to the Saudi nuclear energy programme. This level of engagement suggests a strong commitment to energy collaboration. The pact highlights a strategic partnership in a critical technological sector. Experts believe this could boost American presence in the Saudi energy market. Both countries anticipate long-term benefits from this joint initiative. The arrangement is seen as a step towards diversifying Saudi Arabia's energy sources.

Words and grammar

1. landmark (adjective): an important or historic event or stage that marks a turning point
2. co-operation (noun): the process of working together to achieve a common goal
3. agreement (noun): a formal decision or promise made between two or more people, groups, or countries
4. access (noun): the opportunity or right to use or approach something
5. programme (noun): a plan of things to be done, especially in a series or sequence

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice
We use the passive voice when the focus is on the action itself and the object of the action, rather than the subject performing the action. It's often used when the doer of the action is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from the context, or when we want to sound more formal. The structure is usually "to be" + past participle.
Example from the text: "The arrangement is seen as a step towards diversifying Saudi Arabia's energy sources."
