Matthew Rhys Wins Two Emmys, Makes History

Beginner · A1–A2

📅 September 15, 2026 - Matthew Rhys Wins Two Emmys, Makes History

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Hollywood actor Matthew Rhys made history at the Emmys. He won two lead acting prizes in one night. He won for best lead actor in a limited series and for a comedy. At the start of his career, Rhys considered joining the British Army. He later received a rejection letter and could not join the army. Rhys speaks Welsh to his son Sam at home in New York. He also voices Han Solo in a new Welsh language adaptation of Star Wars.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. actor (noun): A person who performs in movies, TV shows, or plays.
2. history (noun): When something very important happens for the first time or is remembered for a long time.
3. prizes (noun): Awards or things you win in a competition or for doing something very well.
4. considered (verb): Thought about something carefully before making a decision.
5. joining (verb): Becoming a member of a group, organization, or team.
6. speaks (verb): Uses words to talk in a language.
7. voices (verb): Provides the sound of a character for a movie, show, or game.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that happened and finished at a specific time in the past. We often add '-ed' to regular verbs, but some verbs have different forms, like 'win' becoming 'won'.
Example from the text: "He won two lead acting prizes in one night."
