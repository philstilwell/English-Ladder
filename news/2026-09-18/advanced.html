Read the story

French President Emmanuel Macron has warned that the threat of hybrid attacks by Russia against Europe and France has grown more acute. He revealed that France had recently experienced Russian hybrid attacks and affirmed that these would not go "without a response". Macron reiterated that the Russian hybrid threat against Europeans and France had notably escalated in recent weeks. He stated Russia's intent was to intimidate and disrupt efforts to support Ukraine, asserting that such actions would achieve the opposite effect. The president emphasized that Europe's security is intrinsically linked to the situation in Ukraine, thereby dismissing any notion of intimidation. He announced a directive for a plan to bolster the security of France's vital national assets and sensitive defence industry sites. This security measure specifically addresses threats from drone and cyberattacks, alongside other non-military methods. Hybrid warfare encompasses a broad strategy combining conventional military force with non-military tactics, including cyberattacks, misinformation, and economic pressure. Macron referenced a drone attack on Leipzig airport, reportedly blamed on Russia, and Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk's concerns about potential Russian strikes against Nato's eastern flank. Tusk explicitly warned Polish MPs of a "real risk" of drone and missile strikes on Nato territory, which Moscow might falsely attribute to accidental causes to splinter the alliance. Furthermore, Macron announced France would convene a G7 meeting to address energy issues and discuss gas stocks with the European Commission. The G7 discussion aims to strengthen cooperation among member nations and prevent unnecessary tensions while also considering options for unlocking strategic energy reserves.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. grown more acute (verb phrase): become more serious or severe, often implying a heightened sense of urgency or danger.
2. experienced (verb): undergone or lived through an event or situation, particularly one that is notable or impactful.
3. affirmed (verb): stated something as true or valid with conviction, certainty, or solemn declaration.
4. reiterated (verb): said something again or repeatedly, typically for emphasis, clarification, or to confirm a previous statement.
5. escalated (verb): increased rapidly in intensity, magnitude, or severity, indicating a worsening situation or conflict.
6. intrinsically linked (adverbial phrase): connected in a fundamental, essential, or inherent way; forming an inseparable or integral bond.
7. dismissing (verb, present participle): treating something as unworthy of serious consideration or regard; rejecting or disregarding it without further thought.
8. bolster (verb): to support, strengthen, or improve something, often by providing additional resources or reinforcement.
9. vital national assets (noun phrase): essential resources, systems, or facilities crucial for a nation's functioning, security, and the well-being of its population.
10. encompasses (verb): includes or contains something comprehensively within its scope; to surround or cover completely.
11. splinter (verb): to cause a group, organization, or alliance to break or divide into smaller, often opposing, factions or fragments.
12. convene (verb): to call people together for a formal meeting, conference, or assembly.

Advanced Grammar: Participial Clauses for Result or Manner
Participial clauses, often introduced by "thereby," "thus," or simply used with a comma, can describe the result or manner of the action in the main clause. They provide additional information, showing a consequence or how something is done, making sentences more concise and sophisticated. The participle (present or past) functions adverbially, linking the action to its effect.
Example from the text: "The president emphasized that Europe's security is intrinsically linked to the situation in Ukraine, thereby dismissing any notion of intimidation."
