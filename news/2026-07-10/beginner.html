Title: Wildfire in Spain: Sad News from Los Gallardos

A big fire in southern Spain caused many deaths and some people are still missing.

Read the story

There was a big wildfire in southern Spain. This fire happened near Los Gallardos. Los Gallardos is a place in Almería. At least 12 people are dead because of the fire. Also, 23 people are missing. A local official spoke about the fire. He said some victims might be British. Four of the victims may be from Britain. The fire broke out suddenly. It is a very sad situation for everyone.

Words and grammar

1. wildfire (noun): A large, uncontrolled fire in a natural area like a forest.
2. dead (adjective): Not alive anymore; passed away.
3. missing (adjective): Not found; people do not know where they are.
4. broke out (verb phrase): Started suddenly and violently.
5. sad (adjective): Feeling unhappy or showing sorrow.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions or events that finished in the past. It describes things that already happened.
Example from the text: "The fire broke out suddenly."
