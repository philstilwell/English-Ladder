📅 August 31, 2026 - A difficult rescue in Nepal
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

Flooding in Nepal has left hydropower workers believed to be trapped in an extensive network of mud-filled tunnels. Rescuers have resorted to explosives to penetrate the hillside and reach the affected sites. Engineers have been pumping air underground while the search continues. The operation is led by Chinese and Nepalese military personnel, with specialists from other countries providing support. A team managed to enter one tunnel but withdrew without finding anyone there. An engineer involved in the response described how changes to the landscape had made tunnel entrances difficult to locate. The disaster has affected both operational hydropower projects and facilities still under construction. The damaged projects belong to an industry on which Nepal relies heavily for electricity. Relatives have urged the teams to accelerate the search as their concern grows. Families are waiting for news.

Words and grammar

1. extensive (adjective): covering a large area or involving many parts
2. resorted to (verb phrase): used an option because other approaches were insufficient or difficult
3. penetrate (verb): pass or break through something
4. personnel (plural noun): people employed by an organisation or serving in its forces
5. accelerate (verb): make an activity or process happen faster
6. operational (adjective): in active use or functioning, rather than still being prepared or built
7. under construction (prepositional phrase): still being built and not yet complete
8. relies heavily (verb phrase): depends to a large extent on someone or something
9. landscape (noun): the physical features and appearance of an area of land
10. locate (verb): to establish the position of something being sought

Advanced Grammar: An action happening now
Are waiting describes an activity in progress. It does not tell us when the wait will end.
Example from the text: "Families are waiting for news."
