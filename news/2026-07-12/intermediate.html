Read the story

Recent events have seen the United States undertake fresh strikes in the region. These actions come shortly after Iran declared its intention to close the vital Strait of Hormuz. The Strait of Hormuz is a critical waterway for global oil shipments. The US strikes follow a recent attack on a Cyprus-flagged vessel. This vessel was travelling through the critical waterway when the incident occurred. Such incidents escalate tensions in the already volatile Middle East. The attack on the Cyprus-flagged vessel has raised concerns about maritime security. International observers are closely watching how these developments unfold. The closure of the Strait of Hormuz would significantly impact global trade and oil prices. Diplomatic efforts are urgently needed to de-escalate the situation and prevent further conflict.

Words and grammar

1. strikes (noun): Military attacks, often from the air or with missiles, aimed at a target.
2. declared (verb): Stated something officially, publicly, or strongly.
3. vessel (noun): A ship or large boat, typically used for transporting goods or people by sea.
4. critical (adjective): Extremely important or essential for the success or stability of something.
5. escalate (verb): To increase rapidly in intensity, severity, or magnitude.

Grammar Focus: The Passive Voice
The passive voice is often used when the focus is on the action itself rather than on who or what is performing it. It's formed with a form of the verb 'to be' followed by the past participle of the main verb. We use it when the agent (the one performing the action) is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from the context.
Example from the text: "Diplomatic efforts are urgently needed to de-escalate the situation and prevent further conflict."
