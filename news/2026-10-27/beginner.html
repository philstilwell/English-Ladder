James Bourne Says His Brother Saved His Life

Beginner · A1–A2

📅 October 27, 2026 - James Bourne Says His Brother Saved His Life

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

James Bourne plays guitar in the band Busted. In September 2025, he left a tour just before it began. He said he left because he had health problems. His younger brother Chris played in his place. Doctors told James that he had only a few years left to live. Chris later gave James one of his kidneys to save his life. James says he and Chris are both getting better. James still needs more time to get well. He calls his brother a hero.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. band (noun): A group of people who play music together.
2. tour (noun): A journey to play music for people in different places.
3. health problems (plural noun phrase): Things that are wrong with your body and make you ill.
4. in his place (phrase): Doing something that he usually does.
5. kidneys (plural noun): Parts inside the body that clean the blood.
6. getting better (verb phrase): Becoming less ill.
7. hero (noun): Someone who helps others in a very important way.

Grammar Focus: Simple past
Use the simple past for a finished action: "His younger brother Chris played in his place." Here, "played" is the past form of "play". Many past forms end in "-ed".
Example from the text: "His younger brother Chris played in his place."
