English for Healthcare and Life Sciences

Healthcare and life-science communication often depends on carefully stating what is known, what is pending and who needs to respond. These materials develop the English used in handoffs, research discussions, quality conversations and professional coordination.

Choose the work you do

Nursing and allied health emphasizes team and patient-facing communication. Healthcare administration focuses on services and operations. Biotechnology, medical devices and pharmaceutical courses address distinct research, development, quality and regulatory communication settings.

Make the practice practical

Read the case and separate observations from conclusions. Rehearse a handoff, ask one precise clarification question, and repeat back the open item. Treat every scenario as fictional language practice; actual clinical, regulatory and safety decisions follow qualified supervision and applicable procedures.

Choose your support

These materials suit intermediate to advanced learners. Use B1 sentence frames for support, B2 practice for independence, or C1 challenges for greater precision. These are study suggestions, not certified level assessments.
