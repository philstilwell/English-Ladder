# English for Work learner-book audit

## Scope

Audit and revise all 66 learner books. Preserve the eight web-aligned lessons,
English Ladder branding, embedded fonts, copyright, uniform cloze blanks, and
structured answerable activities. Work is performed without assistant workers
or paid external services.

## Acceptance criteria

- Review industry terminology, realistic responsibilities, numerical claims,
  authority boundaries, dialogue coherence, cloze ambiguity, and answer keys.
- Improve existing conversations where teaching exposition crowds out speech.
- Add three distinct 20-turn conversations and three four-turn transfer
  exchanges per book: eleven of each, a 37.5% increase from eight of each.
- Each new scenario has a concrete workplace purpose, identifiable roles,
  a shuffled word bank, numbered equal-length blanks, and explained answers.
- Improve speaker differentiation, navigation, spacing, and word-bank design.
- Check every PDF for embedded fonts, missing text, overflow, correct internal
  links, answer coverage, and publication consistency. Visually inspect final
  rendered samples from every book as well as shared page types.
- Publish only after the complete set passes. Update download descriptions and
  page counts, then commit and push only relevant files.

## Baseline

- 66 books; 102 pages each; eight lessons each.
- Per book: 192 vocabulary entries, 128 phrases, eight 20-turn dialogues,
  eight four-turn transfer exchanges, and 112 dialogue gaps.
- Source books contain original fictional scenarios, not authentic transcripts.
- Recurrent editorial risk: repeated evidence/authorization cautions sometimes
  sound like teaching notes rather than natural workplace interaction.
