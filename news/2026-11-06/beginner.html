Tech Share Prices Fall After US Elections

📅 November 06, 2026 - Tech Share Prices Fall After US Elections

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Share prices for big tech companies fell in the US and Asia after the US elections. People worried that work on artificial intelligence, or AI, might slow down. Some Democrats wanted to slow down the building of new data centres. These centres help run AI. People also wondered if all the spending on AI would be worth it. In Japan, shares in the chip maker Kioxia fell by more than 12%. Buying and selling shares on the Seoul market stopped for a short time after share prices fell fast.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. shares (plural noun): Small parts of a company that people can buy and sell.
2. fell (past tense verb): Went down in price.
3. elections (plural noun): Times when people vote to choose their leaders.
4. AI (noun, short form): Technology that helps computers do things like answer questions.
5. slow down (phrasal verb): To happen more slowly or make something happen more slowly.
6. data centres (plural noun phrase): Buildings with many computers that help run services like AI.
7. spending (noun): Using money to pay for things.
8. for a short time (time phrase): For a little while.

Grammar Focus: Simple past
Use the simple past to talk about things that happened before now. Many verbs add “-ed”, as in “start” and “started”. Some verbs change in other ways. In the reading, “fell” is the simple past of “fall”.
Example from the text: "Share prices for big tech companies fell in the US and Asia after the US elections."
