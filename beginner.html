📅 July 31, 2026 [0 days, 0 hours old] - Many Migrants Enter Ceuta: A News Lesson
Nearly 49,000 migrants arrived in the Spanish city of Ceuta very quickly.

Beginner ESL: Migration to Ceuta: A Rapid Influx

I. The News Brief

About 49,000 migrants came to Ceuta. Ceuta is a Spanish territory. They entered in just 24 hours. This happened very quickly. Officials say this information is correct. The city of Ceuta has about 83,600 people. So, many new people arrived. The number of arrivals is more than half of the city's population. This was a very big event for the city. People from other places often want to live in Europe.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. migrants (noun): People who move from one country or area to live in another.
2. Spanish territory (noun phrase): An area of land controlled by the country of Spain.
3. entered (verb (past simple)): Went into a place or country.
4. officials say (phrase): Important people in the government or an organization share this information.
5. city of Ceuta (noun phrase): A city in North Africa that belongs to Spain.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that happened and finished at a specific time in the past. Many verbs add '-ed' to form the simple past, like 'talked' or 'walked'. Some verbs are irregular and change completely, such as 'come' becomes 'came' or 'go' becomes 'went'.
Example from the text: "They entered in just 24 hours."

📅 July 28, 2026 [3 days, 0 hours old] - Ukraine's Leader Visits Washington for Air Defences

Beginner ESL: Ukraine and US Talks on Air Defences

I. The News Brief

Ukraine's leader, Mr. Zelensky, went to Washington. He wants to talk with Mr. Trump. Mr. Zelensky hopes to get more air defences. These are systems to protect Ukraine from missiles. Russia has escalated its attacks on Ukraine. These attacks have been very dangerous and caused deadly attacks. Ukraine needs strong commitments from the US. These commitments mean the US promises to help. Mr. Zelensky wants to make sure the help is firm. So, Ukraine can protect its people better.

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

1. leader (noun): A person who guides or commands a group.
2. air defences (noun phrase): Systems that protect a country from attacks by planes or missiles in the sky.
3. commitments (noun): Strong promises to do something or give something.
4. escalated (verb (past simple)): Became more intense, larger, or more serious.
5. deadly attacks (noun phrase): Violent actions that can cause death or serious harm.

Grammar Focus: Simple Present Tense for Wants and Hopes
We use the Simple Present tense to talk about things we want or hope for. It shows a desire or intention. For example, 'He wants help' or 'She hopes to visit.' Remember to add '-s' for 'he', 'she', 'it' forms, like 'wants' or 'hopes'.
Example from the text: "He wants to talk with Mr. Trump."
