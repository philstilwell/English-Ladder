Read the story

Colombia had a big earthquake. About 180 people died because of it. Many buildings fell down from the earthquake. Aftershocks also happened on Tuesday. Rescuers are looking for people. They want to find survivors who are alive. Some people are trapped in the fallen rubble. The rescuers are working to free them. These rescue efforts continued all day. People hope to save more survivors.

Words and grammar

1. earthquake (noun): When the ground shakes suddenly and strongly.
2. rescuers (noun): People who help to save others from danger.
3. survivors (noun): People who are still alive after a dangerous event.
4. trapped (adjective): Unable to move or get out of a place.
5. rubble (noun): Broken pieces of buildings, like stones and bricks.

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. Many verbs add "-ed" for the past, like "happened". Other verbs change their form, like "had" from "have" or "fell" from "fall".
Example from the text: "Aftershocks also happened on Tuesday."
