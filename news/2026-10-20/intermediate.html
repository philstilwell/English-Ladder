📅 October 20, 2026 - CNN Chief International Anchor Christiane Amanpour Leaves After Forty Years

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

Christiane Amanpour, a chief international anchor, has announced she will separate from her organisation next year after more than four decades. In a recent statement, the broadcaster explained that she made this difficult decision after many months of careful thought. She began her career there in 1983 and eventually became one of their best-known correspondents. Over the years, she has frequently reported on dangerous conflicts around the world. She has presented her own nightly interview program on international television since 2009. Amanpour expressed deep gratitude to the amazingly talented teams and field crews who shared her incredible journey. The chairman of the company described her as a highly trusted professional. She praised her teams because they battled together to seek the facts and broadcast the truth.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. anchor (noun): A person who presents and coordinates a television or radio program.
2. decades (noun): Periods of ten years.
3. statement (noun): An official or formal report that shares facts or opinions.
4. decision (noun): A choice or judgment that you make after thinking carefully.
5. correspondents (noun): Reporters who provide news from a particular area or on a specific subject.
6. conflicts (noun): Long periods of active fighting or wars between groups or countries.
7. gratitude (noun): The feeling of being thankful and wanting to show appreciation.
8. broadcast (verb): To send out programs or information by television or radio.

Grammar Focus: Present Perfect with "Since"
Use the present perfect tense (has/have + past participle) with "since" to talk about an action that started in the past and continues into the present. The word "since" points to the specific starting time, such as a year or an exact date.
Example from the text: "She has presented her own nightly interview program on international television since 2009."
