📅 September 02, 2026 - Europe responds to an accusation
Choose what you read. This report includes distressing events. You can choose an everyday story on Discover instead.

Read the story

Germany has blamed Russia for a failed drone attack on Leipzig airport. Russia says it did not carry out the attack. A robot made an explosive drone safe. The airport is used by Ukrainian transport planes. The EU and Nato say they will put more pressure on Russia. Russia is accused of acting more recklessly.

Words and grammar

1. blamed (verb): said that someone was responsible
2. failed (adjective): did not succeed
3. accused (verb): said to have done something wrong
4. drone (noun): an aircraft that flies without a pilot inside
5. recklessly (adverb): without enough care for safety or danger
6. pressure (noun): strong efforts to make someone change what they do

Grammar Focus: Reporting an accusation
“Is accused of” reports a claim about wrongdoing. It does not establish that the claim is true.
Example from the text: "Russia is accused of acting more recklessly."
