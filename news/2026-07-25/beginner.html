Read the story

This year, the White House Correspondents' Dinner happened. President Trump was there. He spoke at the event. He made some critical remarks about the press. The dinner is usually a time for fun. It also celebrates press freedom. Last year, the dinner was cancelled. This was because of a shooting. The shooting happened in April. So, this year's event was very important for journalists.

Words and grammar

1. press (noun): Press means newspapers, TV, and radio people. They report the news.
2. event (noun): An event is something important that happens, like a party or meeting.
3. celebrates (verb): When you celebrate, you do something special because you are happy about something.
4. cancelled (verb): If something is cancelled, it means it will not happen.
5. journalists (noun): Journalists are people who write news stories for newspapers or report for TV and radio.
6. shooting (noun): an event in which someone fires a gun

Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense
We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. We often add '-ed' to regular verbs. Irregular verbs change their form. It tells us when something finished.
Example from the text: "The shooting happened in April."
