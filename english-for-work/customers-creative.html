English for Customers, Sales and Creative Work

Customer and creative conversations combine clear information with an appropriate tone. Practice understanding a request, explaining a limitation, handling an objection and agreeing on a useful next step without promising an outcome you cannot control.

Choose the work you do

Choose hospitality or retail for service situations, sales for discovery and negotiation, or customer success for adoption and renewals. Marketing, media and real estate courses each introduce their own terminology, audiences and professional scenarios.

Make the practice practical

Read both sides of a dialogue and identify what each speaker needs. Switch roles, change one fictional constraint and rehearse a new ending. Follow the conversation with a concise written message that preserves its commitments and any unresolved questions.
