Nvidia Leadership Turmoil in an Unverified Account

Advanced · C1+

📅 November 13, 2026 - Nvidia Leadership Turmoil in an Unverified Account

Tap an underlined word in the story to see its meaning.

Read the story

The supplied account reports the abrupt departure of Nvidia’s chief executive, portraying it as a severe reversal for the AI chipmaking giant. It links the reported crisis to concerns about professional boundaries and possible national security risks. According to the account, Nvidia has come under scrutiny from US lawmakers seeking answers about a possible connection to Chinese intelligence. The text reports that US Attorney General Todd Blanche said the Department of Justice had not yet drawn any conclusions about whether Alice Huang had engaged in espionage for Beijing. It also says several lawmakers demanded that the departing executive brief Congress on the matter. Should the executive refuse to appear voluntarily, he could be subpoenaed, meaning formally ordered to attend, according to a Senate staff member cited in the account. The account says Nvidia saw no reason to believe national security or company secrets had been compromised, while pledging that an independent investigation would consider all available information. It reports that the executive had initially been expected to weather the storm while the company investigated whether he had been targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. An employee cited in the text says initial reports about the executive’s private conduct were largely brushed off by rank-and-file workers. The account notes that many employees had earned millions through their ability to cash out company stock as its value soared. It describes a succession process in which Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress took temporary charge while Nvidia sought a permanent replacement. According to the supplied figures, Nvidia’s value had risen by more than 470% over five years and was now down by 31% against a backdrop of wider uncertainty in the technology sector. The account says Nvidia represents about 7% of the S&P 500 index, which is available through a popular index fund used in US retirement savings accounts. It explains that those retirement account balances fluctuate with stock market movements.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. professional boundaries (plural noun phrase): Limits on workplace conduct intended to prevent personal relationships from interfering with professional responsibilities.
2. under scrutiny (prepositional phrase): Being examined closely, especially because conduct or decisions have raised concerns.
3. brief Congress (verb phrase): Give members of Congress a focused account of relevant information about a particular matter.
4. subpoenaed (verb, past participle): Formally required by a legal order to appear or provide evidence; here, required to attend.
5. weather the storm (idiomatic verb phrase): Survive a period of serious difficulty without losing one’s position or ability to continue.
6. foreign intelligence effort (noun phrase): An organised attempt by another country’s intelligence services to obtain information or exert influence.
7. brushed off (phrasal verb, past participle): Dismissed as insufficiently important to deserve serious attention, without necessarily being disproved.
8. rank-and-file (adjective): Describing ordinary members of an organisation, as distinct from those in senior positions.
9. cash out (phrasal verb): Convert an investment into money by selling it, thereby realising its current value.
10. succession (noun): The process through which one person replaces another in a position of authority.
11. against a backdrop of (prepositional phrase): Within a wider situation that provides relevant context without necessarily establishing a direct cause.

Advanced Grammar: Inverted future conditional with “should”
In formal future conditionals, place “should” before the subject and use the base verb instead of an “if”-clause. This structure presents a possible condition; “could” in the main clause makes the consequence possible rather than inevitable.
Example from the text: "Should the executive refuse to appear voluntarily, he could be subpoenaed, meaning formally ordered to attend, according to a Senate staff member cited in the account."
