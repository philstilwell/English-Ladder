Nigella Lawson Joins Bake Off’s New Series

📅 September 08, 2026 - Nigella Lawson Joins Bake Off’s New Series

Read the story

TV critics have welcomed Nigella Lawson as a new judge on The Great British Bake Off. Channel 4 will show the first episode of series 17 on 22 September. The opening episode was shown to critics earlier this week. Lawson replaces Prue Leith, who wanted to spend less time working and more time enjoying herself. Anita Singh of the Telegraph suggested that Lawson’s memorable comments could become popular on social media. The new participants have different backgrounds, including work in medicine, hairdressing and the arts. Moyin is a student who balances his studies with athletics. He uses flavours inspired by his Nigerian heritage in his baking. Yannis is a doctor who wants to challenge an unfair belief about Bangladeshi men cooking. Connie works in a hospital and uses baking to relax after a busy day.

Words and grammar

1. critics (plural noun): People whose job is to give opinions about television, films, books or other creative work.
2. episode (noun): One programme in a television series.
3. memorable (adjective): Easy to remember because it makes a strong impression.
4. participants (plural noun): People who take part in an activity or event.
5. backgrounds (plural noun): The experiences, jobs and places that have shaped people’s lives.
6. balances (verb): Gives time and attention to two different activities so that both fit into daily life.
7. inspired by (verb phrase): Influenced by something that gives a person an idea.
8. heritage (noun): The traditions and culture connected with a person’s family or community.

Grammar Focus: Past simple passive
Use “was” or “were” + a past participle to focus on what happened to something. In this example, the episode received the action: someone showed it to the critics. The sentence does not name the person who showed it.
Example from the text: "The opening episode was shown to critics earlier this week."
