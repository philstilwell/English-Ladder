Suggest and recommend

Offer an idea using a natural verb pattern.

Level ranges are study guidance. Start with the examples and choose your own pace.

Try the practice →Learner workbook PDFTeaching guide PDF

ACTIVITY

I recommend booking ahead.

Booking is the suggested activity.

PERSON + ACTION

I suggest that Mina call first.

Use the base verb call in this advice pattern.

RECIPIENT

She suggested a cafe to me.

To me identifies the recipient of the suggestion.

Understand

The useful distinction

Use suggest or recommend with a noun or an -ing activity: suggest a cafe; recommend booking early. With a named person doing the action, a that clause is useful: I suggest that she book early. She should book is also possible after that, especially in British English.

Do not use suggest someone to do for advice. Say suggest that someone do, or suggest doing. Recommend has additional patterns and meanings, such as recommend someone for a job. Learn the meaning and pattern together instead of treating both verbs as identical in every use.
