US and China Leaders Meet, Talk, But Resolve Few Big Issues

📅 September 28, 2026 - US and China Leaders Meet, Talk, But Resolve Few Big Issues

Read the story

Leaders from the US and China met. They talked about important topics, but they did not say everything. China shared what was agreed at the talks last week. But they made no big new agreements. Both countries said they would talk about AI technology. China also said a trade agreement will continue until January. Many hard problems between the US and China are still not solved.

Words and grammar

Definitions in English.

1. met (verb): To come together with someone, for example, for a meeting. This is the past tense of 'meet'.
2. topics (noun): Subjects or things that people talk or write about.
3. agreed (verb): To have the same idea or opinion as someone else. This is the past tense of 'agree'.
4. talks (noun): Meetings where people discuss important issues.
5. agreements (noun): Formal decisions or promises made by two or more groups.
6. continue (verb): To keep doing something; not stop.
7. solved (verb): To find an answer or a way to fix a problem. This is the past tense of 'solve'.

Grammar Focus: Past Simple Tense
We use the past simple tense to talk about actions that happened in the past and are now finished. For regular verbs, we often add '-ed'. For irregular verbs, the form changes, like 'meet' becoming 'met'.
Example from the text: "Leaders from the US and China met."
