Read the story

Rescuers in Nepal are trying to reach trapped workers. The workers were at a hydropower site. The rescuers have used explosives. Families are waiting for news.

Words and grammar

1. rescuers (plural noun): people who try to bring others to safety
2. trapped (adjective): unable to get out
3. waiting (verb): staying until something happens

Grammar Focus: An action happening now
Are waiting describes an activity in progress. It does not tell us when the wait will end.
Example from the text: "Families are waiting for news."
