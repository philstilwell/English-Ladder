Before you read

Read the title. What do you think the story is about?

Read the story

Germany has blamed Russia for a failed drone attack on Leipzig airport. The EU and Nato have promised to increase pressure on Russia. The report describes this as a new escalation. Russia is accused of acting more recklessly.

Words and grammar

1. blamed (verb): said that someone was responsible
2. failed (adjective): did not succeed
3. accused (verb): said to have done something wrong

Grammar Focus: Reporting an accusation
Is accused of reports a claim about wrongdoing. It does not establish that the claim is true.
Example from the text: "Russia is accused of acting more recklessly."
