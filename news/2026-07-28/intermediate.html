Read the story

Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky is visiting Washington with a key objective. He aims to press former US President Donald Trump on strengthening air defence systems. Improved anti-ballistic missile defences are desperately needed to protect Kyiv's cities. Russia has recently escalated its military actions, leading to more deadly attacks on Ukraine. Zelensky is particularly hoping to firm up commitments from the United States. These commitments are crucial for bolstering Ukraine's ability to counter Russian aggression. The discussions will focus on how more advanced protective measures can be provided by the US. Securing these systems is vital for reducing civilian casualties and protecting infrastructure. This diplomatic effort highlights Ukraine's ongoing need for strong international support. The outcome of these talks could significantly impact the ongoing conflict and future aid.

Words and grammar

1. press (verb): To try to persuade someone forcefully to do something or to address a particular issue with urgency.
2. defences (noun): Actions or systems, especially military ones, used to protect against attack or harm.
3. escalated (verb): Increased rapidly in intensity, amount, or seriousness, often referring to conflicts or problems.
4. firm up (phrasal verb): To make something, like an agreement or a plan, more definite, certain, or robust.
5. bolstering (verb): The act of supporting, strengthening, or making something stronger or more effective.

Grammar Focus: Passive Voice (Present Simple)
The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action itself and the object that receives the action, rather than the subject performing the action. It is formed with a form of 'to be' (e.g., is, are) followed by the past participle of the main verb. For example, 'The house is built.'
Example from the text: "Improved anti-ballistic missile defences are desperately needed to protect Kyiv's cities."
